# Disposal with 把 | Subject + 把 + identifiable object + verb + result | core
Use 把 to bring an identifiable affected object before the verb and explain what happens to it. The verb phrase normally includes a result, direction, or other elaboration. Put negation and modal verbs before 把.
请把门关上。|Please close the door.
我把钥匙放在桌子上了。|I put the keys on the table.
她把作业做完了。|She finished the homework.
请把这句话翻译成中文。|Please translate this sentence into Chinese.
他把杯子打破了。|He broke the cup.
我们把会议改到星期五了。|We moved the meeting to Friday.
请把手机关掉。|Please turn off your phone.
我把那本书还给老师了。|I returned that book to the teacher.
她把房间收拾干净了。|She tidied the room until it was clean.
别把护照忘在家里。|Do not leave your passport at home.

# Passive with 被 | Affected subject + 被 + agent + verb + result | core
Use 被 to make the affected person or thing the subject. The agent can sometimes be omitted. The verb phrase usually describes a result or event, and the passive often—but not always—concerns an unwelcome outcome.
我的自行车被人偷了。|My bicycle was stolen by someone.
窗户被风吹开了。|The window was blown open by the wind.
蛋糕被孩子吃完了。|The cake was eaten up by the child.
我的杯子被他打破了。|My cup was broken by him.
她被老师表扬了。|She was praised by the teacher.
我的名字被写错了。|My name was written incorrectly.
门被人锁上了。|The door was locked by someone.
这本书被翻译成了中文。|This book was translated into Chinese.
我的计划被经理拒绝了。|My plan was rejected by the manager.
他被选为班长了。|He was chosen as class monitor.

# Highlighting past details with 是…的 | Subject + 是 + highlighted detail + verb + 的 | core
Use 是…的 to highlight the time, place, manner, or agent of an event treated as already established. It is not a general past tense. 是 is often omitted in affirmative sentences but retained in negative 不是…的.
我是昨天到的。|It was yesterday that I arrived.||我昨天到的。
她是坐地铁来的。|It was by subway that she came.
我们是在北京认识的。|It was in Beijing that we met.
这张照片是我拍的。|It was me who took this photo.
他是跟朋友一起去的。|It was with friends that he went.
你是什么时候买的？|When was it that you bought it?
我是在网上订的票。|It was online that I booked the tickets.
我不是坐飞机来的。|It was not by plane that I came.
我们是去年搬来的。|It was last year that we moved here.
这道菜是妈妈做的。|It was Mom who made this dish.

# Requesting or inviting with 请 | 请 + person + verb | review
Put 请 before a verb or before a person plus verb to make a polite request or invitation. Courtesy also depends on the whole sentence and situation; 请 is not required in every polite Chinese request.
请坐。|Please sit down.
请稍等一下。|Please wait a moment.
请你帮我开一下门。|Please help me open the door.
请大家保持安静。|Everyone, please keep quiet.
请把菜单给我。|Please give me the menu.
请在这里签字。|Please sign here.
请告诉我你的名字。|Please tell me your name.
请明天再来。|Please come again tomorrow.
请不要在这里吸烟。|Please do not smoke here.
请给我一杯温水。|Please give me a glass of warm water.

# Prohibitions with 别 | 别 + verb | review
Use 别 before a verb to tell someone not to do something. 不要 is a common alternative. 别…了 can ask someone to stop an activity already underway.
别担心。|Do not worry.||不要担心。
别迟到。|Do not be late.||不要迟到。
别忘了带护照。|Do not forget to bring your passport.||不要忘了带护照。
别在这里停车。|Do not park here.||不要在这里停车。
别说话了。|Stop talking.||不要说话了。
别把门关上。|Do not close the door.||不要把门关上。
别吃太多糖。|Do not eat too much sugar.||不要吃太多糖。
别告诉别人。|Do not tell other people.||不要告诉别人。
别着急，慢慢来。|Do not rush; take your time.||不要着急，慢慢来。
别再打电话了。|Stop calling.||不要再打电话了。

# Suggestions with 吧 | Proposal + 吧 | review
Place 吧 at the end of a proposal, invitation, or softened instruction. Its force depends on context; it can also signal a tentative guess, which is a separate use.
我们走吧。|Let us go.
一起吃午饭吧。|Let us have lunch together.
你先休息一下吧。|Why don't you rest a little first?
我们坐地铁吧。|Let us take the subway.
明天再说吧。|Let us talk about it tomorrow.
请进来吧。|Please come on in.
我们换个话题吧。|Let us change the subject.
你试试吧。|Why don't you give it a try?
我们在门口见吧。|Let us meet at the entrance.
把窗户打开吧。|Why don't you open the window?

# Brief actions with 一下 | Verb + 一下 | review
Use 一下 after a verb to make an action brief, tentative, or softer in a request. It often means 'for a moment' or 'give it a…', not a literal count of one occurrence.
请等一下。|Please wait briefly.
我想看一下菜单。|I would like to take a quick look at the menu.
你能帮我检查一下吗？|Could you help me check it briefly?
让我想一下。|Let me think for a moment.
请介绍一下你自己。|Please briefly introduce yourself.
我去问一下老师。|I will go and ask the teacher quickly.
你先休息一下。|Rest a little first.
请试一下这双鞋。|Please try on this pair of shoes.
我想了解一下情况。|I would like to get a brief idea of the situation.
请记一下我的号码。|Please take a moment to note my number.

# Tentative actions by reduplication | Verb-verb / verb-一-verb | core
Repeat a suitable action verb to suggest doing it briefly or casually. Single-syllable verbs can use AA or A一A; many two-syllable verbs use ABAB. Do not automatically reduplicate result compounds.
你看看这张照片。|Take a look at this photo.||你看一看这张照片。
我想试试。|I would like to give it a try.||我想试一试。
我们聊聊吧。|Let us have a little chat.||我们聊一聊吧。
你再想想。|Think it over a bit more.||你再想一想。
请尝尝这道菜。|Please have a taste of this dish.||请尝一尝这道菜。
周末我们去公园走走。|Let us go for a stroll in the park this weekend.
我想休息休息。|I would like to rest a little.
我们需要讨论讨论。|We need to discuss it a bit.
你问问他吧。|Why don't you ask him?||你问一问他吧。
有空来我家坐坐。|Come spend a little time at my home when you are free.

# Causing or allowing with 让 | Person + 让 + another person + verb | core
Use 让 to introduce the person caused or allowed to act. Depending on context, it means 'let', 'have', or 'make'. The person after 让 is the subject of the following verb phrase.
老师让我们明天交作业。|The teacher told us to hand in the homework tomorrow.
请让我想一想。|Please let me think it over.
这个消息让我很高兴。|This news makes me happy.
妈妈不让我晚上出去。|Mom does not let me go out at night.
他让我等了半个小时。|He made me wait for half an hour.
这首歌让我想起了家乡。|This song reminds me of my hometown.
请让孩子自己试试。|Please let the child try it independently.
经理让我负责这个项目。|The manager put me in charge of this project.
别让客人等太久。|Do not keep the guests waiting too long.
这件事让我很失望。|This matter makes me disappointed.

# Helping with 帮 | 帮 + person + verb | review
Place the person being helped after 帮 and the action after that person. 帮忙 can also mean 'help out', but its object behavior differs; avoid treating 帮忙 as a freely transitive replacement for 帮.
你能帮我搬一下箱子吗？|Can you help me move the suitcase?
她帮妈妈洗碗。|She helps Mom wash the dishes.
我帮你买票吧。|Let me help you buy tickets.
请帮我看一下行李。|Please watch my luggage for a moment.
他经常帮同学复习。|He often helps his classmates review.
我们帮邻居找到了狗。|We helped the neighbor find the dog.
你可以帮我翻译这句话吗？|Can you help me translate this sentence?
姐姐帮我选了一件外套。|My older sister helped me choose a coat.
谢谢你帮我解决这个问题。|Thank you for helping me solve this problem.
我想帮你准备晚饭。|I want to help you prepare dinner.

# Giving and recipients with 给 | 给 + recipient + verb / verb + 给 + recipient | core
给 introduces a recipient or beneficiary. It can appear before an action, as in 给他打电话, or after suitable transfer verbs, as in 送给他. Which position works depends on the verb and intended meaning.
我给妈妈打了电话。|I called Mom.
她给朋友写了一封信。|She wrote a letter to a friend.
请给我倒一杯水。|Please pour me a glass of water.
他送给我一本书。|He gave me a book as a gift.
我把钱还给他了。|I returned the money to him.
你能给我解释一下吗？|Can you explain it to me?
她给孩子买了一双鞋。|She bought a pair of shoes for the child.
请把照片发给我。|Please send the photo to me.
我想给你一个建议。|I would like to give you a suggestion.
老师给我们讲了一个故事。|The teacher told us a story.

# Toward and concerning with 对 | 对 + person/thing + attitude/effect | core
Use 对 before the person or thing targeted by an attitude, interest, or effect. Common patterns include 对…感兴趣 and 对…有帮助. 对 is not a universal translation of English 'to'.
我对中国历史感兴趣。|I am interested in Chinese history.
运动对身体有好处。|Exercise is good for the body.
他对孩子很有耐心。|He is very patient with children.
这个办法对我很有帮助。|This method is very helpful to me.
她对结果很满意。|She is satisfied with the result.
你对这个计划有什么看法？|What is your view of this plan?
抽烟对健康不好。|Smoking is bad for health.
老师对我们很严格。|The teacher is strict with us.
我对这件事不太了解。|I do not know much about this matter.
他对自己的未来很有信心。|He is confident about his future.

# Distance with 离 | Place A + 离 + place B + distance | review
Use 离 to introduce the reference point in a distance statement. Add 远, 近, or a measured distance after that point. 离 differs from 从, which marks a starting point for movement or a range.
我家离学校很近。|My home is close to the school.
机场离市中心很远。|The airport is far from the city center.
这里离车站有两公里。|It is two kilometers from here to the station.
你住的地方离公司远吗？|Is where you live far from the company?
超市离我们家只有五百米。|The supermarket is only five hundred meters from our home.
这家酒店离海边很近。|This hotel is close to the seaside.
银行离这里不远。|The bank is not far from here.
我坐得离门很近。|I am sitting close to the door.
新办公室离地铁站更近。|The new office is closer to the subway station.
学校离医院大约三公里。|The school is about three kilometers from the hospital.

# Ranges with 从…到… | 从 + start + 到 + endpoint | review
Use 从…到… to mark the endpoints of a range in space or time. For a journey, the travel verb or duration can follow the range. The endpoint is explicit, unlike a simple location phrase with 在.
从这里到机场要一个小时。|It takes an hour to get from here to the airport.
我从星期一到星期五上班。|I work from Monday to Friday.
这家店从早上九点开到晚上十点。|This shop is open from nine in the morning until ten at night.
从北京到上海坐高铁很方便。|Taking the high-speed train from Beijing to Shanghai is convenient.
我们从山脚走到了山顶。|We walked from the foot of the mountain to the top.
请从第一页读到第五页。|Please read from page one through page five.
他从早到晚都很忙。|He is busy from morning to night.
从我家到学校只有两站。|It is only two stops from my home to the school.
她从去年到现在一直在这里工作。|She has been working here continuously from last year until now.
从六月到八月，这里很热。|It is hot here from June to August.

# Direction with 往 | 往 + direction + motion verb | core
Place 往 and a direction before the motion verb to indicate movement toward that direction. 向 can also mark direction in many contexts, but neither automatically means the destination is reached.
请往左走。|Please go to the left.||请向左走。
一直往前走。|Keep going straight ahead.||一直向前走。
他往车站跑去。|He ran toward the station.
这辆公交车往北开。|This bus travels north.
请往里面坐一点儿。|Please sit a little farther inside.
我们往山上走吧。|Let us walk up toward the mountain.
她往窗外看了一眼。|She glanced out of the window.
往右拐就是银行。|Turn right and you will be at the bank.
别往后退。|Do not step backward.||不要往后退。
他把椅子往前搬了一点儿。|He moved the chair a little forward.
