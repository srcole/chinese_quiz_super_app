# Choosing a better alternative with 与其…不如… | 与其 + option A，不如 + preferred option B | stretch
Use 与其…不如… to recommend one course of action over another. The second option is presented as more worthwhile; the construction compares choices rather than simply comparing qualities.
与其抱怨，不如想办法。|Rather than complain, it is better to find a solution.
与其等他，不如先走。|Rather than wait for him, it is better to leave first.
与其买新的，不如修好旧的。|Rather than buy a new one, it is better to repair the old one.
与其猜测，不如直接问她。|Rather than speculate, it is better to ask her directly.
与其熬夜工作，不如早点休息。|Rather than work late into the night, it is better to rest early.
与其追求数量，不如提高质量。|Rather than pursue quantity, it is better to improve quality.
与其争论谁对谁错，不如解决问题。|Rather than argue about who is right, it is better to solve the problem.
与其把钱存起来，不如用来学习。|Rather than save the money, it is better to use it for learning.
与其临时改变计划，不如提前做好准备。|Rather than change the plan at the last minute, it is better to prepare in advance.
与其依赖别人，不如学会自己处理。|Rather than depend on others, it is better to learn to handle it yourself.

# Choosing a sacrifice with 宁可…也不… | 宁可 + acceptable sacrifice，也不 + rejected action | stretch
Use 宁可…也不… to express a firm preference, even when the preferred choice has a cost. The speaker accepts the first situation rather than doing the second action.
我宁可走路，也不坐他的车。|I would rather walk than ride in his car.
她宁可少挣钱，也不放弃自己的原则。|She would rather earn less than give up her principles.
我宁可晚点到，也不开快车。|I would rather arrive late than drive fast.
他宁可自己吃亏，也不欺骗朋友。|He would rather suffer a loss himself than deceive a friend.
我们宁可重新开始，也不接受这样的条件。|We would rather start over than accept such conditions.
她宁可一个人完成，也不交出不合格的作品。|She would rather finish it alone than submit substandard work.
我宁可多花一点钱，也不买假货。|I would rather spend a little more than buy counterfeit goods.
他宁可承认错误，也不找借口。|He would rather admit his mistake than make excuses.
我宁可暂时失业，也不做违法的事。|I would rather be unemployed temporarily than do something illegal.
他们宁可推迟开业，也不降低安全标准。|They would rather delay opening than lower safety standards.

# Reasoning from an accepted fact with 既然…就… | 既然 + accepted fact，就 + conclusion | stretch
既然 introduces a fact the speaker treats as established. 就 leads to a decision, suggestion, or conclusion based on that fact, rather than a hypothetical condition.
既然你已经决定了，就不要犹豫。|Since you have already decided, do not hesitate.
既然大家都到了，就开始吧。|Since everyone is here, let us begin.
既然答应了，就要做到。|Since you promised, you must follow through.
既然时间不够，就先讨论重点。|Since there is not enough time, let us discuss the main points first.
既然他不愿意，我们就别勉强他。|Since he is unwilling, let us not pressure him.
既然发现了问题，就应该及时处理。|Since the problem has been discovered, it should be dealt with promptly.
既然你了解情况，就请你解释一下。|Since you know the situation, please explain it.
既然这条路不通，我们就换一条。|Since this road is blocked, let us take another one.
既然材料已经准备好了，就可以申请了。|Since the documents are ready, we can apply now.
既然选择了这份工作，就要承担相应的责任。|Since you chose this job, you must take on the corresponding responsibilities.

# Acknowledging a fact with 尽管…仍然… | 尽管 + fact，subject + 仍然 + contrasting result | stretch
尽管 introduces an actual circumstance that could have prevented the result. 仍然 means 'still' and highlights that the result holds despite it. This use of 尽管 means 'although', not the separate inviting sense 'feel free to'.
尽管很累，她仍然坚持训练。|Although she is tired, she still keeps training.
尽管价格上涨，需求仍然很大。|Although prices have risen, demand is still high.
尽管大家反对，他仍然没有改变主意。|Although everyone objected, he still did not change his mind.
尽管下着大雨，比赛仍然照常进行。|Although it is raining heavily, the competition is still proceeding as usual.
尽管已经道歉，他们仍然不肯原谅他。|Although he has apologized, they still refuse to forgive him.
尽管条件有限，我们仍然完成了任务。|Although resources were limited, we still completed the task.
尽管离开多年，她仍然记得那条小路。|Although she has been away for years, she still remembers that little path.
尽管有了新证据，结论仍然没有改变。|Although there is new evidence, the conclusion still has not changed.
尽管收入不高，他仍然每月捐款。|Although his income is not high, he still donates every month.
尽管失败过几次，她仍然相信自己。|Although she has failed several times, she still believes in herself.

# Accepting an extreme possibility with 哪怕…也… | 哪怕 + extreme condition，也 + unchanged response | stretch
哪怕 introduces even an extreme or minimal hypothetical case. The main clause says what will still hold under that condition; it often expresses determination or the value of even a small amount.
哪怕只有一个人支持，我也要试试。|Even if only one person supports me, I will still try.
哪怕下雪，我们也按时出发。|Even if it snows, we will still leave on time.
哪怕再忙，你也要吃饭。|Even if you are extremely busy, you still need to eat.
哪怕只能帮一点忙，我也愿意。|Even if I can only help a little, I am willing.
哪怕失败，我也不会后悔。|Even if I fail, I will not regret it.
哪怕他亲自来，我也不会答应。|Even if he comes in person, I will not agree.
哪怕只剩一天，我们也不能放弃。|Even if only one day remains, we must not give up.
哪怕贵一点，我也想买质量好的。|Even if it costs a little more, I still want to buy something of good quality.
哪怕没有人发现，你也应该说实话。|Even if nobody finds out, you should still tell the truth.
哪怕每天只练十分钟，也会有进步。|Even practicing for just ten minutes a day will lead to progress.

# Stating an exception with 除非…否则… | 除非 + necessary exception，否则 + default outcome | stretch
除非 sets out the exception needed to avoid the outcome after 否则. The structure means 'unless...otherwise...' and does not promise that the exception alone guarantees success.
除非下雨，否则我们明天出发。|Unless it rains, we will leave tomorrow.
除非你亲自解释，否则他不会相信。|Unless you explain in person, he will not believe it.
除非价格降低，否则我不考虑购买。|Unless the price drops, I will not consider buying it.
除非提前预约，否则不能参观。|Unless you book in advance, you cannot visit.
除非有新的证据，否则不能改变结论。|Unless there is new evidence, the conclusion cannot be changed.
除非大家都同意，否则计划不变。|Unless everyone agrees, the plan will remain unchanged.
除非你愿意加班，否则今天做不完。|Unless you are willing to work overtime, it cannot be finished today.
除非情况紧急，否则不要打这个电话。|Unless the situation is urgent, do not call this number.
除非得到允许，否则不能拍照。|Unless you obtain permission, you cannot take photos.
除非他道歉，否则我不会再见他。|Unless he apologizes, I will not see him again.

# Covering every alternative with 无论…都… | 无论 + question phrase/alternatives，都 + constant result | stretch
无论 means 'regardless of' and introduces a range of possibilities, usually through a question word or alternatives with 还是. 都 marks the result that stays the same across those possibilities; 无论 is common in formal speech and writing.
无论结果如何，我都接受。|Whatever the result is, I will accept it.
无论谁来，都要登记。|Whoever comes must register.
无论你在哪里，我都会支持你。|Wherever you are, I will support you.
无论天气多冷，他都坚持跑步。|No matter how cold it is, he keeps running.
无论成功还是失败，我们都能学到东西。|Whether we succeed or fail, we can learn something.
无论发生什么，都不要惊慌。|Whatever happens, do not panic.
无论用哪种方法，都要保证安全。|Whichever method you use, you must ensure safety.
无论收入多少，她都会存一部分。|No matter how much she earns, she saves a portion.
无论是老师还是学生，都应该遵守规定。|Teachers and students alike should follow the rules.
无论你什么时候需要帮助，都可以联系我。|Whenever you need help, you can contact me.

# Arguing from the stronger case with 尚且…更何况… | Stronger case + 尚且 + difficulty，更何况 + weaker case | stretch
尚且 means 'even' in a relatively formal argument. If even the first person or situation faces a difficulty, 更何况 implies that the second faces it even more. The second clause often leaves the shared predicate unstated.
专家尚且会犯错，更何况初学者。|Even experts make mistakes, let alone beginners.
大人尚且受不了，更何况孩子。|Even adults cannot stand it, let alone children.
本地人尚且会迷路，更何况游客。|Even locals can get lost, let alone tourists.
他尚且不敢去，更何况我。|Even he does not dare to go, let alone me.
这么简单的题他尚且不会，更何况难题。|He cannot even do such a simple question, let alone a difficult one.
走路尚且困难，更何况跑步。|Even walking is difficult, let alone running.
母语者尚且需要查词典，更何况外国学生。|Even native speakers need to consult a dictionary, let alone foreign students.
一个人尚且忙不过来，更何况还要照顾孩子。|Even managing alone is too much, let alone also having to care for children.
晴天尚且看不清，更何况雾天。|Even on a clear day it is hard to see clearly, let alone on a foggy day.
维持现状尚且不容易，更何况扩大规模。|Even maintaining the current situation is not easy, let alone expanding.

# Reversing an expected outcome with 不仅没有…反而… | 不仅没有 + expected event，反而 + opposite result | stretch
Use 不仅没有…反而… when the expected improvement or event did not happen and the opposite happened instead. This is stronger than merely adding a second fact with 不但…而且….
价格不仅没有下降，反而上涨了。|The price not only failed to fall; it rose instead.
他不仅没有生气，反而笑了。|He not only did not get angry; he laughed instead.
休息以后，我不仅没有精神，反而更困了。|After resting, I not only failed to feel refreshed; I felt sleepier instead.
这次解释不仅没有消除误会，反而引起了争论。|This explanation not only failed to clear up the misunderstanding; it sparked an argument instead.
人数不仅没有减少，反而增加了。|The number of people not only failed to decrease; it increased instead.
她不仅没有放弃，反而更加努力了。|She not only did not give up; she worked even harder instead.
新规定不仅没有节省时间，反而增加了工作量。|The new rule not only failed to save time; it increased the workload instead.
雨不仅没有停，反而越下越大。|The rain not only failed to stop; it grew heavier instead.
他不仅没有感谢我，反而责怪我。|He not only failed to thank me; he blamed me instead.
搬家后，路程不仅没有缩短，反而变长了。|After the move, the journey not only failed to get shorter; it became longer instead.

# Correcting a characterization with 不是…而是… | 不是 + rejected explanation，而是 + actual explanation | stretch
Use 不是…而是… to replace an incorrect characterization with the intended one. Keep the two parts parallel, whether they are nouns, actions, or full explanations.
我不是不想去，而是没有时间。|It is not that I do not want to go; I do not have time.
这不是结束，而是新的开始。|This is not an ending, but a new beginning.
他不是老师，而是翻译。|He is not a teacher, but a translator.
问题不是价格，而是质量。|The issue is not the price, but the quality.
她不是忘了，而是故意没说。|She did not forget; she deliberately said nothing.
我们需要的不是借口，而是行动。|What we need is not excuses, but action.
这不是运气，而是长期努力的结果。|This is not luck, but the result of sustained effort.
我担心的不是失败，而是失去信任。|What worries me is not failure, but losing trust.
他来这里不是为了旅游，而是为了工作。|He came here not to travel, but to work.
决定成败的不是速度，而是方向。|What determines success or failure is not speed, but direction.

# Offering mutually exclusive choices with 要么…要么… | 要么 + choice A，要么 + choice B | stretch
要么…要么… presents alternatives to choose between, often with the implication that one must be selected. Unlike 还是 in a direct choice question, this construction commonly states the available choices.
我们要么坐火车，要么坐飞机。|We can either take a train or fly.
你要么现在决定，要么放弃这次机会。|Either decide now or give up this opportunity.
要么你来，要么我去。|Either you come or I go.
周末我要么看书，要么出去散步。|On weekends I either read or go out for a walk.
这个房间要么做书房，要么做客房。|This room can be either a study or a guest room.
我们要么减少开支，要么增加收入。|We must either reduce spending or increase income.
你要么接受条件，要么退出合作。|Either accept the conditions or withdraw from the partnership.
他要么没听见，要么没听懂。|Either he did not hear it or he did not understand it.
午饭要么在家吃，要么去附近的饭店吃。|For lunch, we can either eat at home or at a nearby restaurant.
这些书要么捐出去，要么留下自己看。|These books can either be donated or kept for us to read.

# Describing recurring alternatives with 不是…就是… | 不是 + possibility A，就是 + possibility B | stretch
不是…就是… says that one of two possibilities holds. It often describes a repeated pattern or a deduction, rather than inviting the listener to choose an option.
他周末不是加班，就是出差。|On weekends he is either working overtime or away on business.
她不是在图书馆，就是在实验室。|She is either in the library or in the laboratory.
最近不是下雨，就是刮风。|Recently it has been either raining or windy.
他一开口不是抱怨，就是批评。|Whenever he speaks, he is either complaining or criticizing.
这把钥匙不是你的，就是他的。|This key is either yours or his.
她每天不是开会，就是写报告。|Every day she is either attending meetings or writing reports.
孩子放学后不是画画，就是搭积木。|After school the child is either drawing or building with blocks.
这个时间他不是在路上，就是已经到家了。|At this time he is either on his way or already home.
他买的衣服不是黑色的，就是灰色的。|The clothes he buys are either black or gray.
这几天来找我的人不是同事，就是邻居。|The people who have come to see me these past few days have been either colleagues or neighbors.

# A consequential trigger with 一旦…就… | 一旦 + triggering event，就 + consequence | stretch
一旦 means 'once' or 'if...ever' and emphasizes a trigger with an important consequence. The consequence can be automatic, difficult to reverse, or a planned response.
一旦签字，合同就生效。|Once it is signed, the contract takes effect.
一旦失去信任，就很难恢复。|Once trust is lost, it is hard to restore.
一旦发现问题，就要立即报告。|Once a problem is discovered, it must be reported immediately.
一旦习惯了，就不觉得麻烦了。|Once you get used to it, it no longer feels troublesome.
一旦错过末班车，就只能打车了。|If you miss the last bus, you will have to take a taxi.
一旦开始，就不要轻易放弃。|Once you start, do not give up easily.
一旦水位超过这条线，就会发出警报。|Once the water level exceeds this line, an alarm will sound.
一旦泄露密码，账户就可能被盗。|Once the password is leaked, the account could be stolen.
一旦有消息，我就通知你。|As soon as there is news, I will notify you.
一旦做出承诺，就应该遵守。|Once you make a promise, you should keep it.

# Preparing for a contingency with 万一 | 万一 + unlikely unwanted event，response / 怎么办？ | stretch
万一 introduces an unlikely but possible event that the speaker wants to prepare for. It often appears with a precaution or 怎么办 to ask what to do if the event happens.
万一下雨，我们就改在室内举行。|If it happens to rain, we will hold it indoors instead.
万一他不来，怎么办？|What should we do if he does not come?
带上充电器，万一手机没电呢？|Bring the charger; what if your phone runs out of power?
万一迷路了，就给我打电话。|If you happen to get lost, call me.
万一航班取消，我们就坐火车。|If the flight happens to be canceled, we will take a train.
多带一点现金，万一不能刷卡呢？|Bring some extra cash; what if cards are not accepted?
万一忘了密码，可以用邮箱找回。|If you happen to forget the password, you can recover it by email.
万一出了问题，由谁负责？|Who will be responsible if something goes wrong?
万一时间不够，就先做最重要的部分。|If time runs short, do the most important part first.
万一你联系不上我，就找前台。|If you happen to be unable to reach me, contact the front desk.

# Preventing an unwanted result with 以免 | Precaution + 以免 + unwanted consequence | stretch
以免 introduces the unwanted result that a precaution is intended to prevent. It means 'to avoid' or 'lest' and is common in instructions and careful written explanations.
请提前出发，以免迟到。|Please leave early to avoid being late.
把地址写下来，以免忘记。|Write down the address so that you do not forget it.
请轻声说话，以免打扰别人。|Please speak quietly to avoid disturbing others.
先备份文件，以免丢失重要资料。|Back up the files first to avoid losing important material.
请仔细核对，以免出现错误。|Please check carefully to avoid errors.
把杯子放远一点，以免孩子碰到。|Put the cup farther away so the child does not knock into it.
出门前查一下路线，以免走错路。|Check the route before leaving to avoid taking the wrong road.
请保留收据，以免退货时遇到麻烦。|Please keep the receipt to avoid trouble when returning the item.
把窗户关上，以免雨水进来。|Close the window to keep rain from coming in.
我们最好提前沟通，以免产生误会。|We had better communicate in advance to avoid misunderstandings.

# Enabling a desired result with 以便 | Preparatory action + 以便 + intended benefit | stretch
以便 introduces the positive purpose of an action: making a later action possible or easier. It is common in formal requests and differs from 以免, which introduces a result to avoid.
请留下电话，以便我们联系您。|Please leave your phone number so that we can contact you.
我把资料整理好了，以便大家查阅。|I organized the materials so that everyone can consult them.
请提前报名，以便安排座位。|Please register in advance so that seats can be arranged.
我们需要确认人数，以便准备午餐。|We need to confirm the number of people so that we can prepare lunch.
请写清楚地址，以便快递员找到您。|Please write the address clearly so that the courier can find you.
她录下了讲座，以便以后复习。|She recorded the lecture so that she could review it later.
请把问题发给我，以便我提前准备。|Please send me the questions so that I can prepare in advance.
我把椅子挪开了，以便轮椅通过。|I moved the chair aside so that a wheelchair could pass through.
请注明日期，以便我们核对。|Please indicate the date so that we can check it.
我们建了一个群，以便及时交流。|We created a group chat so that we could communicate promptly.

# Highlighting a cause with 之所以…是因为… | Subject + 之所以 + result，是因为 + cause | stretch
之所以 marks the result that needs explanation, and 是因为 introduces its cause. This construction foregrounds why something is true; do not put the cause immediately after 之所以.
我之所以迟到，是因为路上堵车。|The reason I was late is that traffic was heavy.
她之所以辞职，是因为想继续学习。|The reason she resigned is that she wanted to continue studying.
这家店之所以受欢迎，是因为服务很好。|The reason this shop is popular is that its service is good.
他之所以沉默，是因为不知道该说什么。|The reason he is silent is that he does not know what to say.
我们之所以改变计划，是因为天气不好。|The reason we changed the plan is that the weather was bad.
我之所以反对，是因为风险太大。|The reason I object is that the risk is too great.
她之所以进步快，是因为每天练习。|The reason she improves quickly is that she practices every day.
这个问题之所以复杂，是因为涉及很多部门。|The reason this issue is complicated is that it involves many departments.
他之所以回来，是因为放心不下家人。|The reason he came back is that he could not stop worrying about his family.
我们之所以成功，是因为大家互相信任。|The reason we succeeded is that everyone trusted one another.

# Applying a rule universally with 凡是…都… | 凡是 + qualifying group，都 + shared rule | stretch
凡是 introduces every person or thing meeting a condition. 都 applies a statement to the entire group. It is especially useful for policies, generalizations, and formal descriptions.
凡是参加活动的人，都要报名。|Everyone taking part in the activity must register.
凡是过期的食品，都不能出售。|No expired food may be sold.
凡是他推荐的书，我都想看看。|I want to read every book he recommends.
凡是进入实验室的人，都必须戴护目镜。|Everyone entering the laboratory must wear safety goggles.
凡是符合条件的申请者，都可以参加面试。|Every applicant who meets the requirements can attend an interview.
凡是借走的书，都要按时归还。|Every borrowed book must be returned on time.
凡是涉及安全的问题，都应该认真处理。|Every issue involving safety should be handled carefully.
凡是答应别人的事，都应该尽力做到。|You should do your best to fulfill every promise you make to others.
凡是需要签字的文件，都放在这里。|All documents requiring a signature are placed here.
凡是发现错误的人，都可以提出修改意见。|Anyone who finds an error can suggest a correction.

# Remaining unmoved with 任凭…也… | 任凭 + action/condition，subject + 也 + unchanged response | stretch
任凭 emphasizes that even persistent efforts or varying circumstances cannot change the main result. With 也 and a negative predicate, it often means 'no matter how much someone tries'.
任凭我怎么解释，他也不相信。|No matter how I explain it, he will not believe me.
任凭大家劝说，她也不肯改变主意。|No matter how much everyone tries to persuade her, she will not change her mind.
任凭风怎么吹，这棵树也没有倒。|No matter how hard the wind blew, this tree did not fall.
任凭他怎么喊，也没有人回答。|No matter how much he shouted, nobody answered.
任凭条件多么艰苦，他们也没有放弃。|No matter how harsh the conditions were, they did not give up.
任凭别人怎么评价，她也坚持自己的选择。|No matter how others judge her, she stands by her choice.
任凭我怎么找，也找不到那张照片。|No matter how much I search, I cannot find that photo.
任凭价格怎么变，他也不打算卖。|No matter how the price changes, he does not plan to sell.
任凭孩子怎么请求，妈妈也不答应。|No matter how much the child pleads, Mom will not agree.
任凭外面多么吵闹，他也能专心看书。|No matter how noisy it is outside, he can concentrate on reading.

# Taking advantage of a window with 趁 | 趁 + favorable circumstance + action | stretch
趁 introduces a temporary opportunity or favorable condition for an action. It often means 'while there is still a chance' and can take a short phrase or a clause.
趁天还没黑，我们赶快回去吧。|Let us hurry back while it is still light.
趁热吃吧。|Eat it while it is hot.
趁大家都在，我说两句。|While everyone is here, let me say a few words.
趁现在有空，把邮件回复了吧。|Reply to the emails while you have time now.
趁商店还没关门，我们去买点东西。|Let us buy a few things before the shop closes.
趁年轻，多学点本事。|Learn more skills while you are young.
趁这个机会，我想感谢大家。|I would like to take this opportunity to thank everyone.
趁孩子睡着了，她休息了一会儿。|She rested for a while when the child was asleep.
趁天气好，我们把被子晒一晒。|Let us air the quilts in the sun while the weather is good.
趁还来得及，赶快修改。|Revise it quickly while there is still time.

# Describing parallel development with 随着 | 随着 + ongoing change，related development | stretch
随着 introduces a change that accompanies another development. It means 'as' or 'with' in descriptions of gradual change, rather than specifying a single completed event.
随着气温下降，湖面开始结冰。|As the temperature falls, the lake begins to freeze.
随着年龄增长，他变得更有耐心。|As he gets older, he becomes more patient.
随着技术进步，成本逐渐降低。|As technology advances, costs gradually decrease.
随着人口增加，住房需求不断上升。|As the population increases, demand for housing keeps rising.
随着经验积累，她越来越自信。|As she gains experience, she becomes increasingly confident.
随着夜色加深，街上的人越来越少。|As night deepens, there are fewer and fewer people on the streets.
随着收入提高，他们开始考虑买房。|As their income rises, they begin considering buying a home.
随着调查深入，更多细节被发现了。|As the investigation progressed, more details were discovered.
随着交通改善，来这里的游客越来越多。|As transportation improves, more and more tourists come here.
随着讨论继续，大家的意见逐渐接近。|As the discussion continues, everyone's views gradually converge.

# Tracing a continuing situation with 自从…以来… | 自从 + starting event + 以来，continuing development | stretch
自从 identifies the starting point of a situation that continues or has effects afterward. 以来 makes the span from that point explicit; the main clause often includes 一直, 再也没, or a description of change.
自从搬家以来，我一直坐地铁上班。|Ever since moving, I have been taking the subway to work.
自从认识她以来，我学到了很多。|Since meeting her, I have learned a lot.
自从毕业以来，我们只见过两次面。|Since graduating, we have met only twice.
自从换了工作以来，他很少加班。|Since changing jobs, he has rarely worked overtime.
自从那次争论以来，他们再也没联系过。|Since that argument, they have never contacted each other again.
自从开始跑步以来，她的睡眠好多了。|Since she started running, her sleep has improved greatly.
自从这家店开业以来，生意一直很好。|Ever since this shop opened, business has been good.
自从孩子出生以来，我们的生活变化很大。|Since the child was born, our lives have changed greatly.
自从实行新规定以来，事故减少了。|Since the new rule was implemented, accidents have decreased.
自从来到这里以来，我每天都很忙。|Ever since coming here, I have been busy every day.

# Marking a delayed endpoint with 直到…才… | 直到 + time/event，subject + 才 + delayed action | stretch
直到…才… means that an action did not occur until the stated time or event. 才 goes before the delayed action and highlights how late it happened.
直到半夜，他才回家。|He did not come home until midnight.
直到老师解释，我才明白。|I did not understand until the teacher explained.
直到最后一天，她才提交申请。|She did not submit her application until the last day.
直到雨停了，我们才出发。|We did not leave until the rain stopped.
直到见到实物，我才相信。|I did not believe it until I saw the actual object.
直到孩子睡着，妈妈才开始工作。|Mom did not start working until the child fell asleep.
直到收到通知，他才知道会议取消了。|He did not learn the meeting was canceled until he received the notice.
直到离开家乡，我才体会到家的重要。|I did not appreciate the importance of home until I left my hometown.
直到检查了三遍，她才放心。|She did not feel reassured until she had checked three times.
直到问题解决，大家才松了一口气。|Nobody breathed a sigh of relief until the problem was solved.

# Denying an extreme outcome with 不至于 | Situation + 不至于 + extreme result | stretch
不至于 says that something will not reach a particular, usually undesirable, degree. It can reassure someone or suggest that a reaction is excessive; it does not simply mean that an event is impossible.
只是晚了五分钟，不至于错过整场演出。|It is only a five-minute delay; we will not miss the entire performance.
他有点生气，但不至于辞职。|He is a little angry, but not to the point of resigning.
多穿一件衣服，就不至于那么冷。|Wear an extra layer, and you will not be so cold.
提前做个计划，就不至于手忙脚乱。|Make a plan in advance, and you will not end up in a fluster.
这点困难不至于让我们放弃。|This small difficulty will not drive us to give up.
少赚一点钱，也不至于影响生活。|Earning a little less will not go so far as to affect our daily lives.
带点吃的，路上就不至于饿肚子。|Bring some food, and you will not go hungry on the way.
他虽然健忘，但不至于忘记自己的生日。|Although he is forgetful, he is not so forgetful as to forget his own birthday.
我们意见不同，但不至于为此吵架。|We disagree, but not enough to argue over it.
有这笔存款，我们暂时不至于陷入困境。|With these savings, we will not fall into hardship for the time being.

# Challenging certainty with 未必 | Subject + 未必 + predicate | stretch
未必 means 'not necessarily'. It questions whether a conclusion must be true, rather than asserting the opposite. It commonly challenges assumptions based on price, appearance, or status.
贵的东西未必好。|Expensive things are not necessarily good.
他未必知道这件事。|He does not necessarily know about this matter.
人多未必效率高。|Having more people does not necessarily mean greater efficiency.
现在出发未必来得及。|Leaving now will not necessarily get us there in time.
她不说话，未必是不同意。|Her silence does not necessarily mean she disagrees.
有经验的人未必适合这个职位。|An experienced person is not necessarily suited to this position.
看起来容易的事，做起来未必容易。|Something that looks easy is not necessarily easy to do.
换个地方未必能解决问题。|Moving somewhere else will not necessarily solve the problem.
大家都喜欢的书，你未必喜欢。|You will not necessarily like a book that everyone else likes.
这次失败未必是坏事。|This failure is not necessarily a bad thing.

# Acknowledging an unavoidable tendency with 难免 | Subject/situation + 难免 + unwanted event | stretch
难免 means that something is hard to avoid and often understandable under the circumstances. It usually introduces an unwelcome feeling, mistake, or difficulty without blaming the person harshly.
第一次上台，难免紧张。|It is hard not to feel nervous the first time you go on stage.
工作太忙，难免出错。|When work is too busy, mistakes are hard to avoid.
离开家久了，难免想家。|After being away from home for a long time, homesickness is hard to avoid.
大家一起生活，难免有矛盾。|When people live together, conflicts are hard to avoid.
刚换工作，难免有些不适应。|When you have just changed jobs, some difficulty adjusting is hard to avoid.
听到这样的消息，他难免失望。|Hearing such news, he can hardly avoid feeling disappointed.
准备得再充分，也难免遇到意外。|No matter how thoroughly you prepare, unexpected events are hard to avoid.
学一门新语言，难免说错话。|When learning a new language, saying things incorrectly is hard to avoid.
长时间开车，难免疲劳。|When driving for a long time, fatigue is hard to avoid.
对情况不了解，难免产生误会。|Without understanding the situation, misunderstandings are hard to avoid.

# Reducing an explanation with 无非 | Subject + 无非 + 是/想/为了 + limited explanation | stretch
无非 means 'nothing more than' and narrows the possibilities or motives to something the speaker considers simple. It can sound dismissive, so its tone depends on context.
他无非是想得到认可。|He simply wants recognition.
办法无非是这两种。|The options are nothing more than these two methods.
她这么做，无非是为了保护家人。|She is doing this simply to protect her family.
我无非是提个建议。|I am merely making a suggestion.
他们争论的无非是钱的问题。|What they are arguing about is nothing more than money.
结果无非是成功或失败。|The outcome is simply success or failure.
他反复解释，无非是怕你误会。|He keeps explaining simply because he fears you will misunderstand.
这个工具无非是帮我们节省时间。|This tool merely helps us save time.
她要的无非是一句道歉。|All she wants is an apology.
多练几次，无非是多花一点时间。|Practicing a few more times just means spending a little more time.

# Tentative surprised inference with 莫非 | 莫非 + surprising possibility？ | stretch
莫非 introduces a tentative explanation that has just occurred to the speaker, often with surprise or suspicion. It means 'could it be that...?' rather than directly asking for a known fact.
莫非你们早就认识？|Could it be that you two have known each other for a long time?
莫非他忘了今天的约会？|Could it be that he forgot today's appointment?
莫非这就是你说的那家店？|Could this be the shop you mentioned?
莫非她已经知道结果了？|Could it be that she already knows the result?
莫非我们走错路了？|Could it be that we took the wrong road?
莫非你不打算参加了？|Could it be that you no longer plan to participate?
莫非门票已经卖完了？|Could it be that the tickets have already sold out?
莫非他是在开玩笑？|Could it be that he is joking?
莫非这封信是你写的？|Could it be that you wrote this letter?
莫非他们提前出发了？|Could it be that they left ahead of schedule?

# Challenging an assumption with 难道 | 难道 + proposition + 吗？ | stretch
难道 marks a rhetorical question expressing disbelief or challenging an assumption. A positive proposition often implies a negative answer, and a negative proposition often implies a positive one. It is stronger than a neutral 吗 question.
难道你不记得我了吗？|Don't you remember me?
难道这还不够清楚吗？|Isn't this clear enough already?
难道我们就这样放弃吗？|Are we really going to give up just like this?
难道钱比健康更重要吗？|Is money really more important than health?
难道你从来没犯过错吗？|Have you really never made a mistake?
难道这都是我的责任吗？|Is all of this really my responsibility?
难道你没收到通知吗？|Didn't you receive the notice?
难道努力没有意义吗？|Does making an effort really have no meaning?
难道他说什么你都相信吗？|Do you really believe everything he says?
难道我们不应该先听听她的意见吗？|Shouldn't we listen to her opinion first?

# Insisting on necessity with 非…不可 | Subject + 非 + verb phrase + 不可 | stretch
非…不可 expresses strong necessity or determination: someone absolutely must do something. The two negative-looking parts work together to rule out not doing it; this is stronger than ordinary advice with 应该.
这件事我非弄清楚不可。|I absolutely must get to the bottom of this matter.
他非亲自去一趟不可。|He insists on making the trip in person.
这个问题今天非解决不可。|This problem absolutely must be solved today.
她非见到负责人不可。|She insists on seeing the person in charge.
我非把这本书看完不可。|I am determined to finish this book.
这笔钱非还不可。|This money absolutely must be paid back.
你非现在走不可吗？|Do you absolutely have to leave now?
这个错误非改不可。|This mistake absolutely must be corrected.
他们非等到结果出来不可。|They insist on waiting until the result comes out.
我们非找到原因不可。|We absolutely must find the cause.
