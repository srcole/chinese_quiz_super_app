# Completed actions with 了 | Verb + 了 + object | core
Put 了 after a verb to present an action as completed or bounded. It does not simply mean past tense. Time words establish when the event happened; negating the event normally uses 没 without this 了.
我昨天买了一台电脑。|I bought a computer yesterday.
她喝了一杯水。|She drank a glass of water.
我们看了一场电影。|We watched a movie.
他写了一封邮件。|He wrote an email.
我洗了三件衣服。|I washed three pieces of clothing.
妈妈做了一碗面条。|Mom made a bowl of noodles.
他们参观了那个博物馆。|They visited that museum.
我给老师打了一个电话。|I made a phone call to the teacher.
她回答了两个问题。|She answered two questions.
我们订了一个房间。|We booked a room.

# Changed situations with 了 | New situation + 了 | core
Sentence-final 了 can mark a new or changed situation, including a current state. With 不…了 it often means 'no longer' or a change of intention, not a completed past action.
天气暖和了。|The weather has become warm.
我不喝酒了。|I no longer drink alcohol.
他现在是经理了。|He is a manager now.
孩子会走路了。|The child can walk now.
我们有自己的房子了。|We have our own home now.
商店关门了。|The shop is now closed.
雨停了。|The rain has stopped.
我懂你的意思了。|I understand what you mean now.
她不生气了。|She is no longer angry.
这条路比以前宽了。|This road has become wider than before.

# Experience with 过 | Verb + 过 + object | core
Use 过 after a verb to report a past experience without focusing on a particular occurrence. Negate it with 没有…过. Do not use it merely as a substitute for completed-action 了.
我去过杭州。|I have been to Hangzhou.
她吃过这种水果。|She has eaten this kind of fruit.
我们看过这部电影。|We have seen this movie.
他坐过飞机。|He has traveled by plane.
我没骑过马。|I have never ridden a horse.||我没有骑过马。
你见过我的哥哥吗？|Have you met my older brother?
她在医院工作过。|She has worked in a hospital.
我用过这个软件。|I have used this software.
他们参加过比赛。|They have taken part in a competition.
我没听过这个故事。|I have never heard this story.||我没有听过这个故事。

# Actions in progress with 正在 | Subject + 正在 + verb | core
Place 正在 before the verb to emphasize that an action is happening at the reference time. 在 and 正 also have progressive uses. A sentence can optionally end with 呢.
我正在做作业。|I am doing my homework right now.||我正在做作业呢。
她正在开会。|She is in a meeting right now.||她正在开会呢。
他们正在修路。|They are repairing the road right now.
爸爸正在洗车。|Dad is washing the car right now.
我们正在讨论这个问题。|We are discussing this issue right now.
医生正在检查病人。|The doctor is examining the patient right now.
孩子正在睡觉。|The child is sleeping right now.
我正在找钥匙。|I am looking for my keys right now.
服务员正在收拾桌子。|The server is clearing the table right now.
她正在准备明天的考试。|She is preparing for tomorrow's exam right now.

# Continuing states with 着 | Verb + 着 | core
Attach 着 to a verb to describe a continuing state, such as an open door or something being worn. It focuses on the maintained state rather than the change that created it.
门开着。|The door is open.
窗户关着。|The window is closed.
他穿着一件蓝衬衫。|He is wearing a blue shirt.
墙上挂着一张地图。|A map is hanging on the wall.
桌子上放着一本杂志。|A magazine is lying on the table.
她戴着一副眼镜。|She is wearing a pair of glasses.
灯亮着。|The light is on.
门口站着一个男人。|A man is standing at the entrance.
孩子手里拿着一个苹果。|The child is holding an apple in their hand.
沙发上坐着两位客人。|Two guests are sitting on the sofa.

# Already with 已经 | 已经 + verb/state + 了 | core
Use 已经 to say that something has already happened or a state already holds. Sentence-final 了 is common when the state is now relevant, but is not required in every 已经 sentence.
我已经吃过饭了。|I have already eaten.
她已经到机场了。|She has already arrived at the airport.
我们已经买好票了。|We have already bought the tickets.
他已经知道这件事了。|He already knows about this matter.
电影已经开始了。|The movie has already started.
我已经做完作业了。|I have already finished my homework.
商店已经关门了。|The shop has already closed.
她已经等了一个小时了。|She has already been waiting for an hour.
我们已经决定了。|We have already decided.
水已经凉了。|The water has already cooled down.

# Not yet with 还没 | 还没(有) + verb | core
Use 还没 or 还没有 to say that an expected event has not happened yet. Unlike 不, this does not normally state a general habit or refusal.
我还没吃午饭。|I have not eaten lunch yet.||我还没有吃午饭。
他还没回来。|He has not come back yet.||他还没有回来。
我们还没决定。|We have not decided yet.||我们还没有决定。
她还没看那封邮件。|She has not read that email yet.
火车还没到。|The train has not arrived yet.
我还没找到工作。|I have not found a job yet.
孩子还没起床。|The child has not got up yet.
他们还没结婚。|They have not got married yet.
我还没想好。|I have not made up my mind yet.
饭还没做好。|The meal is not ready yet.

# Imminent events with 快要…了 | 快要 + verb + 了 | core
Use 快要…了 for an event that is about to happen. 要…了 and 就要…了 are related forms; 快要 is generally unsuitable directly with a precise future time phrase.
快要下雨了。|It is about to rain.
电影快要开始了。|The movie is about to start.
我们快要放假了。|We are about to start our vacation.
火车快要进站了。|The train is about to pull into the station.
商店快要关门了。|The shop is about to close.
我快要毕业了。|I am about to graduate.
她快要生孩子了。|She is about to have a baby.
比赛快要结束了。|The competition is about to end.
天快要黑了。|It is about to get dark.
我们快要到家了。|We are about to get home.

# Duration after the verb | Verb + 了 + duration | core
A duration normally follows the verb. With an object, one common option is verb + 了 + duration + object. A final 了 can additionally show that the activity has continued up to now.
我昨天睡了八个小时。|I slept for eight hours yesterday.
她在北京住了三年。|She lived in Beijing for three years.
我们等了二十分钟。|We waited for twenty minutes.
他昨天工作了十个小时。|He worked for ten hours yesterday.
我学了两年中文。|I studied Chinese for two years.
孩子们玩了一个下午。|The children played for an entire afternoon.
我们聊了半个小时。|We chatted for half an hour.
她休息了两天。|She rested for two days.
我跑了四十分钟。|I ran for forty minutes.
他们讨论了一个小时。|They discussed it for an hour.

# Counting occurrences with 次 | Verb + number + 次 | core
Use 次 to count how often an action happens. 次 counts occasions, whereas 遍 emphasizes complete passes through an activity such as reading a text.
我去过上海三次。|I have been to Shanghai three times.
请再说一次。|Please say it one more time.
她每天吃三次药。|She takes medicine three times a day.
我们每周开一次会。|We have a meeting once a week.
他给我打了两次电话。|He called me twice.
我试了五次。|I tried five times.
这台电脑今天死机了两次。|This computer crashed twice today.
我每年体检一次。|I have a medical checkup once a year.
她问了我三次。|She asked me three times.
我们见过几次面。|We have met a few times.

# Complete repetitions with 遍 | Verb + number + 遍 | core
Use 遍 to count complete runs through an action from beginning to end. It is especially useful for reading, listening, writing, and checking an entire piece of content.
请把课文读一遍。|Please read the text all the way through once.
我看了两遍这部电影。|I watched this movie all the way through twice.
她把地址写了三遍。|She wrote the address out three times.
请再检查一遍。|Please check everything through once more.
我听了五遍这段录音。|I listened to this recording all the way through five times.
老师把问题解释了一遍。|The teacher explained the whole question once.
他把名单看了一遍。|He read through the list once.
我们把房间打扫了一遍。|We cleaned the whole room once.
请把这个句子抄两遍。|Please copy this sentence twice in full.
我把报告从头到尾读了三遍。|I read the report from beginning to end three times.

# Again in the future with 再 | 再 + verb | core
Use 再 for a further occurrence that is requested, planned, or otherwise not yet realized. It can mean 'again' or 'then', depending on context. Contrast 又 for an occurrence presented as happening again.
请再说一遍。|Please say it all the way through again.
我明天再来。|I will come again tomorrow.
我们下次再讨论。|We will discuss it again next time.
请再给我一次机会。|Please give me another chance.
这件事你再想想。|Think about this matter some more.
我想再买一本书。|I want to buy one more book.
休息一下再走吧。|Rest a little, then leave.
请再等五分钟。|Please wait another five minutes.
我们吃完饭再出发。|We will set off after we finish eating.
我不想再犯同样的错误。|I do not want to make the same mistake again.

# Recurrence with 又 | 又 + verb/adjective | core
Use 又 when an action or situation occurs again, often for something already realized. 又 is also possible for predictable recurring events, so the distinction from 再 is not simply past versus future tense.
他又迟到了。|He is late again.
今天又下雨了。|It is raining again today.
我又忘了带钥匙。|I forgot to bring my keys again.
她又换工作了。|She has changed jobs again.
电脑又坏了。|The computer has broken down again.
我们又见面了。|We have met again.
他又问了同一个问题。|He asked the same question again.
这家店又涨价了。|This shop has raised its prices again.
弟弟又感冒了。|My younger brother has caught a cold again.
我昨天又看了一遍那部电影。|I watched that movie all the way through again yesterday.

# Earlier than expected with 就 | Time/amount + 就 + verb | core
Use 就 before a verb to present an event as early, quick, or requiring little effort. The time or small amount usually comes before 就. The judgment depends on the speaker's expectations.
他早上六点就起床了。|He got up as early as six in the morning.
我十分钟就做完了。|I finished it in just ten minutes.
她十八岁就开始工作了。|She started working as early as eighteen.
我们走五分钟就到了。|We arrived after walking for just five minutes.
电影七点就开始了。|The movie started as early as seven.
他看一遍就懂了。|He understood after reading it just once.
我昨天就告诉你了。|I told you as early as yesterday.
她一岁就会走路了。|She could walk as early as one year old.
这家店下午五点就关门了。|This shop closed as early as five in the afternoon.
我们花了二十块钱就吃饱了。|We ate our fill for just twenty yuan.

# Later than expected with 才 | Time/condition + 才 + verb | core
Use 才 before a verb to say an event occurs only at a late time or only after a requirement is met. It emphasizes delay or a necessary threshold, in contrast with the early or easy sense of 就.
他晚上十一点才回家。|He did not get home until eleven at night.
我今天才知道这件事。|I only learned about this matter today.
她三十岁才开始学开车。|She did not start learning to drive until she was thirty.
我们等了一个小时，车才来。|We waited an hour before the bus finally came.
这家店十点才开门。|This shop does not open until ten.
我看了三遍才看懂。|I only understood it after reading it three times.
他下周才有空。|He will not be free until next week.
她吃完药才感觉好一点。|She felt a little better only after taking the medicine.
我月底才能拿到工资。|I will not be able to get my pay until the end of the month.
我们走了两个小时才到山顶。|We reached the mountaintop only after walking for two hours.
