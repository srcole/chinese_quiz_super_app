# Chinese grammar exercise bank

155 rules · 1,550 exercises · simplified Chinese · beginner through advanced

Original draft; editorial levels are not official HSK assignments. Pinyin uses dictionary tones, with one space per syllable. Each numbered item is an English-to-Chinese exercise. The JSON also links three other examples of the same rule for feedback.

## grammar-001: Present descriptions

**review · Subject + 很 + adjective**

Use 很 before a simple adjectival predicate when describing a quality without making a comparison. In this pattern 很 often links the subject and adjective rather than strongly emphasizing 'very'. Do not add 是 before an ordinary adjective.

1. The weather is nice today.
   - 今天天气很好。
   - jīn tiān tiān qì hěn hǎo.
2. This restaurant is quiet.
   - 这家饭店很安静。
   - zhè jiā fàn diàn hěn ān jìng.
3. Your room is clean.
   - 你的房间很干净。
   - nǐ de fáng jiān hěn gān jìng.
4. This question is easy.
   - 这道题很简单。
   - zhè dào tí hěn jiǎn dān.
5. That road is wide.
   - 那条路很宽。
   - nà tiáo lù hěn kuān.
6. This shirt is comfortable.
   - 这件衬衫很舒服。
   - zhè jiàn chèn shān hěn shū fú.
7. His answer is clear.
   - 他的回答很清楚。
   - tā de huí dá hěn qīng chǔ.
8. This city is beautiful.
   - 这个城市很漂亮。
   - zhè ge chéng shì hěn piào liàng.
9. My neighbor is friendly.
   - 我的邻居很友好。
   - wǒ de lín jū hěn yǒu hǎo.
10. This job is important.
   - 这份工作很重要。
   - zhè fèn gōng zuò hěn zhòng yào.

## grammar-002: Possession with 的

**review · Possessor + 的 + noun**

Place the possessor before 的 and the thing possessed after it. 的 may be omitted in some close relationships, but keeping it is a useful default. An English possessive pronoun becomes a Chinese pronoun plus 的.

1. This is my passport.
   - 这是我的护照。
   - zhè shì wǒ de hù zhào.
2. That is the teacher's computer.
   - 那是老师的电脑。
   - nà shì lǎo shī de diàn nǎo.
3. Your phone is on the table.
   - 你的手机在桌子上。
   - nǐ de shǒu jī zài zhuō zi shàng.
4. Her bicycle is new.
   - 她的自行车很新。
   - tā de zì xíng chē hěn xīn.
5. My friend's company is large.
   - 我朋友的公司很大。
   - wǒ péng yǒu de gōng sī hěn dà.
6. This is a map of our school.
   - 这是我们学校的地图。
   - zhè shì wǒ men xué xiào de dì tú.
7. His office is on the third floor.
   - 他的办公室在三楼。
   - tā de bàn gōng shì zài sān lóu.
8. My older sister's birthday is tomorrow.
   - 姐姐的生日是明天。
   - jiě jiě de shēng rì shì míng tiān.
9. This is not my luggage.
   - 这不是我的行李。
   - zhè bù shì wǒ de xíng lǐ.
10. Their child works in Beijing.
   - 他们的孩子在北京工作。
   - tā men de hái zi zài běi jīng gōng zuò.

## grammar-003: Describing nouns with 的

**review · Description + 的 + noun**

A phrase describing a noun normally comes before that noun and is linked with 的. This includes full clauses; Chinese does not place an equivalent of 'who' or 'that' after the noun.

1. I like the book that you bought.
   - 我喜欢你买的那本书。
   - wǒ xǐ huān nǐ mǎi de nà běn shū.
2. The person wearing red clothes is my older sister.
   - 穿红衣服的人是我姐姐。
   - chuān hóng yī fú de rén shì wǒ jiě jiě.
3. This is the photo that I took yesterday.
   - 这是我昨天拍的照片。
   - zhè shì wǒ zuó tiān pāi de zhào piān.
4. The food that he cooks is delicious.
   - 他做的饭很好吃。
   - tā zuò de fàn hěn hǎo chī.
5. I want a cup of coffee without sugar.
   - 我要一杯不加糖的咖啡。
   - wǒ yào yī bēi bù jiā táng de kā fēi.
6. The girl who lives upstairs is a student.
   - 住在楼上的女孩是学生。
   - zhù zài lóu shàng de nǚ hái shì xué shēng.
7. We need a tour guide who can speak Chinese.
   - 我们需要一个会说中文的导游。
   - wǒ men xū yào yī gè huì shuō zhōng wén de dǎo yóu.
8. The movie that you recommended is interesting.
   - 你推荐的电影很有意思。
   - nǐ tuī jiàn de diàn yǐng hěn yǒu yì si.
9. This is the train bound for Shanghai.
   - 这是开往上海的火车。
   - zhè shì kāi wǎng shàng hǎi de huǒ chē.
10. I cannot find the letter that I received yesterday.
   - 我找不到昨天收到的那封信。
   - wǒ zhǎo bù dào zuó tiān shōu dào de nà fēng xìn.

## grammar-004: Location with 在

**review · Subject + 在 + location**

Use 在 as the predicate to say where someone or something is. For actions happening at a location, place 在 plus the location before the action verb instead.

1. My keys are in the bag.
   - 我的钥匙在包里。
   - wǒ de yào shi zài bāo lǐ.
2. The teacher is in the office.
   - 老师在办公室。
   - lǎo shī zài bàn gōng shì.
3. The restroom is on the second floor.
   - 洗手间在二楼。
   - xǐ shǒu jiān zài èr lóu.
4. The children are in the park.
   - 孩子们在公园里。
   - hái zi men zài gōng yuán lǐ.
5. Your glasses are on the bed.
   - 你的眼镜在床上。
   - nǐ de yǎn jìng zài chuáng shàng.
6. The bank is next to the supermarket.
   - 银行在超市旁边。
   - yín háng zài chāo shì páng biān.
7. I am at the subway station now.
   - 我现在在地铁站。
   - wǒ xiàn zài zài dì tiě zhàn.
8. The manager is not at the company today.
   - 经理今天不在公司。
   - jīng lǐ jīn tiān bù zài gōng sī.
9. That bookstore is across from the school.
   - 那家书店在学校对面。
   - nà jiā shū diàn zài xué xiào duì miàn.
10. The station is behind the hospital.
   - 车站在医院后面。
   - chē zhàn zài yī yuàn hòu miàn.

## grammar-005: Actions at a location

**review · Subject + 在 + location + verb**

Place the location phrase 在… before the action verb. This tells where the action happens, not where the subject goes as a result of the action.

1. I read books at the library.
   - 我在图书馆看书。
   - wǒ zài tú shū guǎn kàn shū.
2. She cooks in the kitchen.
   - 她在厨房做饭。
   - tā zài chú fáng zuò fàn.
3. They run in the park.
   - 他们在公园跑步。
   - tā men zài gōng yuán pǎo bù.
4. We buy tickets online.
   - 我们在网上买票。
   - wǒ men zài wǎng shàng mǎi piào.
5. He studies Chinese in Beijing.
   - 他在北京学习中文。
   - tā zài běi jīng xué xí zhōng wén.
6. The children play in the yard.
   - 孩子们在院子里玩。
   - hái zi men zài yuàn zi lǐ wán.
7. I eat lunch in the office.
   - 我在办公室吃午饭。
   - wǒ zài bàn gōng shì chī wǔ fàn.
8. You can wait for me here.
   - 你可以在这里等我。
   - nǐ kě yǐ zài zhè lǐ děng wǒ.
9. Dad drinks tea on the balcony.
   - 爸爸在阳台上喝茶。
   - bà bà zài yáng tái shàng hē chá.
10. She works at the airport.
   - 她在机场工作。
   - tā zài jī chǎng gōng zuò.

## grammar-006: Existence with 有

**review · Location + 有 + noun**

Start with a location and use 有 to introduce something present there. Negate 有 with 没有, not 不有. This structure introduces a thing rather than locating an already identified thing.

1. There is a book on the table.
   - 桌子上有一本书。
   - zhuō zi shàng yǒu yī běn shū.
2. There is a pharmacy nearby.
   - 附近有一家药店。
   - fù jìn yǒu yī jiā yào diàn.
3. There are three eggs in the fridge.
   - 冰箱里有三个鸡蛋。
   - bīng xiāng lǐ yǒu sān gè jī dàn.
4. There is a cafe downstairs.
   - 楼下有一家咖啡馆。
   - lóu xià yǒu yī jiā kā fēi guǎn.
5. There is nobody in the classroom.
   - 教室里没有人。
   - jiào shì lǐ méi yǒu rén.
6. There is a lake in the park.
   - 公园里有一个湖。
   - gōng yuán lǐ yǒu yī gè hú.
7. There are two beds in the room.
   - 房间里有两张床。
   - fáng jiān lǐ yǒu liǎng zhāng chuáng.
8. There is a cat at the entrance.
   - 门口有一只猫。
   - mén kǒu yǒu yī zhī māo.
9. There is no bank on this street.
   - 这条街上没有银行。
   - zhè tiáo jiē shàng méi yǒu yín háng.
10. There is an umbrella in my bag.
   - 我的包里有一把伞。
   - wǒ de bāo lǐ yǒu yī bǎ sǎn.

## grammar-007: Time before the verb

**review · Subject + time + verb**

Put a time expression before the verb, either before or after the subject. Chinese verbs do not change form for tense; the time expression supplies the time reference.

1. I am going to Shanghai tomorrow.
   - 我明天去上海。
   - wǒ míng tiān qù shàng hǎi.
   - Also accepted: 明天我去上海。
2. She takes the subway to work every day.
   - 她每天坐地铁上班。
   - tā měi tiān zuò dì tiě shàng bān.
   - Also accepted: 每天她坐地铁上班。
3. We are moving next week.
   - 我们下周搬家。
   - wǒ men xià zhōu bān jiā.
   - Also accepted: 下周我们搬家。
4. He comes home at eight in the evening.
   - 他晚上八点回家。
   - tā wǎn shàng bā diǎn huí jiā.
5. I am free on Saturday.
   - 我星期六有空。
   - wǒ xīng qī liù yǒu kòng.
   - Also accepted: 星期六我有空。
6. The meeting starts at two in the afternoon.
   - 会议下午两点开始。
   - huì yì xià wǔ liǎng diǎn kāi shǐ.
7. They are traveling to China this year.
   - 他们今年去中国旅行。
   - tā men jīn nián qù zhōng guó lǚ xíng.
8. I get up at seven in the morning.
   - 我早上七点起床。
   - wǒ zǎo shàng qī diǎn qǐ chuáng.
9. The shop opens at nine in the morning.
   - 商店上午九点开门。
   - shāng diàn shàng wǔ jiǔ diǎn kāi mén.
10. We cook together on weekends.
   - 我们周末一起做饭。
   - wǒ men zhōu mò yī qǐ zuò fàn.

## grammar-008: Negation with 不

**review · Subject + 不 + verb/adjective**

Use 不 for habits, unwillingness, future refusals, and many descriptions. To deny that an event occurred, usually use 没 instead. 不 precedes the verb or adjective it negates.

1. I do not drink coffee.
   - 我不喝咖啡。
   - wǒ bù hē kā fēi.
2. He does not like rainy days.
   - 他不喜欢下雨天。
   - tā bù xǐ huān xià yǔ tiān.
3. This piece of clothing is not expensive.
   - 这件衣服不贵。
   - zhè jiàn yī fú bù guì.
4. She is not coming tomorrow.
   - 她明天不来。
   - tā míng tiān bù lái.
5. I do not know that person.
   - 我不认识那个人。
   - wǒ bù rèn shí nà ge rén.
6. This road is not safe.
   - 这条路不安全。
   - zhè tiáo lù bù ān quán.
7. We do not work on weekends.
   - 我们周末不上班。
   - wǒ men zhōu mò bù shàng bān.
8. My younger brother does not eat fish.
   - 弟弟不吃鱼。
   - dì di bù chī yú.
9. I do not agree with your view.
   - 我不同意你的看法。
   - wǒ bù tóng yì nǐ de kàn fǎ.
10. It is not cold today.
   - 今天不冷。
   - jīn tiān bù lěng.

## grammar-009: Negation with 没

**review · Subject + 没(有) + verb**

Use 没 or 没有 to say that an event did not happen or has not happened. Normally remove completed-action 了 when negating that event. 没有 also negates possession and existence.

1. I did not go to the company yesterday.
   - 我昨天没去公司。
   - wǒ zuó tiān méi qù gōng sī.
   - Also accepted: 我昨天没有去公司。
2. She did not eat breakfast.
   - 她没吃早饭。
   - tā méi chī zǎo fàn.
   - Also accepted: 她没有吃早饭。
3. We did not buy that car.
   - 我们没买那辆车。
   - wǒ men méi mǎi nà liàng chē.
   - Also accepted: 我们没有买那辆车。
4. He did not tell me the address.
   - 他没告诉我地址。
   - tā méi gào sù wǒ dì zhǐ.
   - Also accepted: 他没有告诉我地址。
5. I did not receive your email.
   - 我没收到你的邮件。
   - wǒ méi shōu dào nǐ de yóu jiàn.
   - Also accepted: 我没有收到你的邮件。
6. It did not rain yesterday.
   - 昨天没下雨。
   - zuó tiān méi xià yǔ.
   - Also accepted: 昨天没有下雨。
7. They did not attend the meeting.
   - 他们没参加会议。
   - tā men méi cān jiā huì yì.
   - Also accepted: 他们没有参加会议。
8. I did not bring cash.
   - 我没带现金。
   - wǒ méi dài xiàn jīn.
   - Also accepted: 我没有带现金。
9. She did not hear the phone ring.
   - 她没听见电话响。
   - tā méi tīng jiàn diàn huà xiǎng.
   - Also accepted: 她没有听见电话响。
10. My younger brother did not do his homework.
   - 弟弟没做作业。
   - dì di méi zuò zuò yè.
   - Also accepted: 弟弟没有做作业。

## grammar-010: Yes-no questions with 吗

**review · Statement + 吗？**

Add 吗 to a statement to ask whether it is true. Keep ordinary statement word order. Do not combine this 吗 pattern with an A-not-A question or a question word such as 什么.

1. Do you like this book?
   - 你喜欢这本书吗？
   - nǐ xǐ huān zhè běn shū ma?
2. Is he your colleague?
   - 他是你的同事吗？
   - tā shì nǐ de tóng shì ma?
3. Do you have time tomorrow?
   - 你明天有时间吗？
   - nǐ míng tiān yǒu shí jiān ma?
4. Can we park here?
   - 这儿可以停车吗？
   - zhè er kě yǐ tíng chē ma?
   - Also accepted: 这里可以停车吗？
5. Have you ever eaten Beijing roast duck?
   - 你吃过北京烤鸭吗？
   - nǐ chī guò běi jīng kǎo yā ma?
6. Has she arrived home already?
   - 她已经到家了吗？
   - tā yǐ jīng dào jiā le ma?
7. Is this seat taken?
   - 这个座位有人吗？
   - zhè ge zuò wèi yǒu rén ma?
8. Do you need help?
   - 你需要帮助吗？
   - nǐ xū yào bāng zhù ma?
9. Is it still raining outside?
   - 外面还在下雨吗？
   - wài miàn hái zài xià yǔ ma?
10. Can you use chopsticks?
   - 你会用筷子吗？
   - nǐ huì yòng kuài zi ma?

## grammar-011: A-not-A questions

**review · Verb + 不/没 + verb**

Ask a yes-no question by repeating a verb or adjective with 不 between the two forms. Use 有没有 for 有. This pattern normally replaces sentence-final 吗.

1. Are you going or not?
   - 你去不去？
   - nǐ qù bù qù?
2. Is this piece of clothing expensive or not?
   - 这件衣服贵不贵？
   - zhè jiàn yī fú guì bù guì?
3. Do you have a passport or not?
   - 你有没有护照？
   - nǐ yǒu méi yǒu hù zhào?
4. Is he a doctor or not?
   - 他是不是医生？
   - tā shì bù shì yī shēng?
5. Can you swim or not?
   - 你会不会游泳？
   - nǐ huì bù huì yóu yǒng?
6. Should we take a taxi or not?
   - 我们要不要打车？
   - wǒ men yào bù yào dǎ chē?
7. Is this question difficult or not?
   - 这个问题难不难？
   - zhè ge wèn tí nán bù nán?
8. Do you like it here or not?
   - 你喜不喜欢这里？
   - nǐ xǐ bù xǐ huān zhè lǐ?
   - Also accepted: 你喜欢不喜欢这里？
9. Can she attend or not?
   - 她能不能参加？
   - tā néng bù néng cān jiā?
10. Are you tired or not?
   - 你累不累？
   - nǐ lèi bù lèi?

## grammar-012: Question words in place

**review · Subject + verb + 什么/谁/哪里**

Leave a question word in the position where its answer would go. Chinese generally does not move 'what', 'who', or 'where' to the beginning just to form a question.

1. What would you like to eat?
   - 你想吃什么？
   - nǐ xiǎng chī shén me?
2. Who called you?
   - 谁给你打的电话？
   - shuí gěi nǐ dǎ dī diàn huà?
3. Where do you live?
   - 你住在哪里？
   - nǐ zhù zài nǎ lǐ?
   - Also accepted: 你住在哪儿？
4. When does the meeting start?
   - 会议什么时候开始？
   - huì yì shén me shí hòu kāi shǐ?
5. Why do you study Chinese?
   - 你为什么学中文？
   - nǐ wèi shén me xué zhōng wén?
6. How much does this piece of clothing cost?
   - 这件衣服多少钱？
   - zhè jiàn yī fú duō shǎo qián?
7. How are you getting to the airport?
   - 你怎么去机场？
   - nǐ zěn me qù jī chǎng?
8. Which book is yours?
   - 哪本书是你的？
   - nǎ běn shū shì nǐ de?
9. Who are you going with?
   - 你和谁一起去？
   - nǐ hé shuí yī qǐ qù?
10. How many hours do you sleep every day?
   - 你每天睡几个小时？
   - nǐ měi tiān shuì jǐ gè xiǎo shí?

## grammar-013: Choice questions with 还是

**review · Option A + 还是 + option B？**

Use 还是 to present alternatives in a question. Use 或者 for alternatives in ordinary statements. A direct choice question normally does not end in 吗.

1. Would you like tea or coffee?
   - 你喝茶还是喝咖啡？
   - nǐ hē chá hái shì hē kā fēi?
2. Shall we take the bus or the subway?
   - 我们坐公交车还是坐地铁？
   - wǒ men zuò gōng jiāo chē hái shì zuò dì tiě?
3. Are you going today or tomorrow?
   - 你今天去还是明天去？
   - nǐ jīn tiān qù hái shì míng tiān qù?
4. Do you prefer red or blue?
   - 你喜欢红色还是蓝色？
   - nǐ xǐ huān hóng sè hái shì lán sè?
5. Is he a teacher or a doctor?
   - 他是老师还是医生？
   - tā shì lǎo shī hái shì yī shēng?
6. Do you want to eat at home or go out to eat?
   - 你想在家吃还是出去吃？
   - nǐ xiǎng zài jiā chī hái shì chū qù chī?
7. Do you want the big one or the small one?
   - 你要大的还是小的？
   - nǐ yào dà de hái shì xiǎo de?
8. Shall we eat first or buy tickets first?
   - 我们先吃饭还是先买票？
   - wǒ men xiān chī fàn hái shì xiān mǎi piào?
9. Are you using cash or a credit card?
   - 你用现金还是信用卡？
   - nǐ yòng xiàn jīn hái shì xìn yòng kǎ?
10. Does she live in Beijing or Shanghai?
   - 她住在北京还是上海？
   - tā zhù zài běi jīng hái shì shàng hǎi?

## grammar-014: Numbers and measure words

**review · Number + measure word + noun**

Place an appropriate measure word between a number and a countable noun. Use 两 rather than 二 before most ordinary measure words when counting two objects.

1. I bought three books.
   - 我买了三本书。
   - wǒ mǎi le sān běn shū.
2. There are two cups of tea on the table.
   - 桌子上有两杯茶。
   - zhuō zi shàng yǒu liǎng bēi chá.
3. She has one cat.
   - 她有一只猫。
   - tā yǒu yī zhī māo.
4. We need four chairs.
   - 我们需要四把椅子。
   - wǒ men xū yào sì bǎ yǐ zi.
5. He wrote two letters.
   - 他写了两封信。
   - tā xiě le liǎng fēng xìn.
6. Please give me a sheet of paper.
   - 请给我一张纸。
   - qǐng gěi wǒ yī zhāng zhǐ.
7. I brought two coats.
   - 我带了两件外套。
   - wǒ dài le liǎng jiàn wài tào.

10. She ordered three bowls of noodles.

   - tā diǎn le sān wǎn miàn tiáo.

## grammar-015: Approximate numbers

**review · Adjacent numbers / number + 几 or 多 + measure word**

Put adjacent numbers before a measure word for an approximate amount, such as 两三天 ('two or three days'). 十几 means ten-odd (eleven to nineteen), and a round number plus 多 means more than that number. For ages, 三十多岁 means in one's thirties. Do not insert 或者 inside an adjacent-number expression.

1. I drink two or three cups of tea every day.
   - 我每天喝两三杯茶。
   - wǒ měi tiān hē liǎng sān bēi chá.
2. We need three or four chairs.
   - 我们需要三四把椅子。
   - wǒ men xū yào sān sì bǎ yǐ zi.
3. He lived here for five or six years.
   - 他在这里住了五六年。
   - tā zài zhè lǐ zhù le wǔ liù nián.
4. Please wait two or three minutes.
   - 请等两三分钟。
   - qǐng děng liǎng sān fēn zhōng.
5. There are seven or eight people in the room.
   - 房间里有七八个人。
   - fáng jiān lǐ yǒu qī bā gè rén.
6. I want to rest for three or four days.
   - 我想休息三四天。
   - wǒ xiǎng xiū xī sān sì tiān.
7. She bought four or five books.
   - 她买了四五本书。
   - tā mǎi le sì wǔ běn shū.
8. It takes ten-odd minutes to walk to the station.
   - 走到车站要十几分钟。
   - zǒu dào chē zhàn yào shí jǐ fēn zhōng.
9. He is already in his thirties.
   - 他已经三十多岁了。
   - tā yǐ jīng sān shí duō suì le.
10. There are more than twenty students here.
   - 这里有二十多个学生。
   - zhè lǐ yǒu èr shí duō gè xué shēng.

## grammar-016: Completed actions with 了

**core · Verb + 了 + object**

Put 了 after a verb to present an action as completed or bounded. It does not simply mean past tense. Time words establish when the event happened; negating the event normally uses 没 without this 了.

1. I bought a computer yesterday.
   - 我昨天买了一台电脑。
   - wǒ zuó tiān mǎi le yī tái diàn nǎo.
2. She drank a glass of water.
   - 她喝了一杯水。
   - tā hē le yī bēi shuǐ.
3. We watched a movie.
   - 我们看了一场电影。
   - wǒ men kàn le yī cháng diàn yǐng.
4. He wrote an email.
   - 他写了一封邮件。
   - tā xiě le yī fēng yóu jiàn.
5. I washed three pieces of clothing.
   - 我洗了三件衣服。
   - wǒ xǐ le sān jiàn yī fú.
6. Mom made a bowl of noodles.
   - 妈妈做了一碗面条。
   - mā mā zuò le yī wǎn miàn tiáo.
7. They visited that museum.
   - 他们参观了那个博物馆。
   - tā men cān guān le nà ge bó wù guǎn.
8. I made a phone call to the teacher.
   - 我给老师打了一个电话。
   - wǒ gěi lǎo shī dǎ le yī gè diàn huà.
9. She answered two questions.
   - 她回答了两个问题。
   - tā huí dá le liǎng gè wèn tí.
10. We booked a room.
   - 我们订了一个房间。
   - wǒ men dìng le yī gè fáng jiān.

## grammar-017: Changed situations with 了

**core · New situation + 了**

Sentence-final 了 can mark a new or changed situation, including a current state. With 不…了 it often means 'no longer' or a change of intention, not a completed past action.

1. The weather has become warm.
   - 天气暖和了。

4. The child can walk now.
   - 孩子会走路了。
   - hái zi huì zǒu lù le.
5. We have our own home now.

   - 商店关门了。
   - shāng diàn guān mén le.
7. The rain has stopped.
   - 雨停了。
   - yǔ tíng le.
8. I understand what you mean now.
   - 我懂你的意思了。
   - wǒ dǒng nǐ de yì si le.
9. She is no longer angry.
   - 她不生气了。
   - tā bù shēng qì le.
10. This road has become wider than before.
   - 这条路比以前宽了。
   - zhè tiáo lù bǐ yǐ qián kuān le.

## grammar-018: Experience with 过

**core · Verb + 过 + object**

Use 过 after a verb to report a past experience without focusing on a particular occurrence. Negate it with 没有…过. Do not use it merely as a substitute for completed-action 了.

1. I have been to Hangzhou.
   - 我去过杭州。
   - wǒ qù guò háng zhōu.
2. She has eaten this kind of fruit.
   - 她吃过这种水果。
   - tā chī guò zhè zhǒng shuǐ guǒ.
3. We have seen this movie.
   - 我们看过这部电影。
   - wǒ men kàn guò zhè bù diàn yǐng.
4. He has traveled by plane.
   - 他坐过飞机。
   - tā zuò guò fēi jī.
5. I have never ridden a horse.
   - 我没骑过马。
   - wǒ méi qí guò mǎ.
   - Also accepted: 我没有骑过马。
6. Have you met my older brother?
   - 你见过我的哥哥吗？
   - nǐ jiàn guò wǒ de gē ge ma?
7. She has worked in a hospital.
   - 她在医院工作过。
   - tā zài yī yuàn gōng zuò guò.
8. I have used this software.
   - 我用过这个软件。
   - wǒ yòng guò zhè ge ruǎn jiàn.
9. They have taken part in a competition.
   - 他们参加过比赛。
   - tā men cān jiā guò bǐ sài.
10. I have never heard this story.
   - 我没听过这个故事。
   - wǒ méi tīng guò zhè ge gù shì.
   - Also accepted: 我没有听过这个故事。

## grammar-019: Actions in progress with 正在

**core · Subject + 正在 + verb**

1. I am doing my homework right now.

6. The doctor is examining the patient right now.
   - 医生正在检查病人。
   - yī shēng zhèng zài jiǎn chá bìng rén.
7. The child is sleeping right now.
   - 孩子正在睡觉。
   - hái zi zhèng zài shuì jiào.
8. I am looking for my keys right now.

## grammar-020: Continuing states with 着

**core · Verb + 着**

Attach 着 to a verb to describe a continuing state, such as an open door or something being worn. It focuses on the maintained state rather than the change that created it.

   - 他给我打了两次电话。

7. This computer crashed twice today.
   - 这台电脑今天死机了两次。
   - zhè tái diàn nǎo jīn tiān sǐ jī le liǎng cì.
8. I have a medical checkup once a year.
   - 我每年体检一次。
   - wǒ měi nián tǐ jiǎn yī cì.
9. She asked me three times.
   - 她问了我三次。

10. We have met a few times.

   - wǒ men jiàn guò jǐ cì miàn.

## grammar-026: Complete repetitions with 遍

**core · Verb + number + 遍**

Use 遍 to count complete runs through an action from beginning to end. It is especially useful for reading, listening, writing, and checking an entire piece of content.

1. Please read the text all the way through once.

   - 请再检查一遍。
   - qǐng zài jiǎn chá yī biàn.

   - 我听了五遍这段录音。
   - wǒ tīng le wǔ biàn zhè duàn lù yīn.
6. The teacher explained the whole question once.
   - 老师把问题解释了一遍。
   - lǎo shī bǎ wèn tí jiě shì le yī biàn.
7. He read through the list once.
   - 他把名单看了一遍。
   - tā bǎ míng dān kàn le yī biàn.

   - wǒ bǎ bào gào cóng tóu dào wěi dú le sān biàn.

## grammar-027: Again in the future with 再

**core · 再 + verb**

Use 再 for a further occurrence that is requested, planned, or otherwise not yet realized. It can mean 'again' or 'then', depending on context. Contrast 又 for an occurrence presented as happening again.

1. Please say it all the way through again.

   - 请再给我一次机会。
   - qǐng zài gěi wǒ yī cì jī huì.
5. Think about this matter some more.
   - 这件事你再想想。
   - zhè jiàn shì nǐ zài xiǎng xiǎng.
6. I want to buy one more book.
   - 我想再买一本书。
   - wǒ xiǎng zài mǎi yī běn shū.
7. Rest a little, then leave.
   - 休息一下再走吧。
   - xiū xī yī xià zài zǒu ba.
8. Please wait another five minutes.
   - 请再等五分钟。
   - qǐng zài děng wǔ fēn zhōng.

   - 我们吃完饭再出发。

## grammar-028: Recurrence with 又

**core · 又 + verb/adjective**

Use 又 when an action or situation occurs again, often for something already realized. 又 is also possible for predictable recurring events, so the distinction from 再 is not simply past versus future tense.

1. He is late again.
   - 他又迟到了。
   - tā yòu chí dào le.
2. It is raining again today.
   - 今天又下雨了。
   - jīn tiān yòu xià yǔ le.
3. I forgot to bring my keys again.
   - 我又忘了带钥匙。
   - wǒ yòu wàng le dài yào shi.
4. She has changed jobs again.
   - 她又换工作了。
   - tā yòu huàn gōng zuò le.
5. The computer has broken down again.
   - 电脑又坏了。

6. We have met again.

## grammar-029: Earlier than expected with 就

**core · Time/amount + 就 + verb**

Use 就 before a verb to present an event as early, quick, or requiring little effort. The time or small amount usually comes before 就. The judgment depends on the speaker's expectations.

1. He got up as early as six in the morning.
   - 他早上六点就起床了。
   - tā zǎo shàng liù diǎn jiù qǐ chuáng le.

   - 我十分钟就做完了。

   - 她十八岁就开始工作了。

   - 我们走五分钟就到了。

   - 电影七点就开始了。
   - diàn yǐng qī diǎn jiù kāi shǐ le.
6. He understood after reading it just once.
   - 他看一遍就懂了。
   - tā kàn yī biàn jiù dǒng le.
7. I told you as early as yesterday.
   - 我昨天就告诉你了。
   - wǒ zuó tiān jiù gào sù nǐ le.

   - wǒ men huā le èr shí kuài qián jiù chī bǎo le.

## grammar-030: Later than expected with 才

**core · Time/condition + 才 + verb**

Use 才 before a verb to say an event occurs only at a late time or only after a requirement is met. It emphasizes delay or a necessary threshold, in contrast with the early or easy sense of 就.

1. He did not get home until eleven at night.

   - 我们等了一个小时，车才来。

5. This shop does not open until ten.
   - 这家店十点才开门。
   - zhè jiā diàn shí diǎn cái kāi mén.
6. I only understood it after reading it three times.
   - 我看了三遍才看懂。
   - wǒ kàn le sān biàn cái kàn dǒng.
7. He will not be free until next week.
   - 他下周才有空。
   - tā xià zhōu cái yǒu kòng.
8. She felt a little better only after taking the medicine.
   - 她吃完药才感觉好一点。

   - wǒ men zǒu le liǎng gè xiǎo shí cái dào shān dǐng.

## grammar-031: Completion with 完

**core · Verb + 完**

Put 完 directly after an action verb to show that the action reaches completion. Negate completion with 没…完; inability to finish is expressed with …不完 instead.

1. I have finished my homework.

5. He has not finished washing the clothes yet.

   - 他们已经搬完东西了。

9. The movie has not finished playing yet.

10. I want to finish reading this page first.

## grammar-032: Readiness with 好

Use 好 after a verb to express that something has been properly done and is ready. This is a result complement, distinct from placing 好 before a verb to describe something as pleasant.

8. The car has been repaired and is ready.
   - 车修好了。
   - chē xiū hǎo le.
9. Please put the things in their proper places.
   - 请把东西放好。
   - qǐng bǎ dōng xī fàng hǎo.
10. Have you finished getting everything packed up?
   - 你收拾好了吗？
   - nǐ shōu shí hǎo le ma?

## grammar-033: Successful attainment with 到

**core · Verb + 到**

Attach 到 to an action verb to indicate that it successfully reaches its target, as in finding, buying successfully, or receiving. It is different from 到 used independently to mean 'arrive'.

1. I found my keys.

5. He finally got to meet that writer.

6. We waited but he did not turn up.

7. I did not manage to buy fresh fish.
   - 我没买到新鲜的鱼。

8. She managed to borrow that book.
   - 她借到了那本书。

9. Did you guess the answer?

10. I caught the smell of bread.

## grammar-034: Understanding with 懂

Put 懂 after a perception or learning verb to show that understanding results. 听懂 concerns understanding what is heard; 看懂 concerns understanding what is read or seen.

1. I understood what you meant when you spoke.

7. The child understood the game rules after reading them.

## grammar-035: Incorrect results with 错

## grammar-036: Potential complements with 得/不

**core · Verb + 得/不 + result**

Insert 得 or 不 between a verb and its result complement to express whether achieving the result is possible. This differs from 没 plus a result compound, which says the result did not occur.

1. I can understand this recording.
   - 我听得懂这段录音。
   - wǒ tīng de dǒng zhè duàn lù yīn.
2. I cannot understand this contract.
   - 我看不懂这份合同。
   - wǒ kàn bù dǒng zhè fèn hé tóng.
3. Can this work be finished today?
   - 今天做得完这些工作吗？

4. I cannot finish this much food by myself.
   - 我一个人吃不完这么多菜。
   - wǒ yī gè rén chī bù wán zhè me duō cài.
5. Can you manage to buy this kind of tea?
   - 你买得到这种茶吗？
   - nǐ mǎi de dào zhè zhǒng chá ma?
6. I cannot find my glasses.
   - 我找不到我的眼镜。
   - wǒ zhǎo bù dào wǒ de yǎn jìng.
7. It is too noisy here; I cannot hear clearly.
   - 这里太吵，我听不清楚。
   - zhè lǐ tài chǎo, wǒ tīng bù qīng chǔ.
8. This photo can be seen clearly.
   - 这张照片看得清楚。
   - zhè zhāng zhào piān kàn de qīng chǔ.
9. The suitcase is too heavy; I cannot lift it.
   - 箱子太重，我拿不动。
   - xiāng zi tài zhòng, wǒ ná bù dòng.
10. The door is too small; the sofa cannot be moved inside.
   - 门太小，沙发搬不进去。
   - mén tài xiǎo, shā fā bān bù jìn qù.

## grammar-037: Degree and manner with 得

**core · Verb + 得 + description**

Use 得 after the verb to describe how an action is performed. If the verb has an object, move that object into the topic or repeat the verb before 得.

1. She speaks very clearly.
   - 她说得很清楚。
   - tā shuō de hěn qīng chǔ.

   - 他跑得很快。

   - 你唱得很好。

4. I did not sleep well last night.
   - 我昨晚睡得不好。
   - wǒ zuó wǎn shuì de bù hǎo.
5. The children had a lot of fun playing.
   - 孩子们玩得很开心。
   - hái zi men wán de hěn kāi xīn.
6. She speaks Chinese fluently.
   - 她中文说得很流利。
   - tā zhōng wén shuō de hěn liú lì.
   - Also accepted: 她说中文说得很流利。
7. He writes beautifully.
   - 他字写得很漂亮。
   - tā zì xiě de hěn piào liàng.
   - Also accepted: 他写字写得很漂亮。
8. You came too late.
   - 你来得太晚了。
   - nǐ lái de tài wǎn le.
9. We had a pleasant chat.
   - 我们聊得很愉快。
   - wǒ men liáo de hěn yú kuài.
10. Mom cooks delicious food.
   - 妈妈菜做得很好吃。
   - mā mā cài zuò de hěn hǎo chī.
   - Also accepted: 妈妈做菜做得很好吃。

## grammar-038: Adverbial manner with 地

5. The music started playing.
   - 音乐响起来了。
   - yīn yuè xiǎng qǐ lái le.
6. She started singing.
   - 她唱起歌来了。
   - tā chàng qǐ gē lái le.
7. He stood up.
   - 他站起来了。
   - tā zhàn qǐ lái le.

10. The students started discussing.

## grammar-042: Impressions with 起来

**core · 看/听/吃/闻 + 起来 + adjective**

1. You look tired.
   - 你看起来很累。

2. This approach sounds good.

## grammar-043: Continuing onward with 下去

**core · Verb + 下去**

Use 下去 after a verb to mean continuing an activity from the present point onward. This is an extended meaning of the directional complement, rather than literal downward motion.

1. Please go on speaking.
   - 请说下去。
   - qǐng shuō xià qù.
2. I want to keep studying.
   - 我想继续学下去。
   - wǒ xiǎng jì xù xué xià qù.
3. We cannot keep waiting like this.
   - 我们不能这样等下去。
   - wǒ men bù néng zhè yàng děng xià qù.
4. This story is interesting; I want to keep reading.
   - 这个故事很有意思，我想看下去。
   - zhè ge gù shì hěn yǒu yì si, wǒ xiǎng kàn xià qù.
5. As long as I have time, I will keep practicing.
   - 只要有时间，我就会练下去。
   - zhǐ yào yǒu shí jiān, wǒ jiù huì liàn xià qù.
6. Do you plan to keep living here indefinitely?
   - 你打算一直住下去吗？
   - nǐ dǎ suàn yī zhí zhù xià qù ma?
7. Stop making noise and let him continue talking.
   - 别吵了，让他讲下去。
   - bié chǎo le, ràng tā jiǎng xià qù.
8. If we keep walking, we will reach the riverbank.
   - 再走下去就到河边了。
   - zài zǒu xià qù jiù dào hé biān le.
9. There is not enough money; this project cannot continue.
   - 钱不够了，这个项目做不下去了。
   - qián bù gòu le, zhè ge xiàng mù zuò bù xià qù le.
10. He decided to keep persevering.
   - 他决定坚持下去。
   - tā jué dìng jiān chí xià qù.

## grammar-044: Keeping a result with 下来

**core · Verb + 下来**

After verbs such as 写, 记, 停, and 安静, 下来 can signal recording, retention, or settling into a stable state. Its exact meaning depends on the verb; it is not interchangeable with onward-continuing 下去.

1. Please write down the address.

2. I noted down the phone number.

   - 大家都安静下来了。
   - dà jiā dōu ān jìng xià lái le.
5. Please keep this photo.
   - 请把这张照片留下来。

6. She recorded what the teacher said.
   - 她把老师的话录下来了。
   - tā bǎ lǎo shī de huà lù xià lái le.

## grammar-045: Affordability with 得起/不起

**core · Verb + 得起/不起**

Use 买得起 and 买不起 to express whether someone can afford to buy something. Similar forms with other verbs concern bearing the cost, and should not be generalized to every action verb.

1. I cannot afford this car.
   - 我买不起这辆车。

2. We can afford to stay at this hotel.

   - 我吃不起这么贵的饭。
   - wǒ chī bù qǐ zhè me guì de fàn.
6. She can afford a home now.
   - 她现在买得起房子了。
   - tā xiàn zài mǎi de qǐ fáng zi le.
7. We cannot afford to live in an apartment in the city center.
   - 我们住不起市中心的公寓。
   - wǒ men zhù bù qǐ shì zhōng xīn de gōng yù.
8. I can afford this pair of shoes.
   - 这双鞋我买得起。
   - zhè shuāng xié wǒ mǎi de qǐ.
9. He cannot even afford a bus ticket.
   - 他连公交车票都买不起。

10. This restaurant is not expensive; we can afford to eat here.

   - zhè jiā cān tīng bù guì, wǒ men chī de qǐ.

## grammar-046: Comparing with 比

**core · A + 比 + B + adjective**

Put 比 and the comparison standard before the adjective. Do not add 很 immediately before the adjective in a simple 比 comparison. The adjective itself does not change form.

1. Today is colder than yesterday.
   - 今天比昨天冷。
   - jīn tiān bǐ zuó tiān lěng.
2. My older brother is taller than me.
   - 我哥哥比我高。
   - wǒ gē gē bǐ wǒ gāo.
3. Taking the subway is faster than driving.
   - 坐地铁比开车快。
   - zuò dì tiě bǐ kāi chē kuài.
4. This book is more interesting than that one.
   - 这本书比那本书有意思。
   - zhè běn shū bǐ nà běn shū yǒu yì si.
5. This shop is cheaper than that one.
   - 这家店比那家店便宜。
   - zhè jiā diàn bǐ nà jiā diàn pián yi.
6. My room is bigger than your room.
   - 我的房间比你的房间大。
   - wǒ de fáng jiān bǐ nǐ de fáng jiān dà.
7. The air is better in the morning than in the evening.
   - 早上的空气比晚上的空气好。
   - zǎo shàng de kōng qì bǐ wǎn shàng de kōng qì hǎo.
8. This exam is harder than the last one.
   - 这次考试比上次难。
   - zhè cì kǎo shì bǐ shàng cì nán.
9. She knows this city better than I do.

10. Taking this road is more convenient than taking that one.

   - zǒu zhè tiáo lù bǐ zǒu nà tiáo lù fāng biàn.

## grammar-047: Specifying a difference

**core · A + 比 + B + adjective + amount**

Put a measured difference or a degree phrase after the adjective in a 比 comparison. 一点儿 means a little, while 得多 and 多了 indicate a large difference.

1. He is three years older than me.
   - 他比我大三岁。
   - tā bǐ wǒ dà sān suì.
2. This table is ten centimeters longer than that one.
   - 这张桌子比那张长十厘米。

   - 今天比昨天暖和一点儿。

4. This book costs twenty yuan more than that one.
   - 这本书比那本贵二十块钱。
   - zhè běn shū bǐ nà běn guì èr shí kuài qián.
5. My luggage is two kilograms heavier than yours.
   - 我的行李比你的重两公斤。
   - wǒ de xíng lǐ bǐ nǐ de zhòng liǎng gōng jīn.
6. Flying is much faster than taking the train.
   - 坐飞机比坐火车快得多。
   - zuò fēi jī bǐ zuò huǒ chē kuài de duō.
7. It is much quieter here than I expected.
   - 这里比我想的安静多了。
   - zhè lǐ bǐ wǒ xiǎng de ān jìng duō le.
8. She arrived five minutes earlier than me.
   - 她比我早到五分钟。
   - tā bǐ wǒ zǎo dào wǔ fēn zhōng.
9. This room is a little bigger than that one.
   - 这间房比那间大一点儿。
   - zhè jiān fáng bǐ nà jiān dà yī diǎn er.
10. There is much more rain this year than last year.
   - 今年的雨水比去年多得多。
   - jīn nián de yǔ shuǐ bǐ qù nián duō de duō.

## grammar-048: Not as…as with 没有

## grammar-049: Equal degree with 一样

**core · A + 跟/和 + B + 一样 + adjective**

Use 跟 or 和 to introduce the comparison standard, then 一样 and the shared quality. Omit the adjective when you simply mean 'the same'. Negate equality with 不一样.

Place 最 before an adjective or suitable mental-state verb to mark the highest degree within a context. A phrase such as 在我们班 establishes the comparison group.

## grammar-051: Increasing degree with 越来越

**core · 越来越 + adjective/mental verb**

Use 越来越 before a quality or certain mental-state verbs to express change over time: 'more and more'. Do not add 更 or 很 between 越来越 and the adjective.

## grammar-052: Linked changes with 越…越…

**core · 越 + condition/action，越 + result**

Use paired 越 phrases for 'the more…, the more…'. The second change varies with the first; unlike 越来越, this structure links two conditions rather than simply describing change over time.

1. The more you practice, the more fluently you speak.

   - 这件事越早解决越好。
   - zhè jiàn shì yuè zǎo jiě jué yuè hǎo.
7. The more we chat, the more we feel we get along.

   - wǒ men yuè liáo yuè jué de tóu jī.
8. The farther we walk, the narrower the road gets.

   - lù yuè zǒu yuè zhǎi.
9. The more you know, the more confident you are.

10. The more I listen to this song, the better it sounds.
   - 这首歌越听越好听。
   - zhè shǒu gē yuè tīng yuè hǎo tīng.

## grammar-053: Two qualities with 又…又…

**core · 又 + quality/action + 又 + quality/action**

Use 又…又… to combine two parallel qualities or actions of the same subject. The two parts usually fit the same context or evaluation; do not treat it as a general replacement for every English 'and'.

1. This apple is both big and sweet.

   - tā yòu huì chàng gē yòu huì tiào wǔ.

   - hái zi yòu kū yòu nào.
8. These shoes are both light and comfortable.
   - 这双鞋又轻又舒服。
   - zhè shuāng xié yòu qīng yòu shū fú.

## grammar-054: Somewhat negative degree with 有点儿

**core · 有点儿 + adjective**

Place 有点儿 before an adjective to express 'a little', often with an undesirable or unexpected quality. Contrast 一点儿 after an adjective in a request or comparison.

   - jīn tiān yǒu diǎn er lěng.

## grammar-055: Adjusting degree with 一点儿

**core · Adjective + 一点儿**

Place 一点儿 after an adjective to ask for a small adjustment or specify a small difference. In requests, the understood comparison is with the current degree: slower, louder, or cheaper.

1. Please speak a little more slowly.
   - 请说慢一点儿。
   - qǐng shuō màn yī diǎn er.
   - Also accepted: 请说慢一点。

   - bǎ fáng jiān shōu shí gān jìng yī diǎn er.

## grammar-056: Excessive or emphatic degree with 太…了

**review · 太 + adjective + 了**

1. This piece of clothing is too expensive.

10. You came back too late.

## grammar-058: Further degree with 更

## grammar-059: Not at all with 一点儿也不

**core · 一点儿 + 也/都 + 不/没 + predicate**

Use 一点儿也不 or 一点儿都不 for emphatic 'not at all'. With events or possession, use 没 or 没有 as appropriate. The tiny amount expression must be interpreted with the following negation.

1. I am not tired at all.

## grammar-060: Both and all with 都

Put 都 before the predicate to distribute it across a previously identified group: 'all' or 'both'. 都不 negates the predicate for everyone; 不都 means 'not all' and has a different scope.

1. We all like Chinese food.

## grammar-061: Reasons with 因为…所以…

**core · 因为 + cause，所以 + result**

Use 因为 to introduce a cause and 所以 to introduce its result. Chinese can use both in one sentence. Either can sometimes be omitted when the relationship is already clear.

1. Because it was raining, we did not go to the park.
   - 因为下雨，所以我们没去公园。
   - yīn wèi xià yǔ, suǒ yǐ wǒ men méi qù gōng yuán.
2. Because I am sick, I am taking leave today.
   - 因为我生病了，所以今天请假。
   - yīn wèi wǒ shēng bìng le, suǒ yǐ jīn tiān qǐng jià.
3. Because there was a traffic jam, he was late.
   - 因为路上堵车，所以他迟到了。
   - yīn wèi lù shàng dǔ chē, suǒ yǐ tā chí dào le.
4. Because this shop is cheap, there are many people here.
   - 因为这家店便宜，所以人很多。
   - yīn wèi zhè jiā diàn pián yi, suǒ yǐ rén hěn duō.
5. Because there is an exam tomorrow, I need to review.
   - 因为明天考试，所以我要复习。
   - yīn wèi míng tiān kǎo shì, suǒ yǐ wǒ yào fù xí.
6. Because she lives far away, she leaves home early every day.
   - 因为她住得远，所以每天很早出门。
   - yīn wèi tā zhù de yuǎn, suǒ yǐ měi tiān hěn zǎo chū mén.
7. Because I did not bring an umbrella, I got wet in the rain.
   - 因为没带伞，所以我淋湿了。
   - yīn wèi méi dài sǎn, suǒ yǐ wǒ lín shī le.
8. Because the room is too small, we want to move.
   - 因为房间太小，所以我们想搬家。
   - yīn wèi fáng jiān tài xiǎo, suǒ yǐ wǒ men xiǎng bān jiā.
9. Because he does not eat meat, I cooked vegetables.
   - 因为他不吃肉，所以我做了蔬菜。
   - yīn wèi tā bù chī ròu, suǒ yǐ wǒ zuò le shū cài.
10. Because there was not enough time, we took a taxi.
   - 因为时间不够，所以我们坐了出租车。
   - yīn wèi shí jiān bù gòu, suǒ yǐ wǒ men zuò le chū zū chē.

## grammar-062: Concession with 虽然…但是…

**core · 虽然 + fact，但是 + contrasting fact**

Use 虽然 for an acknowledged fact and 但是 for an unexpected or contrasting fact. Both connectors can appear together in Chinese. 可是 and 不过 are possible contrast connectors in suitable contexts.

1. Although it was raining, we still went out.
   - 虽然下雨，但是我们还是出门了。
   - suī rán xià yǔ, dàn shì wǒ men hái shì chū mén le.
2. Although this piece of clothing is expensive, its quality is very good.
   - 虽然这件衣服贵，但是质量很好。
   - suī rán zhè jiàn yī fú guì, dàn shì zhì liàng hěn hǎo.
3. Although he is busy, he exercises every day.
   - 虽然他很忙，但是每天都运动。
   - suī rán tā hěn máng, dàn shì měi tiān dōu yùn dòng.
4. Although I studied it before, I have already forgotten it.
   - 虽然我学过，但是已经忘了。
   - suī rán wǒ xué guò, dàn shì yǐ jīng wàng le.
5. Although the journey is long, the scenery is beautiful.
   - 虽然路很远，但是风景很美。
   - suī rán lù hěn yuǎn, dàn shì fēng jǐng hěn měi.
6. Although she is young, she has a lot of experience.
   - 虽然她年轻，但是经验很多。
   - suī rán tā nián qīng, dàn shì jīng yàn hěn duō.
7. Although it is cold today, there is no wind.
   - 虽然今天冷，但是没有风。
   - suī rán jīn tiān lěng, dàn shì méi yǒu fēng.
8. Although this question is difficult, I want to try it.
   - 虽然这道题难，但是我想试试。
   - suī rán zhè dào tí nán, dàn shì wǒ xiǎng shì shì.
9. Although the room is small, it is comfortable.
   - 虽然房间小，但是很舒服。
   - suī rán fáng jiān xiǎo, dàn shì hěn shū fú.
10. Although I disagree, I understand your thinking.
   - 虽然我不同意，但是我理解你的想法。
   - suī rán wǒ bù tóng yì, dàn shì wǒ lǐ jiě nǐ de xiǎng fǎ.

## grammar-063: Conditions with 如果…就…

**core · 如果 + condition，就 + consequence**

Use 如果 to introduce a hypothetical condition. 就 usually appears before the main-clause verb, after its subject. An English conditional does not require a special verb tense in Chinese.

1. If it rains tomorrow, we will not go.
   - 如果明天下雨，我们就不去了。
   - rú guǒ míng tiān xià yǔ, wǒ men jiù bù qù le.
2. If you have time, call me.
   - 如果你有时间，就给我打电话。
   - rú guǒ nǐ yǒu shí jiān, jiù gěi wǒ dǎ diàn huà.
3. If it is too expensive, I will not buy it.
   - 如果太贵，我就不买。
   - rú guǒ tài guì, wǒ jiù bù mǎi.
4. If you do not understand, ask the teacher.
   - 如果你不懂，就问老师。
   - rú guǒ nǐ bù dǒng, jiù wèn lǎo shī.
5. If we cannot finish today, we will continue tomorrow.
   - 如果今天做不完，我们就明天继续。
   - rú guǒ jīn tiān zuò bù wán, wǒ men jiù míng tiān jì xù.
6. If we take the subway, we will not get stuck in traffic.
   - 如果坐地铁，就不会堵车。
   - rú guǒ zuò dì tiě, jiù bù huì dǔ chē.
7. If you like it, take it.
   - 如果你喜欢，就拿走吧。
   - rú guǒ nǐ xǐ huān, jiù ná zǒu ba.
8. If he comes, I will tell you.
   - 如果他来，我就告诉你。
   - rú guǒ tā lái, wǒ jiù gào sù nǐ.
9. If you feel unwell, rest for a little while.
   - 如果身体不舒服，就休息一下。
   - rú guǒ shēn tǐ bù shū fú, jiù xiū xī yī xià.
10. If you cannot find the address, call the front desk.
   - 如果找不到地址，就给前台打电话。
   - rú guǒ zhǎo bù dào dì zhǐ, jiù gěi qián tái dǎ diàn huà.

## grammar-064: Sufficient conditions with 只要…就…

**core · 只要 + sufficient condition，就 + result**

只要 states a condition sufficient for the result: 'as long as'. It does not mean that this is the only possible route to the result; that necessary-condition meaning belongs to 只有…才….

1. As long as you work hard, you will improve.
   - 只要你努力，就会进步。
   - zhǐ yào nǐ nǔ lì, jiù huì jìn bù.
2. As long as it does not rain, we will go hiking.
   - 只要不下雨，我们就去爬山。
   - zhǐ yào bù xià yǔ, wǒ men jiù qù pá shān.
3. As long as I have time, I will come to see you.
   - 只要有时间，我就会来看你。
   - zhǐ yào yǒu shí jiān, wǒ jiù huì lái kàn nǐ.
4. As long as you bring your passport, you can check in.
   - 只要带护照，就能办理入住。
   - zhǐ yào dài hù zhào, jiù néng bàn lǐ rù zhù.
5. As long as you are willing, we will study together.
   - 只要你愿意，我们就一起学。
   - zhǐ yào nǐ yuàn yì, wǒ men jiù yī qǐ xué.
6. As long as you book in advance, you can get a seat.
   - 只要提前订票，就能买到座位。
   - zhǐ yào tí qián dìng piào, jiù néng mǎi dào zuò wèi.
7. As long as you practice every day, you will not forget.
   - 只要每天练习，就不会忘。
   - zhǐ yào měi tiān liàn xí, jiù bù huì wàng.
8. As long as the price is right, I will buy it.
   - 只要价格合适，我就买。
   - zhǐ yào jià gé hé shì, wǒ jiù mǎi.
9. As long as you tell me the address, I can find it.
   - 只要你告诉我地址，我就能找到。
   - zhǐ yào nǐ gào sù wǒ dì zhǐ, wǒ jiù néng zhǎo dào.
10. As long as everyone agrees, we will start.
   - 只要大家同意，我们就开始。
   - zhǐ yào dà jiā tóng yì, wǒ men jiù kāi shǐ.

## grammar-065: Necessary conditions with 只有…才…

**core · 只有 + necessary condition，才 + result**

只有 introduces a necessary condition: 'only if' or 'only by'. 才 links that requirement to the result. It does not claim the condition by itself guarantees success.

1. Only by practicing more can you speak fluently.
   - 只有多练习，才能说得流利。
   - zhǐ yǒu duō liàn xí, cái néng shuō de liú lì.

   - 只有买票，才能进去。
   - zhǐ yǒu mǎi piào, cái néng jìn qù.
3. Only by entering the correct password can you open the file.
   - 只有输入正确的密码，才能打开文件。
   - zhǐ yǒu shū rù zhèng què de mì mǎ, cái néng dǎ kāi wén jiàn.
4. Only on weekends do I have time to cook.
   - 只有周末，我才有时间做饭。
   - zhǐ yǒu zhōu mò, wǒ cái yǒu shí jiān zuò fàn.
5. Only by flying can we arrive on time.
   - 只有坐飞机，才能准时到。
   - zhǐ yǒu zuò fēi jī, cái néng zhǔn shí dào.
6. You can take photos only if you get permission.
   - 只有得到允许，才能拍照。
   - zhǐ yǒu dé dào yǔn xǔ, cái néng pāi zhào.
7. Only by going to see it yourself will you understand.
   - 只有亲自去看，才会明白。
   - zhǐ yǒu qīn zì qù kàn, cái huì míng bái.
8. Only if we close the window will the room get warm.
   - 只有把窗户关上，房间才会暖和。
   - zhǐ yǒu bǎ chuāng hù guān shàng, fáng jiān cái huì nuǎn huo.
9. You can play games only if you finish your homework first.
   - 只有先完成作业，才能玩游戏。
   - zhǐ yǒu xiān wán chéng zuò yè, cái néng wán yóu xì.
10. Only by checking carefully can you find these mistakes.
   - 只有认真检查，才能发现这些错误。
   - zhǐ yǒu rèn zhēn jiǎn chá, cái néng fā xiàn zhè xiē cuò wù.

## grammar-066: Even if with 即使…也…

**stretch · 即使 + hypothetical concession，也 + result**

即使 introduces a hypothetical or conceded condition that does not change the result. Pair it with 也. Unlike ordinary 虽然, 即使 does not require the condition to be an established fact.

1. Even if it rains, I will go.
   - 即使下雨，我也会去。
   - jí shǐ xià yǔ, wǒ yě huì qù.
2. Even if he is busy, he will call home.
   - 即使很忙，他也会给家里打电话。
   - jí shǐ hěn máng, tā yě huì gěi jiā lǐ dǎ diàn huà.
3. Even if I fail, I will not give up.
   - 即使失败了，我也不放弃。
   - jí shǐ shī bài le, wǒ yě bù fàng qì.
4. Even if you do not say it, I know.
   - 即使你不说，我也知道。
   - jí shǐ nǐ bù shuō, wǒ yě zhī dào.
5. Even if it is cheap, I do not want to buy it.
   - 即使便宜，我也不想买。
   - jí shǐ pián yi, wǒ yě bù xiǎng mǎi.
6. Even if you have only ten minutes, you should practice.
   - 即使只有十分钟，也要练习。
   - jí shǐ zhǐ yǒu shí fēn zhōng, yě yào liàn xí.
7. Even if nobody helps, she can finish it.
   - 即使没有人帮忙，她也能完成。
   - jí shǐ méi yǒu rén bāng máng, tā yě néng wán chéng.
8. Even if we take a taxi, we will not make it in time.
   - 即使坐出租车，也来不及了。
   - jí shǐ zuò chū zū chē, yě lái bù jí le.
9. Even if we live far apart, we can meet often.
   - 即使住得远，我们也能常见面。
   - jí shǐ zhù de yuǎn, wǒ men yě néng cháng jiàn miàn.
10. Even if you disagree, please hear me out.
   - 即使你不同意，也请听我说完。
   - jí shǐ nǐ bù tóng yì, yě qǐng tīng wǒ shuō wán.

## grammar-067: Regardless with 不管…都…

**core · 不管 + alternatives/question phrase，都 + result**

Use 不管 with a question-word expression or alternatives, followed by 都 or 也, to mean 'no matter'. The question word has an unrestricted meaning here rather than asking a direct question.

1. No matter how busy I am, I read every day.
   - 不管多忙，我都每天看书。
   - bù guǎn duō máng, wǒ dōu měi tiān kàn shū.
2. No matter where you go, I support you.
   - 不管你去哪儿，我都支持你。
   - bù guǎn nǐ qù nǎ er, wǒ dōu zhī chí nǐ.
3. No matter what the weather is like, we must set off.
   - 不管天气怎么样，我们都要出发。
   - bù guǎn tiān qì zěn me yàng, wǒ men dōu yào chū fā.
4. No matter who comes, they must register.
   - 不管谁来，都要登记。
   - bù guǎn shuí lái, dōu yào dēng jì.
5. Whether it is expensive or not, I will buy it.
   - 不管贵不贵，我都要买。
   - bù guǎn guì bù guì, wǒ dōu yào mǎi.
6. No matter which one you choose, I agree.
   - 不管你选哪个，我都同意。
   - bù guǎn nǐ xuǎn nǎ ge, wǒ dōu tóng yì.
7. You can contact me at any time.
   - 不管什么时候，你都可以联系我。
   - bù guǎn shén me shí hòu, nǐ dōu kě yǐ lián xì wǒ.
8. No matter what problems you encounter, do not give up.
   - 不管遇到什么问题，都别放弃。
   - bù guǎn yù dào shén me wèn tí, dōu bié fàng qì.
9. Whether it rains or not, he goes running.
   - 不管下不下雨，他都去跑步。
   - bù guǎn xià bù xià yǔ, tā dōu qù pǎo bù.
10. Regardless of the result, we thank you.
   - 不管结果如何，我们都感谢你。
   - bù guǎn jié guǒ rú hé, wǒ men dōu gǎn xiè nǐ.

## grammar-068: Addition with 不但…而且…

**core · 不但 + first point，而且 + additional point**

Use 不但…而且… for 'not only…but also'. With a shared subject, that subject can precede 不但. When the subjects differ, 不但 commonly comes before the first subject.

1. She can speak not only Chinese but also Japanese.
   - 她不但会说中文，而且会说日语。
   - tā bù dàn huì shuō zhōng wén, ér qiě huì shuō rì yǔ.
2. This shop is not only cheap but also has very good service.
   - 这家店不但便宜，而且服务很好。
   - zhè jiā diàn bù dàn pián yi, ér qiě fú wù hěn hǎo.
3. He not only finished his work but also helped others.
   - 他不但完成了工作，而且帮助了别人。
   - tā bù dàn wán chéng le gōng zuò, ér qiě bāng zhù le bié rén.
4. Exercise is not only good for the body but also helps people relax.
   - 运动不但对身体好，而且能让人放松。
   - yùn dòng bù dàn duì shēn tǐ hǎo, ér qiě néng ràng rén fàng sōng.
5. This room is not only big but also bright.
   - 这个房间不但大，而且很亮。
   - zhè ge fáng jiān bù dàn dà, ér qiě hěn liàng.
6. Not only do I like this book, but my younger sister likes it too.
   - 不但我喜欢这本书，而且我妹妹也喜欢。
   - bù dàn wǒ xǐ huān zhè běn shū, ér qiě wǒ mèi mèi yě xǐ huān.
7. She is not only smart but also very patient.
   - 她不但聪明，而且很有耐心。
   - tā bù dàn cōng míng, ér qiě hěn yǒu nài xīn.
8. This movie is not only interesting but also makes people think.
   - 这部电影不但有趣，而且让人思考。
   - zhè bù diàn yǐng bù dàn yǒu qù, ér qiě ràng rén sī kǎo.
9. Taking the subway is not only fast but also convenient.
   - 坐地铁不但快，而且方便。
   - zuò dì tiě bù dàn kuài, ér qiě fāng biàn.
10. He remembered not only the name but also the address.
   - 他不但记住了名字，而且记住了地址。
   - tā bù dàn jì zhù le míng zì, ér qiě jì zhù le dì zhǐ.

## grammar-069: Besides with 除了…以外，还…

**core · 除了 + included item + 以外，还/也 + addition**

Use 除了…以外 with 还 or 也 to add something besides the named item. This inclusive use differs from the exception structure 除了…以外，都….

1. Besides Chinese, she can also speak French.
   - 除了中文以外，她还会说法语。
   - chú le zhōng wén yǐ wài, tā hái huì shuō fǎ yǔ.
2. Besides coffee, I also bought tea.
   - 除了咖啡以外，我还买了茶。
   - chú le kā fēi yǐ wài, wǒ hái mǎi le chá.
3. Besides reading, he also likes swimming.
   - 除了看书以外，他还喜欢游泳。
   - chú le kàn shū yǐ wài, tā hái xǐ huān yóu yǒng.
4. Besides Beijing, we also went to Xi'an.
   - 除了北京以外，我们还去了西安。
   - chú le běi jīng yǐ wài, wǒ men hái qù le xī ān.
5. Besides Monday, I am also free on Wednesday.
   - 除了星期一以外，星期三我也有空。
   - chú le xīng qī yī yǐ wài, xīng qī sān wǒ yě yǒu kòng.
6. Besides your passport, you also need to bring photos.
   - 除了护照以外，你还要带照片。
   - chú le hù zhào yǐ wài, nǐ hái yào dài zhào piān.
7. Besides the price, we also need to consider the quality.
   - 除了价格以外，我们还要考虑质量。
   - chú le jià gé yǐ wài, wǒ men hái yào kǎo lǜ zhì liàng.
8. Besides the teachers, the students also took part in the activity.
   - 除了老师以外，学生也参加了活动。
   - chú le lǎo shī yǐ wài, xué shēng yě cān jiā le huó dòng.
9. Besides cooking, he is also responsible for washing the dishes.
   - 除了做饭以外，他还负责洗碗。
   - chú le zuò fàn yǐ wài, tā hái fù zé xǐ wǎn.
10. Besides this book, I also recommend that one.
   - 除了这本书以外，我还推荐那本。
   - chú le zhè běn shū yǐ wài, wǒ hái tuī jiàn nà běn.

## grammar-070: Exceptions with 除了…以外，都…

**core · 除了 + exception + 以外，group + 都 + predicate**

Use 除了 to set aside an exception, then 都 to make a statement about the rest of a group. The predicate determines what the exception is; 还 would instead signal addition.

1. Everyone has arrived except him.
   - 除了他以外，大家都到了。
   - chú le tā yǐ wài, dà jiā dōu dào le.
2. I work every day except Sunday.
   - 除了星期天以外，我每天都上班。
   - chú le xīng qī tiān yǐ wài, wǒ měi tiān dōu shàng bān.
3. I can do all the other questions except this one.
   - 除了这道题以外，其他题我都会做。
   - chú le zhè dào tí yǐ wài, qí tā tí wǒ dōu huì zuò.
4. I like all these dishes except the fish.
   - 除了鱼以外，这些菜我都喜欢。
   - chú le yú yǐ wài, zhè xiē cài wǒ dōu xǐ huān.
5. All of us have been to Shanghai except Xiao Wang.
   - 除了小王以外，我们都去过上海。
   - chú le xiǎo wáng yǐ wài, wǒ men dōu qù guò shàng hǎi.
6. All the other rooms have been cleaned except the kitchen.
   - 除了厨房以外，其他房间都打扫了。
   - chú le chú fáng yǐ wài, qí tā fáng jiān dōu dǎ sǎo le.
7. All the other colors are available except red.
   - 除了红色以外，其他颜色都有。
   - chú le hóng sè yǐ wài, qí tā yán sè dōu yǒu.
8. I have read everything except the last page.
   - 除了最后一页以外，我都看完了。
   - chú le zuì hòu yī yè yǐ wài, wǒ dōu kàn wán le.
9. All the nearby shops have closed except this one.
   - 除了这家店以外，附近的商店都关门了。
   - chú le zhè jiā diàn yǐ wài, fù jìn de shāng diàn dōu guān mén le.
10. He gets up at six every day except on weekends.
   - 除了周末以外，他每天都六点起床。
   - chú le zhōu mò yǐ wài, tā měi tiān dōu liù diǎn qǐ chuáng.

## grammar-071: Simultaneous actions with 一边…一边…

**core · 一边 + action A，一边 + action B**

Use 一边…一边… for two activities performed at the same time, typically by the same person. It suits ongoing actions more naturally than brief one-time results.

1. I listen to music while cooking.
   - 我一边做饭，一边听音乐。
   - wǒ yī biān zuò fàn, yī biān tīng yīn yuè.
2. She talks on the phone while walking.
   - 她一边走路，一边打电话。
   - tā yī biān zǒu lù, yī biān dǎ diàn huà.
3. We chat while eating.
   - 我们一边吃饭，一边聊天。
   - wǒ men yī biān chī fàn, yī biān liáo tiān.
4. He eats fruit while watching television.
   - 他一边看电视，一边吃水果。
   - tā yī biān kàn diàn shì, yī biān chī shuǐ guǒ.
5. The teacher draws diagrams while explaining.
   - 老师一边解释，一边画图。
   - lǎo shī yī biān jiě shì, yī biān huà tú.
6. The child sings and dances at the same time.
   - 孩子一边唱歌，一边跳舞。
   - hái zi yī biān chàng gē, yī biān tiào wǔ.
7. I take notes while listening in class.
   - 我一边听课，一边记笔记。
   - wǒ yī biān tīng kè, yī biān jì bǐ jì.
8. She talks to me while tidying the room.
   - 她一边收拾房间，一边跟我说话。
   - tā yī biān shōu shí fáng jiān, yī biān gēn wǒ shuō huà.
9. He listens to the news while running.
   - 他一边跑步，一边听新闻。
   - tā yī biān pǎo bù, yī biān tīng xīn wén.
10. We discuss the plan while drinking tea.
   - 我们一边喝茶，一边讨论计划。
   - wǒ men yī biān hē chá, yī biān tǎo lùn jì huà.

## grammar-072: Immediate sequence with 一…就…

**core · 一 + trigger，就 + consequence**

Use 一…就… for 'as soon as' or an immediate, regular response to a trigger. It can describe one occasion or a recurring pattern, depending on the time context.

1. I will call you as soon as I get home.
   - 我一到家就给你打电话。
   - wǒ yī dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diàn huà.
2. As soon as he drinks coffee, he cannot get to sleep.
   - 他一喝咖啡就睡不着。
   - tā yī hē kā fēi jiù shuì bù zháo.
3. She cried as soon as she heard the news.
   - 她一听到消息就哭了。
   - tā yī tīng dào xiāo xī jiù kū le.
4. We go to the cafeteria as soon as class ends.
   - 我们一下课就去食堂。
   - wǒ men yī xià kè jiù qù shí táng.
5. As soon as the door opened, the child ran in.
   - 门一开，孩子就跑进来了。
   - mén yī kāi, hái zi jiù pǎo jìn lái le.
6. I understood as soon as I looked at it.
   - 我一看就明白了。
   - wǒ yī kàn jiù míng bái le.
7. He goes swimming as soon as he has free time.
   - 他一有空就去游泳。
   - tā yī yǒu kòng jiù qù yóu yǒng.
8. We will set off as soon as the rain stops.
   - 雨一停，我们就出发。
   - yǔ yī tíng, wǒ men jiù chū fā.
9. She fell asleep as soon as she got in the car.
   - 她一上车就睡着了。
   - tā yī shàng chē jiù shuì zháo le.
10. Tell me as soon as you receive the email.
   - 你一收到邮件就告诉我。
   - nǐ yī shōu dào yóu jiàn jiù gào sù wǒ.

## grammar-073: Ordered steps with 先…再…然后…

**review · 先 + first action，再 + second，然后 + next**

Use 先, 再, and 然后 to order steps. 再 introduces the next intended action, while 然后 is a general sequence connector. Include only as many stages as the situation needs.

1. Wash your hands first, then eat.
   - 先洗手，再吃饭。
   - xiān xǐ shǒu, zài chī fàn.
2. We will buy tickets first, then go in.
   - 我们先买票，再进去。
   - wǒ men xiān mǎi piào, zài jìn qù.
3. Rest a little first, then get back to work.
   - 你先休息一下，然后再工作。
   - nǐ xiān xiū xī yī xià, rán hòu zài gōng zuò.
4. I will check the map first, then decide which way to go.
   - 我先查地图，再决定怎么走。
   - wǒ xiān chá dì tú, zài jué dìng zěn me zǒu.
5. Fill out the form first, then give it to the front desk.
   - 先填表，然后交给前台。
   - xiān tián biǎo, rán hòu jiāo gěi qián tái.
6. She does her homework first, then watches television.
   - 她先做作业，再看电视。
   - tā xiān zuò zuò yè, zài kàn diàn shì.
7. We will eat first, then go to see a movie.
   - 我们先吃饭，再去看电影。
   - wǒ men xiān chī fàn, zài qù kàn diàn yǐng.
8. Boil the water first, then add the noodles.
   - 先把水烧开，然后放面条。
   - xiān bǎ shuǐ shāo kāi, rán hòu fàng miàn tiáo.
9. Listen once first, then read along.
   - 你先听一遍，再跟着读。
   - nǐ xiān tīng yī biàn, zài gēn zhe dú.
10. I will call to make an appointment first, then go to the hospital.
   - 我先打电话预约，再去医院。
   - wǒ xiān dǎ diàn huà yù yuē, zài qù yī yuàn.

## grammar-074: Before with 以前

**review · Event/time + 以前**

Place 以前 after a time or event to mean 'before'. Without a preceding event, 以前 can mean 'formerly'. For a not-yet-completed event, Chinese commonly uses the event phrase without future tense marking.

1. Before going to sleep, I read for a little while.
   - 睡觉以前，我会看一会儿书。
   - shuì jiào yǐ qián, wǒ huì kàn yī huì er shū.
2. Please turn off the lights before going out.
   - 出门以前，请关灯。
   - chū mén yǐ qián, qǐng guān dēng.
3. You should wash your hands before eating.
   - 吃饭以前要洗手。
   - chī fàn yǐ qián yào xǐ shǒu.
4. Before coming to China, I could not use chopsticks.
   - 来中国以前，我不会用筷子。
   - lái zhōng guó yǐ qián, wǒ bù huì yòng kuài zi.
5. Before the exam, she was nervous.
   - 考试以前，她很紧张。
   - kǎo shì yǐ qián, tā hěn jǐn zhāng.
6. Please read the contract carefully before signing.
   - 签字以前，请仔细看合同。
   - qiān zì yǐ qián, qǐng zǐ xì kàn hé tóng.
7. I will definitely arrive before nine.
   - 九点以前，我一定到。
   - jiǔ diǎn yǐ qián, wǒ yī dìng dào.
8. Before starting university, he lived at home.
   - 上大学以前，他住在家里。
   - shàng dà xué yǐ qián, tā zhù zài jiā lǐ.
9. Check the weather before buying tickets.
   - 买票以前，先看看天气。
   - mǎi piào yǐ qián, xiān kàn kàn tiān qì.
10. Before moving, we sold the old furniture.
   - 搬家以前，我们卖了旧家具。
   - bān jiā yǐ qián, wǒ men mài le jiù jiā jù.

## grammar-075: After with 以后

**review · Event/time + 以后**

Place 以后 after a time or event to mean 'after'. Used alone, it can mean 'in the future' or 'later'. The main clause may use 就 or 再 to make the sequence explicit.

1. After work, I go to the supermarket.
   - 下班以后，我去超市。
   - xià bān yǐ hòu, wǒ qù chāo shì.
2. Let us take a walk after finishing the meal.
   - 吃完饭以后，我们散步吧。
   - chī wán fàn yǐ hòu, wǒ men sàn bù ba.
3. After graduating, she wants to become a teacher.
   - 毕业以后，她想当老师。
   - bì yè yǐ hòu, tā xiǎng dāng lǎo shī.
4. Send me a message after you get home.
   - 到家以后，给我发个消息。
   - dào jiā yǐ hòu, gěi wǒ fā gè xiāo xī.
5. After watching the movie, we chatted for a long time.
   - 看完电影以后，我们聊了很久。
   - kàn wán diàn yǐng yǐ hòu, wǒ men liáo le hěn jiǔ.
6. Please keep quiet after ten.
   - 十点以后，请保持安静。
   - shí diǎn yǐ hòu, qǐng bǎo chí ān jìng.
7. After moving, I live closer to the company.
   - 搬家以后，我离公司更近了。
   - bān jiā yǐ hòu, wǒ lí gōng sī gèng jìn le.
8. After learning to drive, he rarely takes the bus.
   - 学会开车以后，他很少坐公交车。
   - xué huì kāi chē yǐ hòu, tā hěn shǎo zuò gōng jiāo chē.
9. After receiving the money, I will send the parcel.
   - 收到钱以后，我会寄出包裹。
   - shōu dào qián yǐ hòu, wǒ huì jì chū bāo guǒ.
10. Come and help after washing your hands.
   - 洗完手以后，再来帮忙。
   - xǐ wán shǒu yǐ hòu, zài lái bāng máng.

## grammar-076: Disposal with 把

**core · Subject + 把 + identifiable object + verb + result**

Use 把 to bring an identifiable affected object before the verb and explain what happens to it. The verb phrase normally includes a result, direction, or other elaboration. Put negation and modal verbs before 把.

1. Please close the door.
   - 请把门关上。
   - qǐng bǎ mén guān shàng.
2. I put the keys on the table.
   - 我把钥匙放在桌子上了。
   - wǒ bǎ yào shi fàng zài zhuō zi shàng le.
3. She finished the homework.
   - 她把作业做完了。
   - tā bǎ zuò yè zuò wán le.
4. Please translate this sentence into Chinese.
   - 请把这句话翻译成中文。
   - qǐng bǎ zhè jù huà fān yì chéng zhōng wén.
5. He broke the cup.
   - 他把杯子打破了。
   - tā bǎ bēi zi dǎ pò le.
6. We moved the meeting to Friday.
   - 我们把会议改到星期五了。
   - wǒ men bǎ huì yì gǎi dào xīng qī wǔ le.
7. Please turn off your phone.
   - 请把手机关掉。
   - qǐng bǎ shǒu jī guān diào.
8. I returned that book to the teacher.
   - 我把那本书还给老师了。
   - wǒ bǎ nà běn shū huán gěi lǎo shī le.
9. She tidied the room until it was clean.
   - 她把房间收拾干净了。
   - tā bǎ fáng jiān shōu shí gān jìng le.
10. Do not leave your passport at home.
   - 别把护照忘在家里。
   - bié bǎ hù zhào wàng zài jiā lǐ.

## grammar-077: Passive with 被

**core · Affected subject + 被 + agent + verb + result**

Use 被 to make the affected person or thing the subject. The agent can sometimes be omitted. The verb phrase usually describes a result or event, and the passive often—but not always—concerns an unwelcome outcome.

1. My bicycle was stolen by someone.
   - 我的自行车被人偷了。
   - wǒ de zì xíng chē bèi rén tōu le.
2. The window was blown open by the wind.
   - 窗户被风吹开了。
   - chuāng hù bèi fēng chuī kāi le.
3. The cake was eaten up by the child.
   - 蛋糕被孩子吃完了。
   - dàn gāo bèi hái zi chī wán le.
4. My cup was broken by him.
   - 我的杯子被他打破了。
   - wǒ de bēi zi bèi tā dǎ pò le.
5. She was praised by the teacher.
   - 她被老师表扬了。
   - tā bèi lǎo shī biǎo yáng le.
6. My name was written incorrectly.
   - 我的名字被写错了。
   - wǒ de míng zì bèi xiě cuò le.
7. The door was locked by someone.
   - 门被人锁上了。
   - mén bèi rén suǒ shàng le.
8. This book was translated into Chinese.
   - 这本书被翻译成了中文。
   - zhè běn shū bèi fān yì chéng le zhōng wén.
9. My plan was rejected by the manager.
   - 我的计划被经理拒绝了。
   - wǒ de jì huà bèi jīng lǐ jù jué le.
10. He was chosen as class monitor.
   - 他被选为班长了。
   - tā bèi xuǎn wéi bān zhǎng le.

## grammar-078: Highlighting past details with 是…的

**core · Subject + 是 + highlighted detail + verb + 的**

Use 是…的 to highlight the time, place, manner, or agent of an event treated as already established. It is not a general past tense. 是 is often omitted in affirmative sentences but retained in negative 不是…的.

1. It was yesterday that I arrived.
   - 我是昨天到的。
   - wǒ shì zuó tiān dào de.
   - Also accepted: 我昨天到的。
2. It was by subway that she came.
   - 她是坐地铁来的。
   - tā shì zuò dì tiě lái de.
3. It was in Beijing that we met.
   - 我们是在北京认识的。
   - wǒ men shì zài běi jīng rèn shí de.
4. It was me who took this photo.
   - 这张照片是我拍的。
   - zhè zhāng zhào piān shì wǒ pāi de.
5. It was with friends that he went.
   - 他是跟朋友一起去的。
   - tā shì gēn péng yǒu yī qǐ qù de.
6. When was it that you bought it?
   - 你是什么时候买的？
   - nǐ shì shén me shí hòu mǎi de?
7. It was online that I booked the tickets.
   - 我是在网上订的票。
   - wǒ shì zài wǎng shàng dìng de piào.
8. It was not by plane that I came.
   - 我不是坐飞机来的。
   - wǒ bù shì zuò fēi jī lái de.
9. It was last year that we moved here.
   - 我们是去年搬来的。
   - wǒ men shì qù nián bān lái de.
10. It was Mom who made this dish.
   - 这道菜是妈妈做的。
   - zhè dào cài shì mā mā zuò de.

## grammar-079: Requesting or inviting with 请

**review · 请 + person + verb**

Put 请 before a verb or before a person plus verb to make a polite request or invitation. Courtesy also depends on the whole sentence and situation; 请 is not required in every polite Chinese request.

1. Please sit down.
   - 请坐。
   - qǐng zuò.
2. Please wait a moment.
   - 请稍等一下。
   - qǐng shāo děng yī xià.
3. Please help me open the door.
   - 请你帮我开一下门。
   - qǐng nǐ bāng wǒ kāi yī xià mén.
4. Everyone, please keep quiet.
   - 请大家保持安静。
   - qǐng dà jiā bǎo chí ān jìng.
5. Please give me the menu.
   - 请把菜单给我。
   - qǐng bǎ cài dān gěi wǒ.
6. Please sign here.
   - 请在这里签字。
   - qǐng zài zhè lǐ qiān zì.
7. Please tell me your name.
   - 请告诉我你的名字。
   - qǐng gào sù wǒ nǐ de míng zì.
8. Please come again tomorrow.
   - 请明天再来。
   - qǐng míng tiān zài lái.
9. Please do not smoke here.
   - 请不要在这里吸烟。
   - qǐng bù yào zài zhè lǐ xī yān.
10. Please give me a glass of warm water.
   - 请给我一杯温水。
   - qǐng gěi wǒ yī bēi wēn shuǐ.

## grammar-080: Prohibitions with 别

**review · 别 + verb**

Use 别 before a verb to tell someone not to do something. 不要 is a common alternative. 别…了 can ask someone to stop an activity already underway.

1. Do not worry.
   - 别担心。
   - bié dān xīn.
   - Also accepted: 不要担心。
2. Do not be late.
   - 别迟到。
   - bié chí dào.
   - Also accepted: 不要迟到。
3. Do not forget to bring your passport.
   - 别忘了带护照。
   - bié wàng le dài hù zhào.
   - Also accepted: 不要忘了带护照。
4. Do not park here.
   - 别在这里停车。
   - bié zài zhè lǐ tíng chē.
   - Also accepted: 不要在这里停车。
5. Stop talking.
   - 别说话了。
   - bié shuō huà le.
   - Also accepted: 不要说话了。
6. Do not close the door.
   - 别把门关上。
   - bié bǎ mén guān shàng.
   - Also accepted: 不要把门关上。
7. Do not eat too much sugar.
   - 别吃太多糖。
   - bié chī tài duō táng.
   - Also accepted: 不要吃太多糖。
8. Do not tell other people.
   - 别告诉别人。
   - bié gào sù bié rén.
   - Also accepted: 不要告诉别人。
9. Do not rush; take your time.
   - 别着急，慢慢来。
   - bié zháo jí, màn màn lái.
   - Also accepted: 不要着急，慢慢来。
10. Stop calling.
   - 别再打电话了。
   - bié zài dǎ diàn huà le.
   - Also accepted: 不要再打电话了。

## grammar-081: Suggestions with 吧

**review · Proposal + 吧**

Place 吧 at the end of a proposal, invitation, or softened instruction. Its force depends on context; it can also signal a tentative guess, which is a separate use.

1. Let us go.
   - 我们走吧。
   - wǒ men zǒu ba.
2. Let us have lunch together.
   - 一起吃午饭吧。
   - yī qǐ chī wǔ fàn ba.
3. Why don't you rest a little first?
   - 你先休息一下吧。
   - nǐ xiān xiū xī yī xià ba.
4. Let us take the subway.
   - 我们坐地铁吧。
   - wǒ men zuò dì tiě ba.
5. Let us talk about it tomorrow.
   - 明天再说吧。
   - míng tiān zài shuō ba.
6. Please come on in.
   - 请进来吧。
   - qǐng jìn lái ba.
7. Let us change the subject.
   - 我们换个话题吧。
   - wǒ men huàn gè huà tí ba.
8. Why don't you give it a try?
   - 你试试吧。
   - nǐ shì shì ba.
9. Let us meet at the entrance.
   - 我们在门口见吧。
   - wǒ men zài mén kǒu jiàn ba.
10. Why don't you open the window?
   - 把窗户打开吧。
   - bǎ chuāng hù dǎ kāi ba.

## grammar-082: Brief actions with 一下

**review · Verb + 一下**

Use 一下 after a verb to make an action brief, tentative, or softer in a request. It often means 'for a moment' or 'give it a…', not a literal count of one occurrence.

1. Please wait briefly.
   - 请等一下。
   - qǐng děng yī xià.
2. I would like to take a quick look at the menu.
   - 我想看一下菜单。
   - wǒ xiǎng kàn yī xià cài dān.
3. Could you help me check it briefly?
   - 你能帮我检查一下吗？
   - nǐ néng bāng wǒ jiǎn chá yī xià ma?
4. Let me think for a moment.
   - 让我想一下。
   - ràng wǒ xiǎng yī xià.
5. Please briefly introduce yourself.
   - 请介绍一下你自己。
   - qǐng jiè shào yī xià nǐ zì jǐ.
6. I will go and ask the teacher quickly.
   - 我去问一下老师。
   - wǒ qù wèn yī xià lǎo shī.
7. Rest a little first.
   - 你先休息一下。
   - nǐ xiān xiū xī yī xià.
8. Please try on this pair of shoes.
   - 请试一下这双鞋。
   - qǐng shì yī xià zhè shuāng xié.
9. I would like to get a brief idea of the situation.
   - 我想了解一下情况。
   - wǒ xiǎng liǎo jiě yī xià qíng kuàng.
10. Please take a moment to note my number.
   - 请记一下我的号码。
   - qǐng jì yī xià wǒ de hào mǎ.

## grammar-083: Tentative actions by reduplication

**core · Verb-verb / verb-一-verb**

Repeat a suitable action verb to suggest doing it briefly or casually. Single-syllable verbs can use AA or A一A; many two-syllable verbs use ABAB. Do not automatically reduplicate result compounds.

1. Take a look at this photo.
   - 你看看这张照片。
   - nǐ kàn kàn zhè zhāng zhào piān.
   - Also accepted: 你看一看这张照片。
2. I would like to give it a try.
   - 我想试试。
   - wǒ xiǎng shì shì.
   - Also accepted: 我想试一试。
3. Let us have a little chat.
   - 我们聊聊吧。
   - wǒ men liáo liáo ba.
   - Also accepted: 我们聊一聊吧。
4. Think it over a bit more.
   - 你再想想。
   - nǐ zài xiǎng xiǎng.
   - Also accepted: 你再想一想。
5. Please have a taste of this dish.
   - 请尝尝这道菜。
   - qǐng cháng cháng zhè dào cài.
   - Also accepted: 请尝一尝这道菜。
6. Let us go for a stroll in the park this weekend.
   - 周末我们去公园走走。
   - zhōu mò wǒ men qù gōng yuán zǒu zǒu.
7. I would like to rest a little.
   - 我想休息休息。
   - wǒ xiǎng xiū xī xiū xī.
8. We need to discuss it a bit.
   - 我们需要讨论讨论。
   - wǒ men xū yào tǎo lùn tǎo lùn.
9. Why don't you ask him?
   - 你问问他吧。
   - nǐ wèn wèn tā ba.
   - Also accepted: 你问一问他吧。
10. Come spend a little time at my home when you are free.
   - 有空来我家坐坐。
   - yǒu kòng lái wǒ jiā zuò zuò.

## grammar-084: Causing or allowing with 让

**core · Person + 让 + another person + verb**

Use 让 to introduce the person caused or allowed to act. Depending on context, it means 'let', 'have', or 'make'. The person after 让 is the subject of the following verb phrase.

1. The teacher told us to hand in the homework tomorrow.
   - 老师让我们明天交作业。
   - lǎo shī ràng wǒ men míng tiān jiāo zuò yè.
2. Please let me think it over.
   - 请让我想一想。
   - qǐng ràng wǒ xiǎng yī xiǎng.
3. This news makes me happy.
   - 这个消息让我很高兴。
   - zhè ge xiāo xī ràng wǒ hěn gāo xìng.
4. Mom does not let me go out at night.
   - 妈妈不让我晚上出去。
   - mā mā bù ràng wǒ wǎn shàng chū qù.
5. He made me wait for half an hour.
   - 他让我等了半个小时。
   - tā ràng wǒ děng le bàn gè xiǎo shí.
6. This song reminds me of my hometown.
   - 这首歌让我想起了家乡。
   - zhè shǒu gē ràng wǒ xiǎng qǐ le jiā xiāng.
7. Please let the child try it independently.
   - 请让孩子自己试试。
   - qǐng ràng hái zi zì jǐ shì shì.
8. The manager put me in charge of this project.
   - 经理让我负责这个项目。
   - jīng lǐ ràng wǒ fù zé zhè ge xiàng mù.
9. Do not keep the guests waiting too long.
   - 别让客人等太久。
   - bié ràng kè rén děng tài jiǔ.
10. This matter makes me disappointed.
   - 这件事让我很失望。
   - zhè jiàn shì ràng wǒ hěn shī wàng.

## grammar-085: Helping with 帮

**review · 帮 + person + verb**

Place the person being helped after 帮 and the action after that person. 帮忙 can also mean 'help out', but its object behavior differs; avoid treating 帮忙 as a freely transitive replacement for 帮.

1. Can you help me move the suitcase?
   - 你能帮我搬一下箱子吗？
   - nǐ néng bāng wǒ bān yī xià xiāng zi ma?
2. She helps Mom wash the dishes.
   - 她帮妈妈洗碗。
   - tā bāng mā mā xǐ wǎn.
3. Let me help you buy tickets.
   - 我帮你买票吧。
   - wǒ bāng nǐ mǎi piào ba.
4. Please watch my luggage for a moment.
   - 请帮我看一下行李。
   - qǐng bāng wǒ kàn yī xià xíng lǐ.
5. He often helps his classmates review.
   - 他经常帮同学复习。
   - tā jīng cháng bāng tóng xué fù xí.
6. We helped the neighbor find the dog.
   - 我们帮邻居找到了狗。
   - wǒ men bāng lín jū zhǎo dào le gǒu.
7. Can you help me translate this sentence?
   - 你可以帮我翻译这句话吗？
   - nǐ kě yǐ bāng wǒ fān yì zhè jù huà ma?
8. My older sister helped me choose a coat.
   - 姐姐帮我选了一件外套。
   - jiě jiě bāng wǒ xuǎn le yī jiàn wài tào.
9. Thank you for helping me solve this problem.
   - 谢谢你帮我解决这个问题。
   - xiè xiè nǐ bāng wǒ jiě jué zhè ge wèn tí.
10. I want to help you prepare dinner.
   - 我想帮你准备晚饭。
   - wǒ xiǎng bāng nǐ zhǔn bèi wǎn fàn.

## grammar-086: Giving and recipients with 给

**core · 给 + recipient + verb / verb + 给 + recipient**

给 introduces a recipient or beneficiary. It can appear before an action, as in 给他打电话, or after suitable transfer verbs, as in 送给他. Which position works depends on the verb and intended meaning.

1. I called Mom.
   - 我给妈妈打了电话。
   - wǒ gěi mā mā dǎ le diàn huà.
2. She wrote a letter to a friend.
   - 她给朋友写了一封信。
   - tā gěi péng yǒu xiě le yī fēng xìn.
3. Please pour me a glass of water.
   - 请给我倒一杯水。
   - qǐng gěi wǒ dào yī bēi shuǐ.
4. He gave me a book as a gift.
   - 他送给我一本书。
   - tā sòng gěi wǒ yī běn shū.
5. I returned the money to him.
   - 我把钱还给他了。
   - wǒ bǎ qián huán gěi tā le.
6. Can you explain it to me?
   - 你能给我解释一下吗？
   - nǐ néng gěi wǒ jiě shì yī xià ma?
7. She bought a pair of shoes for the child.
   - 她给孩子买了一双鞋。
   - tā gěi hái zi mǎi le yī shuāng xié.
8. Please send the photo to me.
   - 请把照片发给我。
   - qǐng bǎ zhào piān fā gěi wǒ.
9. I would like to give you a suggestion.
   - 我想给你一个建议。
   - wǒ xiǎng gěi nǐ yī gè jiàn yì.
10. The teacher told us a story.
   - 老师给我们讲了一个故事。
   - lǎo shī gěi wǒ men jiǎng le yī gè gù shì.

## grammar-087: Toward and concerning with 对

**core · 对 + person/thing + attitude/effect**

Use 对 before the person or thing targeted by an attitude, interest, or effect. Common patterns include 对…感兴趣 and 对…有帮助. 对 is not a universal translation of English 'to'.

1. I am interested in Chinese history.
   - 我对中国历史感兴趣。
   - wǒ duì zhōng guó lì shǐ gǎn xìng qù.
2. Exercise is good for the body.
   - 运动对身体有好处。
   - yùn dòng duì shēn tǐ yǒu hǎo chù.
3. He is very patient with children.
   - 他对孩子很有耐心。
   - tā duì hái zi hěn yǒu nài xīn.
4. This method is very helpful to me.
   - 这个办法对我很有帮助。
   - zhè ge bàn fǎ duì wǒ hěn yǒu bāng zhù.
5. She is satisfied with the result.
   - 她对结果很满意。
   - tā duì jié guǒ hěn mǎn yì.
6. What is your view of this plan?
   - 你对这个计划有什么看法？
   - nǐ duì zhè ge jì huà yǒu shén me kàn fǎ?
7. Smoking is bad for health.
   - 抽烟对健康不好。
   - chōu yān duì jiàn kāng bù hǎo.
8. The teacher is strict with us.
   - 老师对我们很严格。
   - lǎo shī duì wǒ men hěn yán gé.
9. I do not know much about this matter.
   - 我对这件事不太了解。
   - wǒ duì zhè jiàn shì bù tài liǎo jiě.
10. He is confident about his future.
   - 他对自己的未来很有信心。
   - tā duì zì jǐ de wèi lái hěn yǒu xìn xīn.

## grammar-088: Distance with 离

**review · Place A + 离 + place B + distance**

Use 离 to introduce the reference point in a distance statement. Add 远, 近, or a measured distance after that point. 离 differs from 从, which marks a starting point for movement or a range.

1. My home is close to the school.
   - 我家离学校很近。
   - wǒ jiā lí xué xiào hěn jìn.
2. The airport is far from the city center.
   - 机场离市中心很远。
   - jī chǎng lí shì zhōng xīn hěn yuǎn.
3. It is two kilometers from here to the station.
   - 这里离车站有两公里。
   - zhè lǐ lí chē zhàn yǒu liǎng gōng lǐ.
4. Is where you live far from the company?
   - 你住的地方离公司远吗？
   - nǐ zhù de dì fang lí gōng sī yuǎn ma?
5. The supermarket is only five hundred meters from our home.
   - 超市离我们家只有五百米。
   - chāo shì lí wǒ men jiā zhǐ yǒu wǔ bǎi mǐ.
6. This hotel is close to the seaside.
   - 这家酒店离海边很近。
   - zhè jiā jiǔ diàn lí hǎi biān hěn jìn.
7. The bank is not far from here.
   - 银行离这里不远。
   - yín háng lí zhè lǐ bù yuǎn.
8. I am sitting close to the door.
   - 我坐得离门很近。
   - wǒ zuò de lí mén hěn jìn.
9. The new office is closer to the subway station.
   - 新办公室离地铁站更近。
   - xīn bàn gōng shì lí dì tiě zhàn gèng jìn.
10. The school is about three kilometers from the hospital.
   - 学校离医院大约三公里。
   - xué xiào lí yī yuàn dà yuē sān gōng lǐ.

## grammar-089: Ranges with 从…到…

**review · 从 + start + 到 + endpoint**

Use 从…到… to mark the endpoints of a range in space or time. For a journey, the travel verb or duration can follow the range. The endpoint is explicit, unlike a simple location phrase with 在.

1. It takes an hour to get from here to the airport.
   - 从这里到机场要一个小时。
   - cóng zhè lǐ dào jī chǎng yào yī gè xiǎo shí.
2. I work from Monday to Friday.
   - 我从星期一到星期五上班。
   - wǒ cóng xīng qī yī dào xīng qī wǔ shàng bān.
3. This shop is open from nine in the morning until ten at night.
   - 这家店从早上九点开到晚上十点。
   - zhè jiā diàn cóng zǎo shàng jiǔ diǎn kāi dào wǎn shàng shí diǎn.
4. Taking the high-speed train from Beijing to Shanghai is convenient.
   - 从北京到上海坐高铁很方便。
   - cóng běi jīng dào shàng hǎi zuò gāo tiě hěn fāng biàn.
5. We walked from the foot of the mountain to the top.
   - 我们从山脚走到了山顶。
   - wǒ men cóng shān jiǎo zǒu dào le shān dǐng.
6. Please read from page one through page five.
   - 请从第一页读到第五页。
   - qǐng cóng dì yī yè dú dào dì wǔ yè.
7. He is busy from morning to night.
   - 他从早到晚都很忙。
   - tā cóng zǎo dào wǎn dōu hěn máng.
8. It is only two stops from my home to the school.
   - 从我家到学校只有两站。
   - cóng wǒ jiā dào xué xiào zhǐ yǒu liǎng zhàn.
9. She has been working here continuously from last year until now.
   - 她从去年到现在一直在这里工作。
   - tā cóng qù nián dào xiàn zài yī zhí zài zhè lǐ gōng zuò.
10. It is hot here from June to August.
   - 从六月到八月，这里很热。
   - cóng liù yuè dào bā yuè, zhè lǐ hěn rè.

## grammar-090: Direction with 往

**core · 往 + direction + motion verb**

Place 往 and a direction before the motion verb to indicate movement toward that direction. 向 can also mark direction in many contexts, but neither automatically means the destination is reached.

1. Please go to the left.
   - 请往左走。
   - qǐng wǎng zuǒ zǒu.
   - Also accepted: 请向左走。
2. Keep going straight ahead.
   - 一直往前走。
   - yī zhí wǎng qián zǒu.
   - Also accepted: 一直向前走。
3. He ran toward the station.
   - 他往车站跑去。
   - tā wǎng chē zhàn pǎo qù.
4. This bus travels north.
   - 这辆公交车往北开。
   - zhè liàng gōng jiāo chē wǎng běi kāi.
5. Please sit a little farther inside.
   - 请往里面坐一点儿。
   - qǐng wǎng lǐ miàn zuò yī diǎn er.
6. Let us walk up toward the mountain.
   - 我们往山上走吧。
   - wǒ men wǎng shān shàng zǒu ba.
7. She glanced out of the window.
   - 她往窗外看了一眼。
   - tā wǎng chuāng wài kàn le yī yǎn.
8. Turn right and you will be at the bank.
   - 往右拐就是银行。
   - wǎng yòu guǎi jiù shì yín háng.
9. Do not step backward.
   - 别往后退。
   - bié wǎng hòu tuì.
   - Also accepted: 不要往后退。
10. He moved the chair a little forward.
   - 他把椅子往前搬了一点儿。
   - tā bǎ yǐ zi wǎng qián bān le yī diǎn er.

## grammar-091: Learned ability with 会

**review · 会 + skill/action**

Use 会 for a learned skill or knowing how to do something. Contrast 能 for ability under particular circumstances and 可以 for permission. 会 also has a separate predictive use.

1. I know how to swim.
   - 我会游泳。
   - wǒ huì yóu yǒng.
2. She knows how to drive.
   - 她会开车。
   - tā huì kāi chē.
3. Do you know how to cook Chinese food?
   - 你会做中国菜吗？
   - nǐ huì zuò zhōng guó cài ma?
4. He does not know how to ride a bicycle.
   - 他不会骑自行车。
   - tā bù huì qí zì xíng chē.
5. My younger sister can play the piano.
   - 我妹妹会弹钢琴。
   - wǒ mèi mèi huì tán gāng qín.
6. We all know how to use this software.
   - 我们都会用这个软件。
   - wǒ men dū huì yòng zhè ge ruǎn jiàn.
7. Do you know how to write this character?
   - 你会写这个字吗？
   - nǐ huì xiě zhè ge zì ma?
8. She can speak three languages.
   - 她会说三种语言。
   - tā huì shuō sān zhǒng yǔ yán.
9. I do not know how to make dumplings yet.
   - 我还不会包饺子。
   - wǒ hái bù huì bāo jiǎo zi.
10. He already knew how to play Chinese chess at five.
   - 他五岁就会下象棋了。
   - tā wǔ suì jiù huì xià xiàng qí le.

## grammar-092: Circumstantial ability with 能

**core · 能 + verb**

Use 能 for what is possible because of capacity or circumstances. It can sometimes overlap with permission, but these exercises focus on practical ability rather than knowing a learned skill.

1. I cannot attend the meeting today.
   - 我今天不能参加会议。
   - wǒ jīn tiān bù néng cān jiā huì yì.
2. This car can seat five people.
   - 这辆车能坐五个人。
   - zhè liàng chē néng zuò wǔ gè rén.
3. Can you return before six?
   - 你能在六点以前回来吗？
   - nǐ néng zài liù diǎn yǐ qián huí lái ma?
4. I can walk now.
   - 我现在能走路了。
   - wǒ xiàn zài néng zǒu lù le.
5. He can read one hundred pages in a day.
   - 他一天能读一百页。
   - tā yī tiān néng dú yī bǎi yè.
6. You can see the sea from here.
   - 这里能看到大海。
   - zhè lǐ néng kàn dào dà hǎi.
7. I cannot hear clearly; can you speak a little louder?
   - 我听不清，你能大声一点儿吗？
   - wǒ tīng bù qīng, nǐ néng dà shēng yī diǎn er ma?
8. Can we find it without a map?
   - 没有地图，我们能找到吗？
   - méi yǒu dì tú, wǒ men néng zhǎo dào ma?
9. She can come and help tomorrow.
   - 她明天能来帮忙。
   - tā míng tiān néng lái bāng máng.
10. This bag can hold many things.
   - 这个包能装很多东西。
   - zhè ge bāo néng zhuāng hěn duō dōng xī.

## grammar-093: Permission with 可以

**review · 可以 + verb**

Use 可以 to grant or ask permission. Negative permission is often 不可以 or 不能. 不用 instead means there is no need, so it does not express a prohibition.

1. May I sit here?
   - 我可以坐这里吗？
   - wǒ kě yǐ zuò zhè lǐ ma?
2. You may use my computer.
   - 你可以用我的电脑。
   - nǐ kě yǐ yòng wǒ de diàn nǎo.
3. Photography is not allowed here.
   - 这里不可以拍照。
   - zhè lǐ bù kě yǐ pāi zhào.
   - Also accepted: 这里不能拍照。
4. May we leave early?
   - 我们可以提前离开吗？
   - wǒ men kě yǐ tí qián lí kāi ma?
5. You may take the book home.
   - 你可以把书带回家。
   - nǐ kě yǐ bǎ shū dài huí jiā.
6. May I ask a question?
   - 我可以问一个问题吗？
   - wǒ kě yǐ wèn yī gè wèn tí ma?
7. Children are not allowed to go in alone.
   - 孩子不可以一个人进去。
   - hái zi bù kě yǐ yī gè rén jìn qù.
8. You may park here.
   - 你可以在这里停车。
   - nǐ kě yǐ zài zhè lǐ tíng chē.
9. You are not allowed to use a phone during the exam.
   - 考试时不可以用手机。
   - kǎo shì shí bù kě yǐ yòng shǒu jī.
10. May I try it on?
   - 我可以试穿一下吗？
   - wǒ kě yǐ shì chuān yī xià ma?

## grammar-094: Advice with 应该

**core · 应该 + verb**

Use 应该 for advice, expected duty, or a reasonable expectation. 不应该 means 'should not'. It is weaker than an explicit necessity such as 必须 in many contexts.

1. You should go to bed earlier.
   - 你应该早点睡觉。
   - nǐ yīng gāi zǎo diǎn shuì jiào.
2. We should protect the environment.
   - 我们应该保护环境。
   - wǒ men yīng gāi bǎo hù huán jìng.
3. He should apologize to you.
   - 他应该向你道歉。
   - tā yīng gāi xiàng nǐ dào qiàn.
4. You should not say that.
   - 你不应该这样说。
   - nǐ bù yīng gāi zhè yàng shuō.
5. I should call her first.
   - 我应该先给她打电话。
   - wǒ yīng gāi xiān gěi tā dǎ diàn huà.
6. Everyone should follow the rules.
   - 大家应该遵守规则。
   - dà jiā yīng gāi zūn shǒu guī zé.
7. You should drink more water.
   - 你应该多喝水。
   - nǐ yīng gāi duō hē shuǐ.
8. We should not waste time.
   - 我们不应该浪费时间。
   - wǒ men bù yīng gāi làng fèi shí jiān.
9. When problems arise, you should communicate promptly.
   - 遇到问题应该及时沟通。
   - yù dào wèn tí yīng gāi jí shí gōu tōng.
10. You should compare prices before buying things.
   - 买东西以前应该比较价格。
   - mǎi dōng xī yǐ qián yīng gāi bǐ jiào jià gé.

## grammar-095: Necessity with 必须

**core · 必须 + verb**

Use 必须 for a requirement or necessity. To say something is unnecessary, use 不必 or 不用, not an invented direct negative of 必须. Prohibitions use forms such as 不可以 or 不能.

1. You must bring your passport.
   - 你必须带护照。
   - nǐ bì xū dài hù zhào.
2. We must arrive on time.
   - 我们必须按时到。
   - wǒ men bì xū àn shí dào.
3. This matter must be resolved today.
   - 这件事必须今天解决。
   - zhè jiàn shì bì xū jīn tiān jiě jué.
4. Students must take the exam.
   - 学生必须参加考试。
   - xué shēng bì xū cān jiā kǎo shì.
5. You must wear a safety helmet to enter the factory.
   - 进入工厂必须戴安全帽。
   - jìn rù gōng chǎng bì xū dài ān quán mào.
6. I must finish the work first.
   - 我必须先完成工作。
   - wǒ bì xū xiān wán chéng gōng zuò.
7. Everyone must register.
   - 所有人都必须登记。
   - suǒ yǒu rén dōu bì xū dēng jì.
8. This document must be signed by the manager.
   - 这份文件必须由经理签字。
   - zhè fèn wén jiàn bì xū yóu jīng lǐ qiān zì.
9. You must think carefully about this issue.
   - 你必须认真考虑这个问题。
   - nǐ bì xū rèn zhēn kǎo lǜ zhè ge wèn tí.
10. We must move out before the end of the month.
   - 我们必须在月底以前搬走。
   - wǒ men bì xū zài yuè dǐ yǐ qián bān zǒu.

## grammar-096: No need with 不用

**core · 不用 + verb**

Use 不用 to say that an action is not necessary. It does not forbid the action. 不必 is a common, somewhat more formal alternative with the same no-need meaning.

1. You do not need to wait for me.
   - 你不用等我。
   - nǐ bù yòng děng wǒ.
   - Also accepted: 你不必等我。
2. There is no need to go to work today.
   - 今天不用上班。
   - jīn tiān bù yòng shàng bān.
3. You do not need to bring a gift.
   - 你不用带礼物。

4. We do not need to buy tickets in advance.
   - 我们不用提前买票。
   - wǒ men bù yòng tí qián mǎi piào.
5. There is no need to worry; I will handle it.
   - 不用担心，我会处理。
   - bù yòng dān xīn, wǒ huì chǔ lǐ.
6. You do not need to answer this question.
   - 你不用回答这个问题。
   - nǐ bù yòng huí dá zhè ge wèn tí.
7. There is no need to change shoes here.
   - 这里不用换鞋。
   - zhè lǐ bù yòng huàn xié.
8. I do not need to explain again.
   - 我不用再解释了。
   - wǒ bù yòng zài jiě shì le.
9. Children do not need to pay.
   - 孩子不用付钱。
   - hái zi bù yòng fù qián.
10. You do not need to be so formal.
   - 你不用这么客气。
   - nǐ bù yòng zhè me kè qì.

## grammar-097: Wanting with 想

**review · 想 + verb**

Use 想 before a verb to express a wish or intention, often softer than 要. 想 can also independently mean 'think' or 'miss', but these exercises focus on wanting to do something.

1. I want to learn to cook Chinese food.

   - 我不想一个人去。

   - tā xiǎng mǎi yī liàng zì xíng chē.
7. I would like to talk with you.
   - 我想跟你谈谈。
   - wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ tán tán.
8. The child wants to play for another ten minutes.
   - 孩子想再玩十分钟。

9. I want to learn about Chinese culture.
   - 我想了解中国文化。

## grammar-098: Plans with 打算

**core · 打算 + verb phrase**

Use 打算 before an action to state an intention or plan. It is a lexical verb, so it can also appear in questions about someone's plans or as a noun meaning 'plan'.

1. I plan to go to China next year.

   - 他不打算搬家。

   - 我打算每天练习半个小时。
   - wǒ dǎ suàn měi tiān liàn xí bàn gè xiǎo shí.
7. How long do you plan to stay?
   - 你打算住多久？
   - nǐ dǎ suàn zhù duō jiǔ?
8. They plan to open a cafe.

## grammar-099: Possibility with 可能

Place 可能 before the predicate for 'possibly' or 'may'. It expresses uncertainty and does not carry the learned-ability meaning of 会. It can also serve as an adjective meaning 'possible'.

5. I may arrive a little late.
   - 我可能要晚一点儿到。
   - wǒ kě néng yào wǎn yī diǎn er dào.
6. They may no longer be coming.
   - 他们可能不来了。
   - tā men kě néng bù lái le.
7. The keys may be in the car.
   - 钥匙可能在车里。
   - yào shi kě néng zài chē lǐ.
8. This approach may be useful.
   - 这个办法可能有用。
   - zhè ge bàn fǎ kě néng yǒu yòng.
9. You may have taken the wrong bag.
   - 你可能拿错包了。
   - nǐ kě néng ná cuò bāo le.
10. We may go hiking this weekend.
   - 周末我们可能去爬山。
   - zhōu mò wǒ men kě néng qù pá shān.

## grammar-100: Confident inference with 一定

**core · 一定 + predicate**

Use 一定 to express a confident conclusion or a firm commitment, depending on context. 不一定 means 'not necessarily'; 一定不 means 'definitely not', so word order changes the scope.

1. He must know the answer.

   - 你一定很累了。

   - wǒ míng tiān yī dìng lái.
4. This must be your bag.
   - 这一定是你的包。
   - zhè yī dìng shì nǐ de bāo.
5. She will definitely like this gift.
   - 她一定会喜欢这个礼物。
   - tā yī dìng huì xǐ huān zhè ge lǐ wù.
6. We can definitely find a way.
   - 我们一定能找到办法。
   - wǒ men yī dìng néng zhǎo dào bàn fǎ.
7. Expensive things are not necessarily good.
   - 贵的东西不一定好。
   - guì de dōng xī bù yī dìng hǎo.
8. He definitely will not forget.
   - 他一定不会忘记。
   - tā yī dìng bù huì wàng jì.
9. You will not necessarily have time tomorrow.
   - 你明天不一定有时间。
   - nǐ míng tiān bù yī dìng yǒu shí jiān.
10. I will definitely finish on time.
   - 我一定按时完成。
   - wǒ yī dìng àn shí wán chéng.

## grammar-101: Continuity with 一直

**core · 一直 + verb/state**

Use 一直 to describe an action or state continuing through a relevant period. It can also mean 'straight ahead' with motion, but the examples here focus on continuous duration.

1. I have been waiting for you the whole time.

5. We have always been good friends.
   - 我们一直是好朋友。
   - wǒ men yī zhí shì hǎo péng yǒu.
6. This issue has been worrying me all along.
   - 这个问题一直让我很担心。
   - zhè ge wèn tí yī zhí ràng wǒ hěn dān xīn.
7. She worked continuously until ten at night.
   - 她一直工作到晚上十点。
   - tā yī zhí gōng zuò dào wǎn shàng shí diǎn.
8. I have always wanted to learn to draw.
   - 我一直想学画画。
   - wǒ yī zhí xiǎng xué huà huà.
9. The door has been open the whole time.
   - 门一直开着。

## grammar-102: Only with 只

**core · 只 + verb/quantity phrase**

Put 只 before the predicate or a quantity phrase to limit its scope: 'only'. It is distinct from 只有…才…, which marks a necessary condition, and from the classifier 只 pronounced zhī.

1. I only drink tea.
   - 我只喝茶。

   - tā zhǐ mǎi le yī běn shū.

   - 我们只在周末见面。
   - wǒ men zhǐ zài zhōu mò jiàn miàn.

   - 他只会说一点儿中文。
   - tā zhǐ huì shuō yī diǎn er zhōng wén.
5. This ticket costs only twenty yuan.
   - 这张票只要二十块钱。
   - zhè zhāng piào zhǐ yào èr shí kuài qián.
6. I only want to rest a little.
   - 我只想休息一下。
   - wǒ zhǐ xiǎng xiū xī yī xià.
7. There is only one exit here.
   - 这里只有一个出口。
   - zhè lǐ zhǐ yǒu yī gè chū kǒu.
8. She told only me.
   - 她只告诉了我一个人。
   - tā zhǐ gào sù le wǒ yī gè rén.
9. I slept for only four hours today.
   - 我今天只睡了四个小时。
   - wǒ jīn tiān zhǐ shuì le sì gè xiǎo shí.
10. This shop only sells fruit.
   - 这家店只卖水果。
   - zhè jiā diàn zhǐ mài shuǐ guǒ.

## grammar-103: Even with 连…都/也…

**core · 连 + surprising item + 都/也 + predicate**

Use 连 to highlight an extreme or unexpected example, followed by 都 or 也 before the predicate. The statement implies that less surprising cases are included as well.

1. He even forgot his own birthday.
   - 他连自己的生日都忘了。
   - tā lián zì jǐ de shēng rì dōu wàng le.
2. Even the teacher cannot solve this question.
   - 这道题连老师都不会做。
   - zhè dào tí lián lǎo shī dōu bù huì zuò.
3. I was so busy that I did not even eat lunch.
   - 我忙得连午饭都没吃。
   - wǒ máng de lián wǔ fàn dōu méi chī.
4. She did not say even a single sentence.
   - 她连一句话都没说。
   - tā lián yī jù huà dōu méi shuō.
5. This place cannot even be found on a map.
   - 这个地方连地图上都找不到。
   - zhè ge dì fang lián dì tú shàng dōu zhǎo bù dào.
6. The child ran out without even putting on shoes.
   - 孩子连鞋都没穿就跑出去了。
   - hái zi lián xié dōu méi chuān jiù pǎo chū qù le.
7. He will not even drink water.
   - 他连水都不喝。
   - tā lián shuǐ dōu bù hē.
8. I do not even know his name.
   - 我连他的名字都不知道。
   - wǒ lián tā de míng zì dōu bù zhī dào.
9. It is warm here even in winter.
   - 这里连冬天都很暖和。
   - zhè lǐ lián dōng tiān dōu hěn nuǎn huo.
10. She has to work even on weekends.
   - 她连周末都要上班。
   - tā lián zhōu mò dōu yào shàng bān.

## grammar-104: Unexpected contrast with 却

**core · Subject + 却 + contrasting predicate**

Put 却 after the subject and before the predicate to mark a contrast with expectations. Unlike 但是, 却 is an adverb and does not normally stand before a new subject as a clause connector.

1. He is tired, yet he cannot get to sleep.
   - 他很累，却睡不着。
   - tā hěn lèi, què shuì bù zháo.
2. This shop is small, yet it has many things.
   - 这家店很小，东西却很多。
   - zhè jiā diàn hěn xiǎo, dōng xī què hěn duō.
3. I called him, but he did not answer.
   - 我给他打了电话，他却没接。
   - wǒ gěi tā dǎ le diàn huà, tā què méi jiē.
4. She is not very old, yet she has extensive experience.
   - 她年纪不大，经验却很丰富。
   - tā nián jì bù dà, jīng yàn què hěn fēng fù.
5. Everyone left, yet he stayed.
   - 大家都走了，他却留下了。
   - dà jiā dōu zǒu le, tā què liú xià le.
6. I want to help, yet I do not know what to do.
   - 我想帮忙，却不知道该做什么。
   - wǒ xiǎng bāng máng, què bù zhī dào gāi zuò shén me.
7. Although it was raining, he did not bring an umbrella.
   - 虽然下雨，他却没带伞。
   - suī rán xià yǔ, tā què méi dài sǎn.
8. This piece of clothing is expensive, yet its quality is poor.
   - 这件衣服很贵，质量却不好。
   - zhè jiàn yī fú hěn guì, zhì liàng què bù hǎo.
9. He said he was not hungry, yet he ate two bowls of rice.
   - 他说不饿，却吃了两碗饭。
   - tā shuō bù è, què chī le liǎng wǎn fàn.
10. I waited a long time, yet the bus never came.
   - 我等了很久，车却一直没来。
   - wǒ děng le hěn jiǔ, chē què yī zhí méi lái.

## grammar-105: Mistaken assumptions with 以为

**core · 以为 + assumed proposition**

Use 以为 to report a belief or assumption that turns out to be wrong or is being questioned. It often contrasts an earlier assumption with a later discovery; 觉得 is more neutral for a current opinion.

1. I thought today was Friday, but I was mistaken.
   - 我以为今天是星期五。
   - wǒ yǐ wéi jīn tiān shì xīng qī wǔ.
2. She mistakenly thought I had already left.
   - 她以为我已经走了。
   - tā yǐ wéi wǒ yǐ jīng zǒu le.
3. I thought this shop was expensive, but it is actually cheap.
   - 我以为这家店很贵，其实很便宜。
   - wǒ yǐ wéi zhè jiā diàn hěn guì, qí shí hěn pián yi.
4. He mistakenly thought the exam had been canceled.
   - 他以为考试取消了。
   - tā yǐ wéi kǎo shì qǔ xiāo le.
5. I had thought you would not come.
   - 我以为你不会来。
   - wǒ yǐ wéi nǐ bù huì lái.
6. We thought the subway would be slower, but unexpectedly it was faster.
   - 我们以为坐地铁更慢，没想到更快。
   - wǒ men yǐ wéi zuò dì tiě gèng màn, méi xiǎng dào gèng kuài.
7. She mistakenly thought that was her bag.
   - 她以为那是她的包。
   - tā yǐ wéi nà shì tā de bāo.
8. I had assumed you knew him.
   - 我以为你认识他。
   - wǒ yǐ wéi nǐ rèn shí tā.
9. He thought it would take only ten minutes, but it ended up taking an hour.
   - 他以为只要十分钟，结果花了一个小时。
   - tā yǐ wéi zhǐ yào shí fēn zhōng, jié guǒ huā le yī gè xiǎo shí.
10. I thought it was closed, but unexpectedly it was still open.
   - 我以为关门了，没想到还开着。
   - wǒ yǐ wéi guān mén le, méi xiǎng dào hái kāi zhe.

## grammar-112: Asking a matching question with 呢

After a statement or question, use a short topic followed by 呢 to ask 'What about...?' The earlier context supplies the missing information.

1. I am fine. How about you?
   - 我很好，你呢？
   - wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?

   - 我喜欢茶，你呢？
   - wǒ xǐ huān chá, nǐ ne?

   - wǒ yǒu yī zhī māo, nǐ ne?

   - 我今天很忙，明天呢？

   - wǒ bà bà shì lǎo shī, nǐ bà bà ne?

   - 我要水，你呢？
   - wǒ yào shuǐ, nǐ ne?

   - 我住北京，你呢？
   - wǒ zhù běi jīng, nǐ ne?
   - Also accepted: 我住在北京，你呢？

   - 我会游泳，你呢？
   - wǒ huì yóu yǒng, nǐ ne?
10. My older sister is at home. What about your older sister?

   - wǒ jiě jiě zài jiā, nǐ jiě jiě ne?

## grammar-126: Choosing a better alternative with 与其…不如…

**stretch · 与其 + option A，不如 + preferred option B**

Use 与其…不如… to recommend one course of action over another. The second option is presented as more worthwhile; the construction compares choices rather than simply comparing qualities.

1. Rather than complain, it is better to find a solution.
   - 与其抱怨，不如想办法。
   - yǔ qí bào yuàn, bù rú xiǎng bàn fǎ.
2. Rather than wait for him, it is better to leave first.
   - 与其等他，不如先走。
   - yǔ qí děng tā, bù rú xiān zǒu.
3. Rather than buy a new one, it is better to repair the old one.
   - 与其买新的，不如修好旧的。
   - yǔ qí mǎi xīn de, bù rú xiū hǎo jiù de.
4. Rather than speculate, it is better to ask her directly.
   - 与其猜测，不如直接问她。
   - yǔ qí cāi cè, bù rú zhí jiē wèn tā.
5. Rather than work late into the night, it is better to rest early.
   - 与其熬夜工作，不如早点休息。
   - yǔ qí áo yè gōng zuò, bù rú zǎo diǎn xiū xī.
6. Rather than pursue quantity, it is better to improve quality.
   - 与其追求数量，不如提高质量。
   - yǔ qí zhuī qiú shù liàng, bù rú tí gāo zhì liàng.
7. Rather than argue about who is right, it is better to solve the problem.
   - 与其争论谁对谁错，不如解决问题。
   - yǔ qí zhēng lùn shuí duì shuí cuò, bù rú jiě jué wèn tí.
8. Rather than save the money, it is better to use it for learning.
   - 与其把钱存起来，不如用来学习。
   - yǔ qí bǎ qián cún qǐ lái, bù rú yòng lái xué xí.
9. Rather than change the plan at the last minute, it is better to prepare in advance.
   - 与其临时改变计划，不如提前做好准备。
   - yǔ qí lín shí gǎi biàn jì huà, bù rú tí qián zuò hǎo zhǔn bèi.
10. Rather than depend on others, it is better to learn to handle it yourself.
   - 与其依赖别人，不如学会自己处理。
   - yǔ qí yī lài bié rén, bù rú xué huì zì jǐ chǔ lǐ.

## grammar-127: Choosing a sacrifice with 宁可…也不…

**stretch · 宁可 + acceptable sacrifice，也不 + rejected action**

Use 宁可…也不… to express a firm preference, even when the preferred choice has a cost. The speaker accepts the first situation rather than doing the second action.

1. I would rather walk than ride in his car.
   - 我宁可走路，也不坐他的车。
   - wǒ nìng kě zǒu lù, yě bù zuò tā de chē.
2. She would rather earn less than give up her principles.
   - 她宁可少挣钱，也不放弃自己的原则。
   - tā nìng kě shǎo zhèng qián, yě bù fàng qì zì jǐ de yuán zé.
3. I would rather arrive late than drive fast.
   - 我宁可晚点到，也不开快车。
   - wǒ nìng kě wǎn diǎn dào, yě bù kāi kuài chē.
4. He would rather suffer a loss himself than deceive a friend.
   - 他宁可自己吃亏，也不欺骗朋友。
   - tā nìng kě zì jǐ chī kuī, yě bù qī piàn péng yǒu.
5. We would rather start over than accept such conditions.
   - 我们宁可重新开始，也不接受这样的条件。
   - wǒ men nìng kě chóng xīn kāi shǐ, yě bù jiē shòu zhè yàng de tiáo jiàn.
6. She would rather finish it alone than submit substandard work.
   - 她宁可一个人完成，也不交出不合格的作品。
   - tā nìng kě yī gè rén wán chéng, yě bù jiāo chū bù hé gé de zuò pǐn.
7. I would rather spend a little more than buy counterfeit goods.
   - 我宁可多花一点钱，也不买假货。
   - wǒ nìng kě duō huā yī diǎn qián, yě bù mǎi jiǎ huò.
8. He would rather admit his mistake than make excuses.
   - 他宁可承认错误，也不找借口。
   - tā nìng kě chéng rèn cuò wù, yě bù zhǎo jiè kǒu.
9. I would rather be unemployed temporarily than do something illegal.
   - 我宁可暂时失业，也不做违法的事。
   - wǒ nìng kě zàn shí shī yè, yě bù zuò wéi fǎ de shì.
10. They would rather delay opening than lower safety standards.
   - 他们宁可推迟开业，也不降低安全标准。
   - tā men nìng kě tuī chí kāi yè, yě bù jiàng dī ān quán biāo zhǔn.

## grammar-128: Reasoning from an accepted fact with 既然…就…

**stretch · 既然 + accepted fact，就 + conclusion**

既然 introduces a fact the speaker treats as established. 就 leads to a decision, suggestion, or conclusion based on that fact, rather than a hypothetical condition.

1. Since you have already decided, do not hesitate.
   - 既然你已经决定了，就不要犹豫。
   - jì rán nǐ yǐ jīng jué dìng le, jiù bù yào yóu yù.
2. Since everyone is here, let us begin.
   - 既然大家都到了，就开始吧。
   - jì rán dà jiā dōu dào le, jiù kāi shǐ ba.
3. Since you promised, you must follow through.
   - 既然答应了，就要做到。
   - jì rán dā yìng le, jiù yào zuò dào.
4. Since there is not enough time, let us discuss the main points first.
   - 既然时间不够，就先讨论重点。
   - jì rán shí jiān bù gòu, jiù xiān tǎo lùn zhòng diǎn.
5. Since he is unwilling, let us not pressure him.
   - 既然他不愿意，我们就别勉强他。
   - jì rán tā bù yuàn yì, wǒ men jiù bié miǎn qiǎng tā.
6. Since the problem has been discovered, it should be dealt with promptly.
   - 既然发现了问题，就应该及时处理。

8. Since this road is blocked, let us take another one.
   - 既然这条路不通，我们就换一条。
   - jì rán zhè tiáo lù bù tōng, wǒ men jiù huàn yī tiáo.
9. Since the documents are ready, we can apply now.

## grammar-129: Acknowledging a fact with 尽管…仍然…

**stretch · 尽管 + fact，subject + 仍然 + contrasting result**

尽管 introduces an actual circumstance that could have prevented the result. 仍然 means 'still' and highlights that the result holds despite it. This use of 尽管 means 'although', not the separate inviting sense 'feel free to'.

1. Although she is tired, she still keeps training.
   - 尽管很累，她仍然坚持训练。
   - jǐn guǎn hěn lèi, tā réng rán jiān chí xùn liàn.
2. Although prices have risen, demand is still high.
   - 尽管价格上涨，需求仍然很大。
   - jǐn guǎn jià gé shàng zhǎng, xū qiú réng rán hěn dà.
3. Although everyone objected, he still did not change his mind.
   - 尽管大家反对，他仍然没有改变主意。
   - jǐn guǎn dà jiā fǎn duì, tā réng rán méi yǒu gǎi biàn zhǔ yì.
4. Although it is raining heavily, the competition is still proceeding as usual.
   - 尽管下着大雨，比赛仍然照常进行。
   - jǐn guǎn xià zhe dà yǔ, bǐ sài réng rán zhào cháng jìn xíng.
5. Although he has apologized, they still refuse to forgive him.
   - 尽管已经道歉，他们仍然不肯原谅他。
   - jǐn guǎn yǐ jīng dào qiàn, tā men réng rán bù kěn yuán liàng tā.
6. Although resources were limited, we still completed the task.
   - 尽管条件有限，我们仍然完成了任务。
   - jǐn guǎn tiáo jiàn yǒu xiàn, wǒ men réng rán wán chéng le rèn wù.
7. Although she has been away for years, she still remembers that little path.
   - 尽管离开多年，她仍然记得那条小路。
   - jǐn guǎn lí kāi duō nián, tā réng rán jì de nà tiáo xiǎo lù.
8. Although there is new evidence, the conclusion still has not changed.
   - 尽管有了新证据，结论仍然没有改变。
   - jǐn guǎn yǒu le xīn zhèng jù, jié lùn réng rán méi yǒu gǎi biàn.
9. Although his income is not high, he still donates every month.
   - 尽管收入不高，他仍然每月捐款。
   - jǐn guǎn shōu rù bù gāo, tā réng rán měi yuè juān kuǎn.
10. Although she has failed several times, she still believes in herself.
   - 尽管失败过几次，她仍然相信自己。
   - jǐn guǎn shī bài guò jǐ cì, tā réng rán xiāng xìn zì jǐ.

## grammar-130: Accepting an extreme possibility with 哪怕…也…

**stretch · 哪怕 + extreme condition，也 + unchanged response**

哪怕 introduces even an extreme or minimal hypothetical case. The main clause says what will still hold under that condition; it often expresses determination or the value of even a small amount.

1. Even if only one person supports me, I will still try.
   - 哪怕只有一个人支持，我也要试试。
   - nǎ pà zhǐ yǒu yī gè rén zhī chí, wǒ yě yào shì shì.

   - 哪怕下雪，我们也按时出发。

3. Even if you are extremely busy, you still need to eat.
   - 哪怕再忙，你也要吃饭。
   - nǎ pà zài máng, nǐ yě yào chī fàn.
4. Even if I can only help a little, I am willing.
   - 哪怕只能帮一点忙，我也愿意。
   - nǎ pà zhǐ néng bāng yī diǎn máng, wǒ yě yuàn yì.
5. Even if I fail, I will not regret it.
   - 哪怕失败，我也不会后悔。
   - nǎ pà shī bài, wǒ yě bù huì hòu huǐ.
6. Even if he comes in person, I will not agree.
   - 哪怕他亲自来，我也不会答应。
   - nǎ pà tā qīn zì lái, wǒ yě bù huì dā yìng.
7. Even if only one day remains, we must not give up.
   - 哪怕只剩一天，我们也不能放弃。
   - nǎ pà zhǐ shèng yī tiān, wǒ men yě bù néng fàng qì.
8. Even if it costs a little more, I still want to buy something of good quality.
   - 哪怕贵一点，我也想买质量好的。
   - nǎ pà guì yī diǎn, wǒ yě xiǎng mǎi zhì liàng hǎo de.
9. Even if nobody finds out, you should still tell the truth.
   - 哪怕没有人发现，你也应该说实话。
   - nǎ pà méi yǒu rén fā xiàn, nǐ yě yīng gāi shuō shí huà.
10. Even practicing for just ten minutes a day will lead to progress.
   - 哪怕每天只练十分钟，也会有进步。
   - nǎ pà měi tiān zhǐ liàn shí fēn zhōng, yě huì yǒu jìn bù.

## grammar-131: Stating an exception with 除非…否则…

**stretch · 除非 + necessary exception，否则 + default outcome**

除非 sets out the exception needed to avoid the outcome after 否则. The structure means 'unless...otherwise...' and does not promise that the exception alone guarantees success.

1. Unless it rains, we will leave tomorrow.
   - 除非下雨，否则我们明天出发。
   - chú fēi xià yǔ, fǒu zé wǒ men míng tiān chū fā.
2. Unless you explain in person, he will not believe it.
   - 除非你亲自解释，否则他不会相信。
   - chú fēi nǐ qīn zì jiě shì, fǒu zé tā bù huì xiāng xìn.
3. Unless the price drops, I will not consider buying it.
   - 除非价格降低，否则我不考虑购买。
   - chú fēi jià gé jiàng dī, fǒu zé wǒ bù kǎo lǜ gòu mǎi.
4. Unless you book in advance, you cannot visit.
   - 除非提前预约，否则不能参观。
   - chú fēi tí qián yù yuē, fǒu zé bù néng cān guān.
5. Unless there is new evidence, the conclusion cannot be changed.
   - 除非有新的证据，否则不能改变结论。
   - chú fēi yǒu xīn de zhèng jù, fǒu zé bù néng gǎi biàn jié lùn.
6. Unless everyone agrees, the plan will remain unchanged.
   - 除非大家都同意，否则计划不变。
   - chú fēi dà jiā dōu tóng yì, fǒu zé jì huà bù biàn.
7. Unless you are willing to work overtime, it cannot be finished today.
   - 除非你愿意加班，否则今天做不完。
   - chú fēi nǐ yuàn yì jiā bān, fǒu zé jīn tiān zuò bù wán.
8. Unless the situation is urgent, do not call this number.
   - 除非情况紧急，否则不要打这个电话。
   - chú fēi qíng kuàng jǐn jí, fǒu zé bù yào dǎ zhè ge diàn huà.
9. Unless you obtain permission, you cannot take photos.
   - 除非得到允许，否则不能拍照。
   - chú fēi dé dào yǔn xǔ, fǒu zé bù néng pāi zhào.
10. Unless he apologizes, I will not see him again.
   - 除非他道歉，否则我不会再见他。
   - chú fēi tā dào qiàn, fǒu zé wǒ bù huì zài jiàn tā.

## grammar-132: Covering every alternative with 无论…都…

**stretch · 无论 + question phrase/alternatives，都 + constant result**

无论 means 'regardless of' and introduces a range of possibilities, usually through a question word or alternatives with 还是. 都 marks the result that stays the same across those possibilities; 无论 is common in formal speech and writing.

1. Whatever the result is, I will accept it.
   - 无论结果如何，我都接受。
   - wú lùn jié guǒ rú hé, wǒ dōu jiē shòu.
2. Whoever comes must register.
   - 无论谁来，都要登记。

3. Wherever you are, I will support you.
   - 无论你在哪里，我都会支持你。
   - wú lùn nǐ zài nǎ lǐ, wǒ dōu huì zhī chí nǐ.
4. No matter how cold it is, he keeps running.
   - 无论天气多冷，他都坚持跑步。
   - wú lùn tiān qì duō lěng, tā dōu jiān chí pǎo bù.
5. Whether we succeed or fail, we can learn something.
   - 无论成功还是失败，我们都能学到东西。
   - wú lùn chéng gōng hái shì shī bài, wǒ men dōu néng xué dào dōng xī.
6. Whatever happens, do not panic.
   - 无论发生什么，都不要惊慌。
   - wú lùn fā shēng shén me, dōu bù yào jīng huāng.
7. Whichever method you use, you must ensure safety.
   - 无论用哪种方法，都要保证安全。
   - wú lùn yòng nǎ zhǒng fāng fǎ, dōu yào bǎo zhèng ān quán.
8. No matter how much she earns, she saves a portion.
   - 无论收入多少，她都会存一部分。
   - wú lùn shōu rù duō shǎo, tā dōu huì cún yī bù fèn.
9. Teachers and students alike should follow the rules.
   - 无论是老师还是学生，都应该遵守规定。
   - wú lùn shì lǎo shī hái shì xué shēng, dōu yīng gāi zūn shǒu guī dìng.
10. Whenever you need help, you can contact me.
   - 无论你什么时候需要帮助，都可以联系我。
   - wú lùn nǐ shén me shí hòu xū yào bāng zhù, dōu kě yǐ lián xì wǒ.

## grammar-133: Arguing from the stronger case with 尚且…更何况…

**stretch · Stronger case + 尚且 + difficulty，更何况 + weaker case**

尚且 means 'even' in a relatively formal argument. If even the first person or situation faces a difficulty, 更何况 implies that the second faces it even more. The second clause often leaves the shared predicate unstated.

1. Even experts make mistakes, let alone beginners.
   - 专家尚且会犯错，更何况初学者。
   - zhuān jiā shàng qiě huì fàn cuò, gèng hé kuàng chū xué zhě.
2. Even adults cannot stand it, let alone children.
   - 大人尚且受不了，更何况孩子。
   - dà rén shàng qiě shòu bù liǎo, gèng hé kuàng hái zi.
3. Even locals can get lost, let alone tourists.
   - 本地人尚且会迷路，更何况游客。
   - běn dì rén shàng qiě huì mí lù, gèng hé kuàng yóu kè.
4. Even he does not dare to go, let alone me.
   - 他尚且不敢去，更何况我。
   - tā shàng qiě bù gǎn qù, gèng hé kuàng wǒ.
5. He cannot even do such a simple question, let alone a difficult one.
   - 这么简单的题他尚且不会，更何况难题。
   - zhè me jiǎn dān de tí tā shàng qiě bù huì, gèng hé kuàng nán tí.
6. Even walking is difficult, let alone running.
   - 走路尚且困难，更何况跑步。
   - zǒu lù shàng qiě kùn nán, gèng hé kuàng pǎo bù.
7. Even native speakers need to consult a dictionary, let alone foreign students.
   - 母语者尚且需要查词典，更何况外国学生。
   - mǔ yǔ zhě shàng qiě xū yào chá cí diǎn, gèng hé kuàng wài guó xué shēng.
8. Even managing alone is too much, let alone also having to care for children.
   - 一个人尚且忙不过来，更何况还要照顾孩子。
   - yī gè rén shàng qiě máng bù guò lái, gèng hé kuàng hái yào zhào gù hái zi.
9. Even on a clear day it is hard to see clearly, let alone on a foggy day.
   - 晴天尚且看不清，更何况雾天。
   - qíng tiān shàng qiě kàn bù qīng, gèng hé kuàng wù tiān.
10. Even maintaining the current situation is not easy, let alone expanding.
   - 维持现状尚且不容易，更何况扩大规模。
   - wéi chí xiàn zhuàng shàng qiě bù róng yì, gèng hé kuàng kuò dà guī mó.

## grammar-134: Reversing an expected outcome with 不仅没有…反而…

**stretch · 不仅没有 + expected event，反而 + opposite result**

Use 不仅没有…反而… when the expected improvement or event did not happen and the opposite happened instead. This is stronger than merely adding a second fact with 不但…而且….

1. The price not only failed to fall; it rose instead.
   - 价格不仅没有下降，反而上涨了。
   - jià gé bù jǐn méi yǒu xià jiàng, fǎn ér shàng zhǎng le.
2. He not only did not get angry; he laughed instead.
   - 他不仅没有生气，反而笑了。
   - tā bù jǐn méi yǒu shēng qì, fǎn ér xiào le.
3. After resting, I not only failed to feel refreshed; I felt sleepier instead.
   - 休息以后，我不仅没有精神，反而更困了。
   - xiū xī yǐ hòu, wǒ bù jǐn méi yǒu jīng shén, fǎn ér gèng kùn le.
4. This explanation not only failed to clear up the misunderstanding; it sparked an argument instead.
   - 这次解释不仅没有消除误会，反而引起了争论。
   - zhè cì jiě shì bù jǐn méi yǒu xiāo chú wù huì, fǎn ér yǐn qǐ le zhēng lùn.
5. The number of people not only failed to decrease; it increased instead.
   - 人数不仅没有减少，反而增加了。
   - rén shù bù jǐn méi yǒu jiǎn shǎo, fǎn ér zēng jiā le.
6. She not only did not give up; she worked even harder instead.
   - 她不仅没有放弃，反而更加努力了。
   - tā bù jǐn méi yǒu fàng qì, fǎn ér gèng jiā nǔ lì le.
7. The new rule not only failed to save time; it increased the workload instead.
   - 新规定不仅没有节省时间，反而增加了工作量。
   - xīn guī dìng bù jǐn méi yǒu jié shěng shí jiān, fǎn ér zēng jiā le gōng zuò liàng.
8. The rain not only failed to stop; it grew heavier instead.
   - 雨不仅没有停，反而越下越大。
   - yǔ bù jǐn méi yǒu tíng, fǎn ér yuè xià yuè dà.
9. He not only failed to thank me; he blamed me instead.
   - 他不仅没有感谢我，反而责怪我。
   - tā bù jǐn méi yǒu gǎn xiè wǒ, fǎn ér zé guài wǒ.
10. After the move, the journey not only failed to get shorter; it became longer instead.
   - 搬家后，路程不仅没有缩短，反而变长了。
   - bān jiā hòu, lù chéng bù jǐn méi yǒu suō duǎn, fǎn ér biàn cháng le.

## grammar-135: Correcting a characterization with 不是…而是…

   - tā bù shì lǎo shī, ér shì fān yì.
4. The issue is not the price, but the quality.
   - 问题不是价格，而是质量。
   - wèn tí bù shì jià gé, ér shì zhì liàng.
5. She did not forget; she deliberately said nothing.
   - 她不是忘了，而是故意没说。
   - tā bù shì wàng le, ér shì gù yì méi shuō.
6. What we need is not excuses, but action.
   - 我们需要的不是借口，而是行动。
   - wǒ men xū yào de bù shì jiè kǒu, ér shì xíng dòng.
7. This is not luck, but the result of sustained effort.
   - 这不是运气，而是长期努力的结果。
   - zhè bù shì yùn qì, ér shì cháng qī nǔ lì de jié guǒ.
8. What worries me is not failure, but losing trust.

   - wǒ dān xīn de bù shì shī bài, ér shì shī qù xìn rèn.

   - tā lái zhè lǐ bù shì wèi le lǚ yóu, ér shì wèi le gōng zuò.
10. What determines success or failure is not speed, but direction.

   - jué dìng chéng bài de bù shì sù dù, ér shì fāng xiàng.

## grammar-136: Offering mutually exclusive choices with 要么…要么…

**stretch · 要么 + choice A，要么 + choice B**

要么…要么… presents alternatives to choose between, often with the implication that one must be selected. Unlike 还是 in a direct choice question, this construction commonly states the available choices.

1. We can either take a train or fly.
   - 我们要么坐火车，要么坐飞机。
   - wǒ men yào me zuò huǒ chē, yào me zuò fēi jī.
2. Either decide now or give up this opportunity.
   - 你要么现在决定，要么放弃这次机会。
   - nǐ yào me xiàn zài jué dìng, yào me fàng qì zhè cì jī huì.
3. Either you come or I go.
   - 要么你来，要么我去。
   - yào me nǐ lái, yào me wǒ qù.
4. On weekends I either read or go out for a walk.
   - 周末我要么看书，要么出去散步。
   - zhōu mò wǒ yào me kàn shū, yào me chū qù sàn bù.
5. This room can be either a study or a guest room.
   - 这个房间要么做书房，要么做客房。
   - zhè ge fáng jiān yào me zuò shū fáng, yào me zuò kè fáng.
6. We must either reduce spending or increase income.
   - 我们要么减少开支，要么增加收入。
   - wǒ men yào me jiǎn shǎo kāi zhī, yào me zēng jiā shōu rù.
7. Either accept the conditions or withdraw from the partnership.
   - 你要么接受条件，要么退出合作。
   - nǐ yào me jiē shòu tiáo jiàn, yào me tuì chū hé zuò.
8. Either he did not hear it or he did not understand it.
   - 他要么没听见，要么没听懂。
   - tā yào me méi tīng jiàn, yào me méi tīng dǒng.
9. For lunch, we can either eat at home or at a nearby restaurant.
   - 午饭要么在家吃，要么去附近的饭店吃。
   - wǔ fàn yào me zài jiā chī, yào me qù fù jìn de fàn diàn chī.
10. These books can either be donated or kept for us to read.
   - 这些书要么捐出去，要么留下自己看。
   - zhè xiē shū yào me juān chū qù, yào me liú xià zì jǐ kàn.

## grammar-137: Describing recurring alternatives with 不是…就是…

**stretch · 不是 + possibility A，就是 + possibility B**

不是…就是… says that one of two possibilities holds. It often describes a repeated pattern or a deduction, rather than inviting the listener to choose an option.

1. On weekends he is either working overtime or away on business.
   - 他周末不是加班，就是出差。
   - tā zhōu mò bù shì jiā bān, jiù shì chū chāi.
2. She is either in the library or in the laboratory.
   - 她不是在图书馆，就是在实验室。
   - tā bù shì zài tú shū guǎn, jiù shì zài shí yàn shì.
3. Recently it has been either raining or windy.
   - 最近不是下雨，就是刮风。
   - zuì jìn bù shì xià yǔ, jiù shì guā fēng.
4. Whenever he speaks, he is either complaining or criticizing.
   - 他一开口不是抱怨，就是批评。
   - tā yī kāi kǒu bù shì bào yuàn, jiù shì pī píng.
5. This key is either yours or his.
   - 这把钥匙不是你的，就是他的。
   - zhè bǎ yào shi bù shì nǐ de, jiù shì tā de.

## grammar-142: Highlighting a cause with 之所以…是因为…

**stretch · Subject + 之所以 + result，是因为 + cause**

之所以 marks the result that needs explanation, and 是因为 introduces its cause. This construction foregrounds why something is true; do not put the cause immediately after 之所以.

1. The reason I was late is that traffic was heavy.
   - 我之所以迟到，是因为路上堵车。
   - wǒ zhī suǒ yǐ chí dào, shì yīn wèi lù shàng dǔ chē.

   - 她之所以辞职，是因为想继续学习。

   - 这家店之所以受欢迎，是因为服务很好。
   - zhè jiā diàn zhī suǒ yǐ shòu huān yíng, shì yīn wèi fú wù hěn hǎo.
4. The reason he is silent is that he does not know what to say.
   - 他之所以沉默，是因为不知道该说什么。
   - tā zhī suǒ yǐ chén mò, shì yīn wèi bù zhī dào gāi shuō shén me.
5. The reason we changed the plan is that the weather was bad.
   - 我们之所以改变计划，是因为天气不好。
   - wǒ men zhī suǒ yǐ gǎi biàn jì huà, shì yīn wèi tiān qì bù hǎo.
6. The reason I object is that the risk is too great.
   - 我之所以反对，是因为风险太大。
   - wǒ zhī suǒ yǐ fǎn duì, shì yīn wèi fēng xiǎn tài dà.
7. The reason she improves quickly is that she practices every day.
   - 她之所以进步快，是因为每天练习。
   - tā zhī suǒ yǐ jìn bù kuài, shì yīn wèi měi tiān liàn xí.
8. The reason this issue is complicated is that it involves many departments.
   - 这个问题之所以复杂，是因为涉及很多部门。
   - zhè ge wèn tí zhī suǒ yǐ fù zá, shì yīn wèi shè jí hěn duō bù mén.
9. The reason he came back is that he could not stop worrying about his family.
   - 他之所以回来，是因为放心不下家人。
   - tā zhī suǒ yǐ huí lái, shì yīn wèi fàng xīn bù xià jiā rén.
10. The reason we succeeded is that everyone trusted one another.
   - 我们之所以成功，是因为大家互相信任。
   - wǒ men zhī suǒ yǐ chéng gōng, shì yīn wèi dà jiā hù xiāng xìn rèn.

## grammar-143: Applying a rule universally with 凡是…都…

**stretch · 凡是 + qualifying group，都 + shared rule**

   - 凡是符合条件的申请者，都可以参加面试。
   - fán shì fú hé tiáo jiàn de shēn qǐng zhě, dōu kě yǐ cān jiā miàn shì.
6. Every borrowed book must be returned on time.
   - 凡是借走的书，都要按时归还。

8. You should do your best to fulfill every promise you make to others.

   - fán shì xū yào qiān zì de wén jiàn, dōu fàng zài zhè lǐ.
10. Anyone who finds an error can suggest a correction.
   - 凡是发现错误的人，都可以提出修改意见。
   - fán shì fā xiàn cuò wù de rén, dōu kě yǐ tí chū xiū gǎi yì jiàn.

## grammar-144: Remaining unmoved with 任凭…也…

   - rèn píng wài miàn duō me chǎo nào, tā yě néng zhuān xīn kàn shū.

## grammar-146: Describing parallel development with 随着

8. As the investigation progressed, more details were discovered.
   - 随着调查深入，更多细节被发现了。
   - suí zhe diào chá shēn rù, gèng duō xì jié bèi fā xiàn le.
9. As transportation improves, more and more tourists come here.
   - 随着交通改善，来这里的游客越来越多。
   - suí zhe jiāo tōng gǎi shàn, lái zhè lǐ de yóu kè yuè lái yuè duō.
10. As the discussion continues, everyone's views gradually converge.
   - 随着讨论继续，大家的意见逐渐接近。
   - suí zhe tǎo lùn jì xù, dà jiā de yì jiàn zhú jiàn jiē jìn.

## grammar-147: Tracing a continuing situation with 自从…以来…

**stretch · 自从 + starting event + 以来，continuing development**

自从 identifies the starting point of a situation that continues or has effects afterward. 以来 makes the span from that point explicit; the main clause often includes 一直, 再也没, or a description of change.

1. Ever since moving, I have been taking the subway to work.
   - 自从搬家以来，我一直坐地铁上班。
   - zì cóng bān jiā yǐ lái, wǒ yī zhí zuò dì tiě shàng bān.
2. Since meeting her, I have learned a lot.
   - 自从认识她以来，我学到了很多。
   - zì cóng rèn shí tā yǐ lái, wǒ xué dào le hěn duō.
3. Since graduating, we have met only twice.
   - 自从毕业以来，我们只见过两次面。
   - zì cóng bì yè yǐ lái, wǒ men zhǐ jiàn guò liǎng cì miàn.
4. Since changing jobs, he has rarely worked overtime.
   - 自从换了工作以来，他很少加班。
   - zì cóng huàn le gōng zuò yǐ lái, tā hěn shǎo jiā bān.
5. Since that argument, they have never contacted each other again.
   - 自从那次争论以来，他们再也没联系过。
   - zì cóng nà cì zhēng lùn yǐ lái, tā men zài yě méi lián xì guò.
6. Since she started running, her sleep has improved greatly.
   - 自从开始跑步以来，她的睡眠好多了。
   - zì cóng kāi shǐ pǎo bù yǐ lái, tā de shuì mián hǎo duō le.
7. Ever since this shop opened, business has been good.
   - 自从这家店开业以来，生意一直很好。
   - zì cóng zhè jiā diàn kāi yè yǐ lái, shēng yì yī zhí hěn hǎo.
8. Since the child was born, our lives have changed greatly.
   - 自从孩子出生以来，我们的生活变化很大。
   - zì cóng hái zi chū shēng yǐ lái, wǒ men de shēng huó biàn huà hěn dà.
9. Since the new rule was implemented, accidents have decreased.
   - 自从实行新规定以来，事故减少了。
   - zì cóng shí xíng xīn guī dìng yǐ lái, shì gù jiǎn shǎo le.
10. Ever since coming here, I have been busy every day.
   - 自从来到这里以来，我每天都很忙。
   - zì cóng lái dào zhè lǐ yǐ lái, wǒ měi tiān dōu hěn máng.

## grammar-148: Marking a delayed endpoint with 直到…才…

**stretch · 直到 + time/event，subject + 才 + delayed action**

直到…才… means that an action did not occur until the stated time or event. 才 goes before the delayed action and highlights how late it happened.

1. He did not come home until midnight.
   - 直到半夜，他才回家。
   - zhí dào bàn yè, tā cái huí jiā.
2. I did not understand until the teacher explained.
   - 直到老师解释，我才明白。
   - zhí dào lǎo shī jiě shì, wǒ cái míng bái.
3. She did not submit her application until the last day.
   - 直到最后一天，她才提交申请。
   - zhí dào zuì hòu yī tiān, tā cái tí jiāo shēn qǐng.
4. We did not leave until the rain stopped.
   - 直到雨停了，我们才出发。
   - zhí dào yǔ tíng le, wǒ men cái chū fā.
5. I did not believe it until I saw the actual object.
   - 直到见到实物，我才相信。
   - zhí dào jiàn dào shí wù, wǒ cái xiāng xìn.
6. Mom did not start working until the child fell asleep.
   - 直到孩子睡着，妈妈才开始工作。
   - zhí dào hái zi shuì zháo, mā mā cái kāi shǐ gōng zuò.
7. He did not learn the meeting was canceled until he received the notice.
   - 直到收到通知，他才知道会议取消了。
   - zhí dào shōu dào tōng zhī, tā cái zhī dào huì yì qǔ xiāo le.
8. I did not appreciate the importance of home until I left my hometown.
   - 直到离开家乡，我才体会到家的重要。
   - zhí dào lí kāi jiā xiāng, wǒ cái tǐ huì dào jiā de zhòng yào.
9. She did not feel reassured until she had checked three times.
   - 直到检查了三遍，她才放心。
   - zhí dào jiǎn chá le sān biàn, tā cái fàng xīn.
10. Nobody breathed a sigh of relief until the problem was solved.
   - 直到问题解决，大家才松了一口气。
   - zhí dào wèn tí jiě jué, dà jiā cái sōng le yī kǒu qì.

## grammar-149: Denying an extreme outcome with 不至于

**stretch · Situation + 不至于 + extreme result**

不至于 says that something will not reach a particular, usually undesirable, degree. It can reassure someone or suggest that a reaction is excessive; it does not simply mean that an event is impossible.

1. It is only a five-minute delay; we will not miss the entire performance.
   - 只是晚了五分钟，不至于错过整场演出。
   - zhǐ shì wǎn le wǔ fēn zhōng, bù zhì yú cuò guò zhěng chǎng yǎn chū.
2. He is a little angry, but not to the point of resigning.
   - 他有点生气，但不至于辞职。
   - tā yǒu diǎn shēng qì, dàn bù zhì yú cí zhí.
3. Wear an extra layer, and you will not be so cold.
   - 多穿一件衣服，就不至于那么冷。
   - duō chuān yī jiàn yī fú, jiù bù zhì yú nà me lěng.
4. Make a plan in advance, and you will not end up in a fluster.
   - 提前做个计划，就不至于手忙脚乱。
   - tí qián zuò gè jì huà, jiù bù zhì yú shǒu máng jiǎo luàn.
5. This small difficulty will not drive us to give up.
   - 这点困难不至于让我们放弃。
   - zhè diǎn kùn nán bù zhì yú ràng wǒ men fàng qì.
6. Earning a little less will not go so far as to affect our daily lives.
   - 少赚一点钱，也不至于影响生活。
   - shǎo zhuàn yī diǎn qián, yě bù zhì yú yǐng xiǎng shēng huó.
7. Bring some food, and you will not go hungry on the way.
   - 带点吃的，路上就不至于饿肚子。
   - dài diǎn chī de, lù shàng jiù bù zhì yú è dù zi.
8. Although he is forgetful, he is not so forgetful as to forget his own birthday.
   - 他虽然健忘，但不至于忘记自己的生日。
   - tā suī rán jiàn wàng, dàn bù zhì yú wàng jì zì jǐ de shēng rì.
9. We disagree, but not enough to argue over it.
   - 我们意见不同，但不至于为此吵架。
   - wǒ men yì jiàn bù tóng, dàn bù zhì yú wèi cǐ chǎo jià.
10. With these savings, we will not fall into hardship for the time being.
   - 有这笔存款，我们暂时不至于陷入困境。
   - yǒu zhè bǐ cún kuǎn, wǒ men zàn shí bù zhì yú xiàn rù kùn jìng.

## grammar-150: Challenging certainty with 未必

**stretch · Subject + 未必 + predicate**

未必 means 'not necessarily'. It questions whether a conclusion must be true, rather than asserting the opposite. It commonly challenges assumptions based on price, appearance, or status.

1. Expensive things are not necessarily good.
   - 贵的东西未必好。
   - guì de dōng xī wèi bì hǎo.
2. He does not necessarily know about this matter.
   - 他未必知道这件事。
   - tā wèi bì zhī dào zhè jiàn shì.
3. Having more people does not necessarily mean greater efficiency.
   - 人多未必效率高。
   - rén duō wèi bì xiào lǜ gāo.
4. Leaving now will not necessarily get us there in time.
   - 现在出发未必来得及。
   - xiàn zài chū fā wèi bì lái de jí.
5. Her silence does not necessarily mean she disagrees.
   - 她不说话，未必是不同意。
   - tā bù shuō huà, wèi bì shì bù tóng yì.
6. An experienced person is not necessarily suited to this position.
   - 有经验的人未必适合这个职位。
   - yǒu jīng yàn de rén wèi bì shì hé zhè ge zhí wèi.
7. Something that looks easy is not necessarily easy to do.
   - 看起来容易的事，做起来未必容易。
   - kàn qǐ lái róng yì de shì, zuò qǐ lái wèi bì róng yì.
8. Moving somewhere else will not necessarily solve the problem.
   - 换个地方未必能解决问题。
   - huàn gè dì fang wèi bì néng jiě jué wèn tí.
9. You will not necessarily like a book that everyone else likes.
   - 大家都喜欢的书，你未必喜欢。
   - dà jiā dōu xǐ huān de shū, nǐ wèi bì xǐ huān.
10. This failure is not necessarily a bad thing.
   - 这次失败未必是坏事。
   - zhè cì shī bài wèi bì shì huài shì.

## grammar-151: Acknowledging an unavoidable tendency with 难免

**stretch · Subject/situation + 难免 + unwanted event**

难免 means that something is hard to avoid and often understandable under the circumstances. It usually introduces an unwelcome feeling, mistake, or difficulty without blaming the person harshly.

1. It is hard not to feel nervous the first time you go on stage.
   - 第一次上台，难免紧张。
   - dì yī cì shàng tái, nán miǎn jǐn zhāng.
2. When work is too busy, mistakes are hard to avoid.
   - 工作太忙，难免出错。
   - gōng zuò tài máng, nán miǎn chū cuò.
3. After being away from home for a long time, homesickness is hard to avoid.
   - 离开家久了，难免想家。
   - lí kāi jiā jiǔ le, nán miǎn xiǎng jiā.
4. When people live together, conflicts are hard to avoid.
   - 大家一起生活，难免有矛盾。
   - dà jiā yī qǐ shēng huó, nán miǎn yǒu máo dùn.
5. When you have just changed jobs, some difficulty adjusting is hard to avoid.
   - 刚换工作，难免有些不适应。
   - gāng huàn gōng zuò, nán miǎn yǒu xiē bù shì yìng.
6. Hearing such news, he can hardly avoid feeling disappointed.
   - 听到这样的消息，他难免失望。
   - tīng dào zhè yàng de xiāo xī, tā nán miǎn shī wàng.
7. No matter how thoroughly you prepare, unexpected events are hard to avoid.
   - 准备得再充分，也难免遇到意外。
   - zhǔn bèi de zài chōng fèn, yě nán miǎn yù dào yì wài.
8. When learning a new language, saying things incorrectly is hard to avoid.
   - 学一门新语言，难免说错话。
   - xué yī mén xīn yǔ yán, nán miǎn shuō cuò huà.
9. When driving for a long time, fatigue is hard to avoid.
   - 长时间开车，难免疲劳。
   - cháng shí jiān kāi chē, nán miǎn pí láo.
10. Without understanding the situation, misunderstandings are hard to avoid.
   - 对情况不了解，难免产生误会。
   - duì qíng kuàng bù liǎo jiě, nán miǎn chǎn shēng wù huì.

## grammar-152: Reducing an explanation with 无非

**stretch · Subject + 无非 + 是/想/为了 + limited explanation**

无非 means 'nothing more than' and narrows the possibilities or motives to something the speaker considers simple. It can sound dismissive, so its tone depends on context.

1. He simply wants recognition.
   - 他无非是想得到认可。
   - tā wú fēi shì xiǎng dé dào rèn kě.
2. The options are nothing more than these two methods.
   - 办法无非是这两种。
   - bàn fǎ wú fēi shì zhè liǎng zhǒng.
3. She is doing this simply to protect her family.
   - 她这么做，无非是为了保护家人。
   - tā zhè me zuò, wú fēi shì wèi le bǎo hù jiā rén.
4. I am merely making a suggestion.
   - 我无非是提个建议。
   - wǒ wú fēi shì tí gè jiàn yì.
5. What they are arguing about is nothing more than money.
   - 他们争论的无非是钱的问题。
   - tā men zhēng lùn de wú fēi shì qián de wèn tí.
6. The outcome is simply success or failure.
   - 结果无非是成功或失败。
   - jié guǒ wú fēi shì chéng gōng huò shī bài.
7. He keeps explaining simply because he fears you will misunderstand.
   - 他反复解释，无非是怕你误会。
   - tā fǎn fù jiě shì, wú fēi shì pà nǐ wù huì.
8. This tool merely helps us save time.
   - 这个工具无非是帮我们节省时间。
   - zhè ge gōng jù wú fēi shì bāng wǒ men jié shěng shí jiān.
9. All she wants is an apology.
   - 她要的无非是一句道歉。
   - tā yào de wú fēi shì yī jù dào qiàn.
10. Practicing a few more times just means spending a little more time.
   - 多练几次，无非是多花一点时间。
   - duō liàn jǐ cì, wú fēi shì duō huā yī diǎn shí jiān.

## grammar-153: Tentative surprised inference with 莫非

莫非 introduces a tentative explanation that has just occurred to the speaker, often with surprise or suspicion. It means 'could it be that...?' rather than directly asking for a known fact.

   - 莫非她已经知道结果了？
   - mò fēi tā yǐ jīng zhī dào jié guǒ le?
5. Could it be that we took the wrong road?
   - 莫非我们走错路了？
   - mò fēi wǒ men zǒu cuò lù le?
6. Could it be that you no longer plan to participate?
   - 莫非你不打算参加了？
   - mò fēi nǐ bù dǎ suàn cān jiā le?
7. Could it be that the tickets have already sold out?
   - 莫非门票已经卖完了？
   - mò fēi mén piào yǐ jīng mài wán le?
8. Could it be that he is joking?
   - 莫非他是在开玩笑？
   - mò fēi tā shì zài kāi wán xiào?
9. Could it be that you wrote this letter?

10. Could it be that they left ahead of schedule?
   - 莫非他们提前出发了？
   - mò fēi tā men tí qián chū fā le?

## grammar-154: Challenging an assumption with 难道

**stretch · 难道 + proposition + 吗？**

难道 marks a rhetorical question expressing disbelief or challenging an assumption. A positive proposition often implies a negative answer, and a negative proposition often implies a positive one. It is stronger than a neutral 吗 question.

1. Don't you remember me?
   - 难道你不记得我了吗？
   - nán dào nǐ bù jì de wǒ le ma?
2. Isn't this clear enough already?
   - 难道这还不够清楚吗？
   - nán dào zhè hái bù gòu qīng chǔ ma?
3. Are we really going to give up just like this?
   - 难道我们就这样放弃吗？
   - nán dào wǒ men jiù zhè yàng fàng qì ma?
4. Is money really more important than health?
   - 难道钱比健康更重要吗？
   - nán dào qián bǐ jiàn kāng gèng zhòng yào ma?
5. Have you really never made a mistake?
   - 难道你从来没犯过错吗？
   - nán dào nǐ cóng lái méi fàn guò cuò ma?
6. Is all of this really my responsibility?
   - 难道这都是我的责任吗？
   - nán dào zhè dōu shì wǒ de zé rèn ma?
7. Didn't you receive the notice?
   - 难道你没收到通知吗？
   - nán dào nǐ méi shōu dào tōng zhī ma?
8. Does making an effort really have no meaning?
   - 难道努力没有意义吗？
   - nán dào nǔ lì méi yǒu yì yì ma?
9. Do you really believe everything he says?
   - 难道他说什么你都相信吗？
   - nán dào tā shuō shén me nǐ dōu xiāng xìn ma?
10. Shouldn't we listen to her opinion first?
   - 难道我们不应该先听听她的意见吗？
   - nán dào wǒ men bù yīng gāi xiān tīng tīng tā de yì jiàn ma?

## grammar-155: Insisting on necessity with 非…不可

**stretch · Subject + 非 + verb phrase + 不可**

非…不可 expresses strong necessity or determination: someone absolutely must do something. The two negative-looking parts work together to rule out not doing it; this is stronger than ordinary advice with 应该.

1. I absolutely must get to the bottom of this matter.
   - 这件事我非弄清楚不可。

   - 他非亲自去一趟不可。
   - tā fēi qīn zì qù yī tàng bù kě.

   - 这个问题今天非解决不可。
   - zhè ge wèn tí jīn tiān fēi jiě jué bù kě.
4. She insists on seeing the person in charge.
   - 她非见到负责人不可。
   - tā fēi jiàn dào fù zé rén bù kě.
5. I am determined to finish this book.
   - 我非把这本书看完不可。
   - wǒ fēi bǎ zhè běn shū kàn wán bù kě.
6. This money absolutely must be paid back.
   - 这笔钱非还不可。
   - zhè bǐ qián fēi huán bù kě.
7. Do you absolutely have to leave now?
   - 你非现在走不可吗？
   - nǐ fēi xiàn zài zǒu bù kě ma?
8. This mistake absolutely must be corrected.
   - 这个错误非改不可。
   - zhè ge cuò wù fēi gǎi bù kě.
9. They insist on waiting until the result comes out.
   - 他们非等到结果出来不可。
   - tā men fēi děng dào jié guǒ chū lái bù kě.
10. We absolutely must find the cause.
   - 我们非找到原因不可。
   - wǒ men fēi zhǎo dào yuán yīn bù kě.
