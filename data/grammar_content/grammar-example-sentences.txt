Chinese grammar example sentences

grammar-001: Present descriptions
Pattern: Subject + 很 + adjective

grammar-001-01
今天天气很好。
jīn tiān tiān qì hěn hǎo.
The weather is nice today.

grammar-001-02
这家饭店很安静。
zhè jiā fàn diàn hěn ān jìng.
This restaurant is quiet.

grammar-001-03
你的房间很干净。
nǐ de fáng jiān hěn gān jìng.
Your room is clean.

grammar-001-04
这道题很简单。
zhè dào tí hěn jiǎn dān.
This question is easy.

grammar-001-05
那条路很宽。
nà tiáo lù hěn kuān.
That road is wide.

grammar-001-06
这件衬衫很舒服。
zhè jiàn chèn shān hěn shū fú.
This shirt is comfortable.

grammar-001-07
他的回答很清楚。
tā de huí dá hěn qīng chǔ.
His answer is clear.

grammar-001-08
这个城市很漂亮。
zhè ge chéng shì hěn piào liàng.
This city is beautiful.

grammar-001-09
我的邻居很友好。
wǒ de lín jū hěn yǒu hǎo.
My neighbor is friendly.

grammar-001-10
这份工作很重要。
zhè fèn gōng zuò hěn zhòng yào.
This job is important.

grammar-002: Possession with 的
Pattern: Possessor + 的 + noun

grammar-002-01
这是我的护照。
zhè shì wǒ de hù zhào.
This is my passport.

grammar-002-02
那是老师的电脑。
nà shì lǎo shī de diàn nǎo.
That is the teacher's computer.

grammar-002-03
你的手机在桌子上。
nǐ de shǒu jī zài zhuō zi shàng.
Your phone is on the table.

grammar-002-04
她的自行车很新。
tā de zì xíng chē hěn xīn.
Her bicycle is new.

grammar-002-05
我朋友的公司很大。
wǒ péng yǒu de gōng sī hěn dà.
My friend's company is large.

grammar-002-06
这是我们学校的地图。
zhè shì wǒ men xué xiào de dì tú.
This is a map of our school.

grammar-002-07
他的办公室在三楼。
tā de bàn gōng shì zài sān lóu.
His office is on the third floor.

grammar-002-08
姐姐的生日是明天。
jiě jiě de shēng rì shì míng tiān.
My older sister's birthday is tomorrow.

grammar-002-09
这不是我的行李。
zhè bù shì wǒ de xíng lǐ.
This is not my luggage.

grammar-002-10
他们的孩子在北京工作。
tā men de hái zi zài běi jīng gōng zuò.
Their child works in Beijing.

grammar-003: Describing nouns with 的
Pattern: Description + 的 + noun

grammar-003-01
我喜欢你买的那本书。
wǒ xǐ huān nǐ mǎi de nà běn shū.
I like the book that you bought.

grammar-003-02
穿红衣服的人是我姐姐。
chuān hóng yī fú de rén shì wǒ jiě jiě.
The person wearing red clothes is my older sister.

grammar-003-03
这是我昨天拍的照片。
zhè shì wǒ zuó tiān pāi de zhào piān.
This is the photo that I took yesterday.

grammar-003-04
他做的饭很好吃。
tā zuò de fàn hěn hǎo chī.
The food that he cooks is delicious.

grammar-003-05
我要一杯不加糖的咖啡。
wǒ yào yī bēi bù jiā táng de kā fēi.
I want a cup of coffee without sugar.

grammar-003-06
住在楼上的女孩是学生。
zhù zài lóu shàng de nǚ hái shì xué shēng.
The girl who lives upstairs is a student.

grammar-003-07
我们需要一个会说中文的导游。
wǒ men xū yào yī gè huì shuō zhōng wén de dǎo yóu.
We need a tour guide who can speak Chinese.

grammar-003-08
你推荐的电影很有意思。
nǐ tuī jiàn de diàn yǐng hěn yǒu yì si.
The movie that you recommended is interesting.

grammar-003-09
这是开往上海的火车。
zhè shì kāi wǎng shàng hǎi de huǒ chē.
This is the train bound for Shanghai.

grammar-003-10
我找不到昨天收到的那封信。
wǒ zhǎo bù dào zuó tiān shōu dào de nà fēng xìn.
I cannot find the letter that I received yesterday.

grammar-004-01

wǒ de yào shi zài bāo lǐ.

grammar-004-02

grammar-004-03

grammar-004-04

hái zi men zài gōng yuán lǐ.
The children are in the park.

grammar-004-05
你的眼镜在床上。
nǐ de yǎn jìng zài chuáng shàng.
Your glasses are on the bed.

grammar-004-06
银行在超市旁边。
yín háng zài chāo shì páng biān.
The bank is next to the supermarket.

grammar-004-07
我现在在地铁站。
wǒ xiàn zài zài dì tiě zhàn.
I am at the subway station now.

grammar-004-08
经理今天不在公司。
jīng lǐ jīn tiān bù zài gōng sī.

grammar-004-09

nà jiā shū diàn zài xué xiào duì miàn.

grammar-004-10

chē zhàn zài yī yuàn hòu miàn.

grammar-006-04

grammar-006-05

grammar-006-06

grammar-007-02

grammar-007-03

grammar-007-04
他晚上八点回家。
tā wǎn shàng bā diǎn huí jiā.
He comes home at eight in the evening.

grammar-007-05
我星期六有空。

grammar-007-06

huì yì xià wǔ liǎng diǎn kāi shǐ.

grammar-007-07

grammar-007-08

grammar-007-09

grammar-007-10

grammar-008: Negation with 不

grammar-008-01

wǒ bù hē kā fēi.
I do not drink coffee.

grammar-008-02
他不喜欢下雨天。

grammar-008-03
这件衣服不贵。
zhè jiàn yī fú bù guì.
This piece of clothing is not expensive.

grammar-008-04
她明天不来。
tā míng tiān bù lái.
She is not coming tomorrow.

grammar-008-05
我不认识那个人。
wǒ bù rèn shí nà ge rén.
I do not know that person.

grammar-008-06
这条路不安全。
zhè tiáo lù bù ān quán.
This road is not safe.

grammar-008-07
我们周末不上班。
wǒ men zhōu mò bù shàng bān.
We do not work on weekends.

grammar-008-08
弟弟不吃鱼。
dì di bù chī yú.
My younger brother does not eat fish.

grammar-008-09
我不同意你的看法。
wǒ bù tóng yì nǐ de kàn fǎ.
I do not agree with your view.

grammar-008-10
今天不冷。
jīn tiān bù lěng.
It is not cold today.

grammar-010: Yes-no questions with 吗
Pattern: Statement + 吗？

grammar-010-01
你喜欢这本书吗？
nǐ xǐ huān zhè běn shū ma?
Do you like this book?

grammar-010-02
他是你的同事吗？
tā shì nǐ de tóng shì ma?
Is he your colleague?

grammar-010-03
你明天有时间吗？
nǐ míng tiān yǒu shí jiān ma?
Do you have time tomorrow?

grammar-010-04
这儿可以停车吗？
zhè er kě yǐ tíng chē ma?
Can we park here?

grammar-010-05
你吃过北京烤鸭吗？
nǐ chī guò běi jīng kǎo yā ma?
Have you ever eaten Beijing roast duck?

grammar-010-06
她已经到家了吗？
tā yǐ jīng dào jiā le ma?
Has she arrived home already?

grammar-010-07
这个座位有人吗？
zhè ge zuò wèi yǒu rén ma?
Is this seat taken?

grammar-010-08
你需要帮助吗？
nǐ xū yào bāng zhù ma?
Do you need help?

grammar-010-09
外面还在下雨吗？
wài miàn hái zài xià yǔ ma?
Is it still raining outside?

grammar-010-10
你会用筷子吗？
nǐ huì yòng kuài zi ma?
Can you use chopsticks?

grammar-013: Choice questions with 还是

grammar-013-03
你今天去还是明天去？
nǐ jīn tiān qù hái shì míng tiān qù?

grammar-013-04
你喜欢红色还是蓝色？
nǐ xǐ huān hóng sè hái shì lán sè?

grammar-013-05

tā shì lǎo shī hái shì yī shēng?

grammar-013-06
你想在家吃还是出去吃？
nǐ xiǎng zài jiā chī hái shì chū qù chī?
Do you want to eat at home or go out to eat?

grammar-013-07
你要大的还是小的？
nǐ yào dà de hái shì xiǎo de?
Do you want the big one or the small one?

grammar-013-08
我们先吃饭还是先买票？
wǒ men xiān chī fàn hái shì xiān mǎi piào?
Shall we eat first or buy tickets first?

grammar-013-09
你用现金还是信用卡？
nǐ yòng xiàn jīn hái shì xìn yòng kǎ?

grammar-013-10
她住在北京还是上海？
tā zhù zài běi jīng hái shì shàng hǎi?
Does she live in Beijing or Shanghai?
